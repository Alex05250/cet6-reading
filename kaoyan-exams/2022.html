Section II Part C · 英译汉（划线句）453 词 · 5 句

Between 1807 and 1814 the Iberian Peninsula (comprising Spain and Portugal) was the scene of a titanic and merciless struggle. It took place on many different planes: between Napoleon's French army and the angry inhabitants; between the British, ever keen to exacerbate the emperor's difficulties, and the marshals sent from Paris to try to keep them in check; between new forces of science and meritocracy and old ones of conservatism and birth. It was also, and this is unknown even to many people well read about the period, a battle between those who made codes and those who broke them.

1807年至1814年间，伊比利亚半岛(包括西班牙和葡萄牙)是一场残酷无情的巨大斗争的舞台。这场斗争在许多不同层面上展开：拿破仑的法国军队与愤怒的当地居民之间；英国人(他们一直热衷于加剧这位皇帝的困境)与从巴黎派来试图遏制他们的元帅们之间；科学与精英制度的新势力与保守主义和血统的旧势力之间。这场斗争还发生在编制密码的人和破译密码的人之间，而这一点即使对许多熟读那个时期历史的人来说也是未知的。

I first discovered the Napoleonic cryptographic battle a few years ago when I was reading Sir Charles Oman's epic History of the Peninsular War. In volume V he had attached an appendix, The Scovell Ciphers. It listed many documents in code that had been captured from the French army of Spain, and whose secrets had been revealed by the work of one George Scovell, an officer in British headquarters. Oman rated Scovell's significance highly, but at the same time, the general nature of his History meant that he could not analyze carefully what this obscure officer may or may not have contributed to that great struggle between nations or indeed tell us anything much about the man himself. I was keen to read more, but was surprised to find that Oman's appendix, published in 1914, was the only considered thing that had been written about this secret war.

几年前，当我阅读查尔斯·奥曼爵士的史诗巨著《半岛战争史》时，我第一次发现了拿破仑时期的密码战。在第五卷中，他附上了一个附录《斯科维尔密码》。该附录列出了许多从西班牙法国军队那里缴获的密码文件，这些文件的秘密被英国司令部的一位军官乔治·斯科维尔的工作所揭示。奥曼对斯科维尔的重要性评价很高，但与此同时，他的《历史》的总体性质意味着他无法仔细分析这位默默无闻的军官可能对国家间的伟大斗争做出或没有做出什么贡献，也无法告诉我们关于这个人本身的太多信息。我渴望阅读更多内容，但惊讶地发现奥曼于1914年出版的附录是唯一一篇关于这场秘密战争的深思熟虑的文章。

I became convinced that this story was every bit as exciting and significant as that of Enigma and the breaking of German codes in the Second World War. The question was, could it be told?

我确信这个故事与第二次世界大战中Enigma和破译德国密码的故事一样激动人心和重要。问题是，这个故事能被讲述出来吗？

Studying Scovell's papers at the Public Record Office, London, I found that he had left an extensive journal and copious notes about his work in the peninsula. What was more, many original French dispatches had been preserved in this collection, which I realized was priceless. There may have been many spies and intelligence officers during the Napoleonic Wars, but it is usually extremely difficult to find the material they actually provided or worked on.

在伦敦公共档案馆研究斯科维尔的文件时，我发现他留下了大量日记和关于他在半岛工作的详尽笔记。更重要的是，这个收藏中保存了许多法国原始公文，我意识到这是无价之宝。在拿破仑战争期间可能有许多间谍和情报官员，但通常极难找到他们实际提供或处理的材料。

As I researched Scovell's story I found far more of interest besides his intelligence work. His status in Lord Wellington's headquarters and the recognition given to him for his work were all bound up with the class politics of the army at the time. His tale of self-improvement and hard work would make a fascinating biography in its own right, but represents something more than that. Just as the code breaking has its wider relevance in the struggle for Spain, so his attempts to make his way up the promotion ladder speak volumes about British society.

在研究斯科维尔的故事时，我发现除了他的情报工作之外，还有更多有趣的内容。他在威灵顿勋爵司令部的地位以及他的工作所获得的认可都与当时军队的阶级政治紧密相连。他自我提升和努力工作的故事本身就足以成为一部引人入胜的传记，但它所代表的意义远不止于此。正如密码破译在西班牙争夺战中具有更广泛的相关性一样，他在晋升阶梯上向上攀登的努力也充分说明了英国社会的方方面面。

As is vividly depicted in the drawing, two female students in school uniforms stand before a bulletin board on which a poster announcing a campus lecture is pinned. Simple as it appears, the drawing conveys a thought-provoking message about our attitude toward learning. In my view, the second girl's attitude deserves our applause. Knowledge is never confined to a single major; a lecture in an unfamiliar area may plant a seed that flourishes years later. Therefore, we young people should step out of our comfort zone and embrace every chance to learn.
