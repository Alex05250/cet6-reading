The Architecture of Credibility: How Quantitative Faith Displaced Qualitative Judgment

In the end, the debate over metrics is a debate about what it means to be human.

归根结底，关于指标的争论，是关于人之为人的意义的争论。

Are we merely collections of measurable attributes—height, weight, income, test scores—or are we something more?

我们是否仅仅是一堆可测量属性的集合——身高、体重、收入、考试分数——还是说，我们不止于此？

The democratic promise of quantification was that it would liberate us from the arbitrary power of lords and priests.

量化曾许下的民主承诺是，它将我们从领主和神父的专断权力中解放出来。

In many ways, it has.

在许多方面，它确实做到了。

But liberty requires more than the removal of old hierarchies; it requires the courage to resist new ones.

但自由不仅仅需要推翻旧的等级制度；它还需要有勇气抵抗新的等级制度。

The statistician who reduces a neighbourhood to a crime index is no less reductive than the lord who reduced a peasant to a serf.

将一片街区简化为犯罪指数的统计学家，其简化程度并不亚于将农民贬为农奴的领主。

The challenge of the coming decades is to build institutions that can hold both kinds of knowledge in tension: the clarity of the number and the complexity of the story.

未来几十年的挑战在于，建立能够同时容纳两种知识并使之保持张力的制度：数字的清晰与故事的复杂。

This is not an easy task.

这并非易事。

It is, however, the only decent one available to us.

然而，这是我们唯一体面的选择。

The alternative is to live in a world where everything is counted and nothing is known.

另一种选择是，活在一个万物皆被计数、却无一物被真正了解的世界里。
