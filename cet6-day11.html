The Algorithmic Gaze: How Quantitative Faith Shapes Modern Institutions

In the final analysis, the future of institutional intelligence depends on our ability to hold two thoughts in tension: the power of numbers and the irreducible complexity of human life.

归根结底，机构智能的未来取决于我们能否在张力中同时把握两种思想：数字的力量与人类生活不可简化的复杂性。

The majority of policy failures in recent decades share a common origin—the conviction that a single quantitative framework can capture the whole of reality.

近几十年来，大多数政策失败的共同根源在于一种信念——认为单一的量化框架能够涵盖全部现实。

Yet every dimension of social existence, from the elementary classroom to the mayor’s office, resists such compression.

然而，社会存在的每一个维度，从小学教室到市长办公室，都抗拒这种压缩。

The germ of wisdom lies not in discarding data, but in recognising that data is a means, not an end.

智慧的萌芽不在于抛弃数据，而在于认识到数据是手段，而非目的。

When we rely on numbers to tell the whole story, we inevitably degrade the very values we seek to uphold.

当我们依赖数字来讲述完整的故事时，不可避免地会贬损我们本欲维护的价值。

Only by embracing a more sophisticated, cross-disciplinary orientation can we hope to build institutions that are both efficient and humane.

唯有拥抱一种更精微、跨学科的取向，我们才有望建立起既高效又人道的机构。

The choice is ours: to remain faithful to the comforting simplicity of metrics, or to accept the messier, more demanding task of understanding the world in all its dimensions.

选择权在我们手中：要么继续忠于指标那令人安心的简单性，要么接受一项更繁乱、更艰巨的任务——在世界的全部维度中去理解世界。
