The desert is deceiving.

沙漠具有欺骗性。

At first glance it looks lifeless, barren, and bone-dry.

乍看之下，它毫无生气，荒芜贫瘠，干燥至极。

For most passersby humming through the Mojave on their way to try their luck in Las Vegas or heading towards the Grand Canyon, it's just a flat stretch of land with some mountains in the distance and more apt to be a setting for a movie that takes place on Mars.

对于大多数驾车疾驰穿过莫哈韦沙漠、前往拉斯维加斯碰运气或驶向大峡谷的过客而言，这里不过是一片平坦的开阔地，远处点缀着几座山，倒更像是以火星为背景的电影取景地。

The desert, however, is overflowing with life, mystery, and magic.

然而，这片沙漠却充满了生命、神秘与魔力。

The Mojave desert sees less than two inches of rain a year, and like most deserts, is a land of extremes.

莫哈韦沙漠年降雨量不足两英寸，而且和大多数沙漠一样，是一片充满极端的土地。

Temperature fluctuations vary from freezing to burning hot, not only between seasons, but even within the same day.

气温从冰天雪地到酷热灼人剧烈起伏，不仅季节之间如此，甚至同一天之内也不例外。

At the heart of this vast, 25,000-square-mile desert is Mojave National Preserve.

在这片广袤的、面积达2.5万平方英里的沙漠腹地，坐落着莫哈韦国家保护区。

The folks managing and working there wear the wide-brimmed hats that have become synonymous with places like Yosemite.

在那里从事管理和工作的人们都戴着宽檐帽，这种帽子已成为约塞米蒂之类国家公园的标志。

The preserve is rich with history, culture, biodiversity, and endless opportunities to stimulate your interest, especially for anyone who loves the outdoors.

保护区历史底蕴深厚，文化丰富多彩，生物种类繁多，还有数不清的机会激发你的兴趣，对任何热爱户外的人来说尤其如此。

For a photographer, it requires patience and probably a few spare tires to maneuver the network of unpaved "roads", but the rewards are plenty.

对摄影师而言，要在这一片未经铺砌的“道路”网络中穿行，需要耐心，还得备上几条备用轮胎，但回报十分丰厚。

Photographing here requires a good, sturdy pair of shoes and a lot of preparation.

在这里摄影，需要一双结实耐穿的好鞋和充分的准备。

Plenty of water, spare tires, and a full tank of gas are needed to explore this unit of the national park system, which is the third largest in the country.

要探索这处国家公园体系中的园区——它是全美第三大的国家公园单元——必须带上充足的水、备用轮胎和满满一箱汽油。

Like all deserts, though, if you are patient, you will be rewarded, as they often reveal their secrets slowly.

不过，就像所有沙漠一样，只要你有耐心，终会有所收获，因为它们往往会慢慢显露自己的秘密。

During my last outing, I was surprised to see how much of the area was shaped by ancient volcanoes and geological forces, much of which remains today, giving the area a unique feel and painting a colorful background for great photographs.

在最近一次出行中，我惊讶地发现这片区域有如此多的地貌是由古代火山和地质力量塑造而成，其中大部分至今犹存，赋予了这片区域独特的韵味，也为精彩的照片描绘出色彩斑斓的背景。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1215 词 · 63 句

A

On the hills of central Kenya, almost lime-green with the sparkle of tea bushes in the sunlight, farmers know all about climate change.

在肯尼亚中部的丘陵地带，茶树丛在阳光下闪着光，满目青翠，这里的农户对气候变化了如指掌。

"The rainy season is no longer predictable," says one.

其中一位说："雨季已经没法预测了。

"When it is supposed to rain it doesn't, then it all comes at once."

该下雨的时候不下，要下就一次性全下下来。"

Climate change is an issue that will affect everyone on the planet.

气候变化是一个将影响地球上每一个人的问题。

For Africans its consequences will be particularly bitter: whereas other regions were able to grow rich by burning coal and oil, Africa will pay much of the human price without having enjoyed the benefits.

对非洲人来说，其后果将尤为惨痛：其他地区靠燃烧煤炭和石油得以致富，而非洲却要在未曾享受发展红利的情况下，承担大部分的人道代价。

"Africa only represents 2% of global greenhouse-gas emissions but it is the continent that is expected to suffer the most from climate impacts," says Mafalda Duarte, who runs the World Bank's $8bn Climate Investment Funds.

管理着世界银行80亿美元气候投资基金的玛法尔达·杜阿尔特说："非洲的温室气体排放量仅占全球的2%，但它却是预计受气候影响最严重的大陆。"

B

Although there are huge uncertainties as to the precise impacts of climate change, enough is known to say that global warming represents one of the main threats to Africa's prosperity.

尽管气候变化的确切影响存在极大的不确定性，但已知的情况足以说明，全球变暖是对非洲繁荣的主要威胁之一。

Parts of the continent are already warming much more quickly than the average: temperatures in southern Africa have increased by about twice the global rate over the past 50 years.

非洲大陆的部分地区升温速度已远超全球平均水平：过去50年间，南部非洲的气温升幅约为全球平均速度的两倍。

Even if the world were to cut emissions enough to keep global warming below 1.5℃, heat-waves would intensify in Africa and diseases would spread to areas not currently affected.

即使全球减排足以将升温幅度控制在1.5℃以内，非洲的热浪仍将加剧，疾病也将蔓延到目前尚未受影响的地区。

Farming would also be hit hard.

农业也将遭受重创。

About 40% of the land now used to grow maize would no longer be suitable for it.

目前用于种植玉米的土地中，约有40%将不再适宜种植玉米。

Overall, it is estimated that maize yields would fall by 18-22%.

总体而言，据估计玉米产量将下降18%至22%。

C

Africa is particularly vulnerable, in part because it is already struggling to feed itself and it will have to vastly increase yields and productivity if it is to put food on the plates of a fast-growing population, even without climate change.

非洲尤其脆弱，部分原因是它目前连养活自己都已十分吃力，而即便不考虑气候变化，要想让快速增长的人口都能吃上饭，也必须大幅提高产量和生产率。

The UN's Food and Agriculture Organisation reckons that by 2050 global food production would have to rise by about 70% over its level of 2009 to meet demand from a population that is growing in numbers and appetite.

联合国粮食及农业组织估计，到2050年，全球粮食产量必须在2009年的水平上增长约70%，才能满足人口数量和食物需求双双增长所带来的需求。

Much of this new demand will be in Africa.

其中大部分新增需求将出现在非洲。

Yet the continent already imports about $50bn-worth of food a year and that figure is expected to more than double over the next five years.

然而，非洲大陆目前每年进口的食物价值约500亿美元，预计这一数字在未来五年内将增长一倍以上。

Self-sufficiency is not Africa's goal, but the fact that it spends more money importing food than it does buying capital goods suggests it has room for improvement.

自给自足并非非洲的目标，但事实是，它用于进口食品的开支超过了购买资本货物的开支，这表明它仍有改进的余地。

D

Finding out why is not hard.

要弄清其中的原因并不难。

Most farms are tiny, ploughed by hand and reliant on rain.

大多数农场规模极小，靠人力耕作，且依赖降雨。

More than half of Africa's people make their living from farming.

非洲有一半以上的人口以务农为生。

Although its total harvest has climbed over the past few decades, this is mainly because there are more people farming more land.

尽管过去几十年间非洲的粮食总产量有所攀升，但这主要是因为从事农业的人口更多、耕种的土地更多。

But in many places there is no spare land to farm.

但在许多地方，已经没有可供开垦的闲置土地了。

Plots in Rwanda are so small that you could fit 250 of them onto the average American farm.

卢旺达的地块是如此之小，一个普通美国农场就能容纳250个这样的地块。

And although output per worker has improved by more than half over the past 30 years in Africa, that is still far behind the 2.5 times improvement in Asia.

尽管过去30年间非洲的劳均产出提高了五成以上，但仍远远落后于亚洲2.5倍的增幅。

Yields of maize are generally less than two tonnes per hectare, a fifth the level in America.

玉米的单产通常不到每公顷两吨，仅为美国水平的五分之一。

E

The low productivity of African farmers is reflected in national economic statistics—despite absorbing so much labour, farming generates just 15% of GDP.

非洲农民的低生产率反映在国家经济统计数据中——农业尽管吸纳了大量劳动力，却仅贡献了15%的国内生产总值。

"They can't even feed their families," says Jennifer Blanke, a vice-president of the African Development Bank in charge of agriculture.

"他们甚至养不活自己的家人，"非洲开发银行负责农业的副行长詹妮弗·布兰克说。

"Farm productivity hasn't improved in many parts of Africa for 100 years."

"在非洲许多地区，农业生产率已经100年没有提高了。"

F

One reason is that in the first few decades of independence, many African governments neglected farming as they focused on industrialising their economies.

原因之一是，在独立后的最初几十年里，许多非洲政府专注于经济工业化，忽视了农业。

Others damaged it by pushing down the prices that state monopolies paid for their crops in order to subsidise workers in cities with cheap food.

另一些国家则通过压低国家垄断企业收购农产品的价格，用廉价粮食补贴城市工人，从而损害了农业。

Ghana taxed cocoa exports so heavily that production collapsed by half between the 1960s and 1980s, despite a jump in the global price of cocoa.

加纳对可可出口征收重税，以致在20世纪60年代至80年代期间，尽管全球可可价格大幅上涨，产量仍下降了一半。

Yet over the past two decades or so governments and donors have begun to look again at farming as a way of providing jobs for the 13 million young people entering the workforce each year.

然而，在过去二十年左右的时间里，各国政府和捐助方已开始重新审视农业，将其视为为每年进入劳动力市场的1300万年轻人提供就业机会的途径。

Much of the focus has been on getting small farmers to use fertiliser and, more important, better seeds.

关注重点大多放在让小农户使用化肥以及更重要的——优良种子上。

The results can be impressive.

其成效可能相当显著。

Improved varieties of sorghum, for instance, can produce a crop that is 40% larger than the usual variety.

例如，改良的高粱品种可比常规品种增产40%。

Infrastructure is important.

基础设施也很重要。

A World Bank irrigation project in Ethiopia helped farmers increase their potato harvest from about 8 tonnes per hectare to 35 tonnes.

世界银行在埃塞俄比亚的一个灌溉项目帮助农民将土豆产量从每公顷约8吨提高到35吨。

G

Better techniques help, too.

更好的种植技术同样能发挥作用。

Small coffee farmers in Kenya are able to increase their incomes by 40% by following a few simple guidelines on caring for their bushes, such as trimming all but three of their stems.

肯尼亚的小型咖啡种植户只需遵循几条简单的咖啡树养护指南，例如每株只保留三根枝条、剪去其余枝条，就能将收入提高40%。

Many of their neighbours do not follow the advice, because it seems counter-intuitive.

许多邻居却不采纳这些建议，因为这样做似乎有悖常理。

More stems ought to lead to more coffee beans, they say.

他们说，枝条越多，结出的咖啡豆应该越多。

Yet after seeing those following the advice get bigger harvests for a season or two, many others start doing the same.

然而，在看到遵循建议的人一两个季度后获得更高产量之后，许多其他人也开始照做。

H

One way of spreading knowledge is to link farms to big buyers of their harvests.

传播知识的一种方式，是将农户与农产品的大买家对接起来。

When Diageo, a British drinks giant, built a brewery in western Kenya, it wanted to use local crops to make a beer cheap enough to compete with illicit home brew.

英国饮料巨头帝亚吉欧在肯尼亚西部建了一座啤酒厂，希望利用当地农作物酿造一种价格足够低廉、能与非法自酿啤酒竞争的啤酒。

It organised farmers into groups, improved supply chains for them to get seeds and fertiliser and then agreed to buy their grain.

公司将农户组织成合作社，帮助他们完善种子和化肥的供应链，然后承诺收购他们的谷物。

It now provides a market to about 17,000 farmers.

目前，公司为约1.7万名农户提供销售渠道。

Across the region it has doubled its use of local raw material to about 80% over five years, says John O'Keeffe, who runs its Africa business.

其非洲业务负责人约翰·奥基夫表示，五年来，公司在整个非洲地区使用的本地原料比例已翻了一番，达到约80%。

I

An even more important change is the move from traditional farming to building businesses that can profitably bring technology and investment to small farmers.

更重要的一项变革，是从传统农业转向构建能够盈利的商业模式，从而将技术和投资引入小农生产中。

Taita Ngetich, a young Kenyan, was studying engineering when he wanted to earn a little money on the side.

年轻的肯尼亚人泰塔·恩格蒂奇在攻读工程学期间，想赚点外快。

He scraped together 20,000 Kenyan shillings (about $200) to plant tomatoes.

他东拼西凑了两万肯尼亚先令（约合200美元），用来种植番茄。

Everything went wrong.

结果事事不顺。

The crop was attacked by pests.

庄稼遭到了虫害。

"Then there was a massive flood that swallowed all our capital," he says.

他说：“随后又发生了一场大洪水，把我们的全部本钱都吞没了。”

Mr Ngetich persevered by looking into buying a greenhouse to protect his plants from bugs and rain.

恩格蒂奇先生没有放弃，他开始研究购买温室大棚，以保护作物免受虫害和雨水侵袭。

The cheapest ones cost more than $2,500 each, so he designed his own for half the price.

最便宜的温室每个也要两千五百多美元，于是他自行设计，造价只需一半。

Soon neighbouring farmers started placing orders with him, and now his firm, Illuminum Greenhouses, has sold more than 1,400 greenhouses that provide livelihoods to about 6,000 people.

很快，邻近的农户开始向他下单，如今他的公司Illuminum Greenhouses已售出一千四百多座温室大棚，为约六千人提供了生计。

The business does not stop there; he also supplies fertiliser, high-quality seedlings and smart sensors that increase yields.

他的生意并不仅限于此；他还供应化肥、优质种苗以及能够提高产量的智能传感器。

J

Illuminum's success shows how technology can help even small farms become more productive.

Illuminum的成功表明，技术如何能够帮助即使是小型农场提高生产力。

Because such a large share of Africa's population earns a living from agriculture, even small improvements in productivity can lift the incomes of millions of people.

由于非洲有很大一部分人口以农业为生，生产力哪怕只有小幅提升，也能改善数百万人的收入。

But over the longer run small-scale farming can go only so far, especially in the face of climate change and population pressure.

但从长远来看，小规模农业的发展空间有限，尤其是在气候变化和人口压力面前。

K

"If we really want to lift people out of poverty we have to finance projects that will get them an income of at least $100 a month so that they can pay for health care and education," says Mr Ngetich.

恩格蒂奇先生说：“如果我们真心想帮助人们摆脱贫困，就必须资助那些能让他们每月至少获得100美元收入的项目，使他们能够支付医疗和教育费用。”

"Projects that give them an extra $2 a month from growing beans or maize aren't going to get them there."

“靠种豆子或玉米每月多赚2美元的项目，是无法让他们脱贫的。”

Getting those big jumps will need better jobs in factories and cities.

要实现这些大幅跨越，就需要在工厂和城市中提供更好的就业岗位。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1449 词 · 27 句

P1

One of the great successes of the Republican Party in recent decades is the relentless propagation of a simple formula for economic growth: tax cuts.

共和党近几十年来最大的成功之一，就是不遗余力地传播一个简单的经济增长公式：减税。

P2

The formula doesn't work, but that has not affected its popularity.

这个公式并不奏效，但这丝毫没有影响它的受欢迎程度。

And while the cult of tax cuts has attracted many critics, it lacks for obvious rivals.

尽管对减税的崇拜引来众多批评者，但它缺少显而易见的替代方案。

P3

Democratic politicians have tended to campaign on helping people left behind by economic growth.

民主党政治家往往以帮助那些被经济增长抛在后面的人为竞选纲领。

When Democrats do talk about encouraging economic growth, they often sound like Republicans.

当民主党人确实谈到促进经济增长时，他们听起来往往像共和党人。

P4

This is not just a political problem for Democrats; it is an economic problem for the United States.

这不仅仅是民主党的政治问题，更是美国的经济问题。

The nation needs a better story about the drivers of economic growth.

美国需要一个更好的关于经济增长驱动力的故事。

The painful lessons of recent decades point to a promising candidate: higher wages.

近几十年来的惨痛教训指向了一个颇有前景的候选答案：提高工资。

P5

Raising the wages of American workers ought to be the priority of economic policymakers.

提高美国工人的工资应当成为经济政策制定者的首要任务。

We'd all be better off paying less attention to quarterly updates on the growth of the nation's gross domestic product (GDP) and focusing instead on the growth of workers' paychecks.

如果我们少关注国内生产总值（GDP）增长的季度数据，转而关注工人工资单的增长，我们都会过得更好。

P6

Set aside, for the moment, the familiar argument for higher wages: fairness.

暂且搁置关于提高工资的常见论点：公平。

The argument here is that higher wages can fuel the engine of economic growth.

这里要论证的是，提高工资可以为经济增长的引擎注入动力。

P7

Perhaps the most famous illustration of the benefits is the story of Henry Ford's decision in 1914 to pay $5 a day to workers on his Model T assembly lines.

要说这一好处最著名的例证，莫过于亨利·福特在1914年决定向其T型车装配线工人每天支付5美元的故事。

He did it to increase production—he was paying a premium to maintain a reliable workforce.

他这样做是为了提高产量——他支付的溢价是为了维持一支稳定可靠的劳动力队伍。

The unexpected benefit was that Ford's factory workers became Ford customers, too.

意想不到的好处是，福特工厂的工人也成了福特的顾客。

P8

The same logic still holds: Consumption drives the American economy, and workers who are paid more can spend more.

同样的逻辑至今依然成立：消费驱动美国经济，而获得更高报酬的工人也能花得更多。

P9

Mainstream economists insist that it is impossible to order up a sustainable increase in wages because compensation levels reflect the unerring judgment of market forces.

主流经济学家坚称，无法人为促成工资的持续上涨，因为薪酬水平反映的是市场力量的准确判断。

P10

The conventional wisdom held that productivity growth was the only route to higher wages.

传统观点认为，生产率增长是提高工资的唯一途径。

Through that lens, efforts to negotiate higher wages were counterproductive.

在这种视角下，通过谈判争取更高工资的努力反而是适得其反的。

Minimum-wage laws would raise unemployment because there was only so much money in the wage pool, and if some people got more, others would get none.

最低工资法会推高失业率，因为工资池中的资金总量有限，如果一些人拿得更多，另一些人就会分文不得。

P11

It was in the context of this worldview that it became popular to argue that tax cuts would drive prosperity.

正是在这一世界观的背景下，主张减税能够带来繁荣的观点变得流行起来。

Rich people would invest, productivity would increase, wages would rise.

富人会投资，生产率会提高，工资就会上涨。

P12

In the real world, things are more complicated.

在现实世界中，事情要复杂得多。

Wages are influenced by a tug of war between employers and workers, and employers have been winning.

工资受到雇主与工人之间拉锯博弈的影响，而雇主一直占据上风。

One clear piece of evidence is the widening gap between productivity growth and wage growth since roughly 1970.

一个明显的证据是，自大约1970年以来，生产率增长与工资增长之间的差距不断扩大。

Productivity has more than doubled; wages have lagged far behind.

生产率已经翻了一倍多，而工资却远远落在后面。

P13

A focus on wage growth would provide an antidote to the attractive simplicity of the belief in the magical power of tax cuts.

关注工资增长，可以对减税神奇魔力这一信念的诱人简单性起到解毒剂般的作用。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2444 词 · 18 句

P1

Journal editors decide what gets published and what doesn't, affecting the careers of other academics and influencing the direction that a field takes.

期刊编辑决定哪些文章能够发表、哪些不能发表，这不仅影响其他学者的职业生涯，也左右着一个学科的发展方向。

You'd hope, then, that journals would do everything they can to establish a diverse editorial board, reflecting a variety of voices, experiences, and identities.

因此，人们会期待期刊竭尽所能组建一个多元化的编辑委员会，以反映不同的声音、经历和身份。

P2

Unfortunately a new study in Nature Neuroscience makes for disheartening reading.

遗憾的是，《自然·神经科学》上的一项新研究读来令人沮丧。

The team finds that the majority of editors in top psychology and neuroscience journals are male and based in the United States: a situation that may be amplifying existing gender inequalities in the field and influencing the kind of research that gets published.

研究团队发现，顶级心理学和神经科学期刊的编辑大多为男性，且驻地在美国：这种状况可能正在放大该领域已有的性别不平等，并影响何种研究得以发表。

P3

Men were found to account for 60% of the editors of psychology journals.

研究发现，心理学期刊的编辑中男性占60%。

There were significantly more male than female editors at each level of seniority, and men made up the majority of editors in over three quarters of the journals.

在各个资历层级中，男性编辑人数均显著多于女性，且在超过四分之三的期刊中，编辑的多数为男性。

Crucially, the proportion of female editors was significantly lower than the overall proportion of women psychology researchers.

关键的是，女性编辑的比例显著低于心理学女性研究者在研究人员中的整体比例。

P4

The differences were even starker in the neuroscience journals: 70% of editors were male, and men held the majority of editorial positions in 88% of journals.

神经科学期刊中的差距更为悬殊：70%的编辑为男性，且在88%的期刊中，男性占据了编辑职位的多数。

In this case, the proportion of female editors was not significantly lower than the proportion of female researchers working in neuroscience—a finding that reveals enduring gender disparities in the field more broadly.

在这一情况下，女性编辑的比例并未显著低于从事神经科学工作的女性研究者的比例——这一发现揭示了该领域更广泛范围内根深蒂固的性别差异。

P5

Based on their results, the team concludes that "the ideas, values and decision-making biases of men are overrepresented in the editorial positions of the most recognized academic journals in psychology and neuroscience."

基于研究结果，该团队得出结论："在心理学和神经科学领域最具影响力的学术期刊的编辑职位中，男性的思想、价值观和决策偏见被过度代表。"

P6

Gender inequality in science is often attributed to the fact that senior academics are more likely to be male, because historically science was male-dominated: it's argued that as time goes on and more women rise to senior roles, the field will become more equal.

科学界的性别不平等通常被归因于资深学者更可能为男性这一事实，因为历史上科学长期由男性主导：有人认为，随着时间推移，越来越多的女性晋升到资深岗位，该领域将变得更加平等。

Yet this study showed that even the junior roles in psychology journals tended to be held disproportionately by men, despite the fact that there are actually more female than male junior psychology faculty.

然而，这项研究表明，即便在心理学期刊中，初级职位也往往不成比例地由男性担任，尽管实际上初级心理学教职中女性人数多于男性。

P7

This implies that a lack of female academics is not the problem.

这意味着，女性学者数量不足并非问题所在。

Instead, there are structural reasons that women are disadvantaged in science.

相反，女性在科学界处于劣势有其结构性原因。

Women receive lower salaries and face greater childcare demands, for instance, which can result in fewer publications and grants—the kinds of things that journals look for when deciding who to appoint.

例如，女性薪资较低，还要承担更重的育儿负担，这会导致她们发表的论文和获得的资助更少——而这些恰恰是期刊在决定聘任谁时看重的东西。

Rather than simply blaming the inequality of editorial boards on tradition, we should be actively breaking down these existing barriers.

与其简单地把编委会的不平等归咎于传统，我们更应主动打破这些既有的障碍。

P8

A lack of diversity among journal editors also likely contributes to psychology's WEIRD problem.

期刊编辑缺乏多样性，很可能也助长了心理学的WEIRD问题（即研究对象过度偏向西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕、民主社会人群的问题）。

If journal editors are largely men from the United States, then they will probably place higher value on papers that are relevant to Western, male populations, whether consciously or not.

如果期刊编辑主要是来自美国的男性，那么无论有意无意，他们很可能更看重那些与西方男性群体相关的论文。
