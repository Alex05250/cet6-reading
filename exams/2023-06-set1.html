Scientists recently examined studies on dog intelligence and compared them with research into the minds of other intelligent animals.

科学家们最近对有关犬类智力的研究进行了梳理，并将其与其他智能动物心智方面的研究作了比较。

The researchers found that dogs are among the more intelligent carnivores, social hunters and domestic animals, but that their intelligence does not surpass other intelligent animals in any of those categories.

研究人员发现，犬类在食肉动物、社会性猎食动物和家养动物中属于较为聪明的，但其智力在上述任何一类动物中都未超越其他智能动物。

Though a significant body of research has examined dog cognition previously, the authors of this new study found little to warrant the volume of work that has been devoted to the topic.

尽管此前已有大量研究考察过犬类认知，但这项新研究的作者发现，几乎没有什么依据足以支撑围绕这一课题所投入的巨大研究量。

Stephen Lea, lead author of the new study, argues that many researchers seem to have designed their studies to prove how clever dogs are, rather than simply to study dogs' brains.

这项新研究的第一作者斯蒂芬·利指出，许多研究人员似乎从设计之初就是为了证明狗有多聪明，而不是单纯地研究狗的大脑。

Lea and a colleague examined more than 300 studies of dog cognition, comparing the studies' results with those from research into other animals.

利和一位同事审阅了300多项犬类认知研究，并将这些研究的结果与其他动物的研究结果进行了比较。

The researchers made specific comparisons between the different species in different categories of intelligence.

研究人员针对不同物种在不同智力类别上进行了具体比较。

These comparisons affirmed that dogs are intelligent, but their intelligence is not as formidable as some researchers might have believed.

这些比较证实了狗确实具有智力，但其智力并不像某些研究人员所认为的那样强大。

In many areas, though, comparisons were not possible.

不过，在许多方面，比较无法进行。

For example, the researchers noted that both dogs and cats are known to be able to recognize and differentiate human voices.

例如，研究人员指出，众所周知，狗和猫都能识别和分辨人声。

But the investigators could not find any data to indicate which species can remember a greater number of distinct human voices, so it was impossible to compare the two on that front.

但调查人员找不到任何数据来说明哪一物种能够记住更多不同的声音，因此无法在这一方面对两者进行比较。

However, not all researchers agree completely with the findings of this study.

然而，并非所有研究人员都完全认同这项研究的结论。

Zachary Silver, an American researcher, believes the authors of the new study overstated the idea that an excessive amount of research has been devoted to dogs, as the field of dog cognition is young, and there is much to be learned about how dogs think.

美国研究人员扎卡里·西尔弗认为，新研究的作者夸大了对犬类研究投入过多这一观点，因为犬类认知这一研究领域尚属新兴，对于狗如何思考仍有许多未知之处有待探索。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1179 词 · 53 句

A

Gratitude may be more beneficial than we commonly suppose.

感恩的益处可能远超我们通常的想象。

One recent study asked participants to write a note of thanks to someone and then estimate how surprised and happy the recipient would feel—an impact that they consistently underestimated.

最近的一项研究请参与者给某人写一封感谢信，然后估计收信人会感到多么惊喜和高兴——结果他们始终低估了这份影响。

Another study assessed the health benefits of writing thank-you notes.

另一项研究评估了写感谢信对健康的益处。

The researchers found that writing as few as three weekly thank-you notes over the course of three weeks improved life satisfaction, increased happy feelings and reduced symptoms of depression.

研究人员发现，在三周的时间里每周写区区三封感谢信，就能提升生活满意度、增加幸福感并减轻抑郁症状。

B

While this research into gratitude is relatively new, the principles involved are anything but.

尽管关于感恩的研究起步较晚，但其中蕴含的原则却绝非新鲜事物。

Students of mine in a political philosophy course at Indiana University are reading Daniel Defoe's 300-year-old Robinson Crusoe, often regarded as the first novel published in English.

我在印第安纳大学政治哲学课上的学生们正在阅读丹尼尔·笛福三百年前写就的《鲁滨逊漂流记》，该书常被视为英语世界出版的第一部小说。

Left alone on an unknown island with no apparent prospect of rescue or escape, Crusoe has much to lament.

独自困在一座荒岛上，既看不到获救的希望，也找不到逃生的可能，鲁滨逊有太多可以哀叹的事。

But instead of giving in to despair, he makes a list of things for which he is grateful, including the fact that he is the sole survivor from the shipwreck and has been able to salvage many useful items from the wreckage.

但他没有屈服于绝望，而是列出了一份感恩清单，其中包括：他是海难中唯一的幸存者，并且已从残骸中打捞出许多有用的物品。

C

Defoe's masterpiece, which is often ranked as one of the world's greatest novels, provides a portrait of gratitude in action that is as timely and relevant today as it has ever been.

笛福的这部杰作常被评为世界最伟大的小说之一，它为我们描绘了一幅将感恩付诸行动的图景，其意义在今天与以往任何时候一样及时而切中时弊。

It is also one with which contemporary psychology and medicine are just beginning to catch up.

这同时也是当代心理学和医学才刚刚开始追赶的一个领域。

Simply put, for most of us, it is far more helpful to focus on the things in life for which we can express gratitude than those that incline us toward resentment and lamentation.

简而言之，对我们大多数人来说，把注意力放在生活中值得感恩的事物上，远比纠结那些让我们怨愤和哀叹的事情更有益。

D

When we focus on the things we regret, such as failed relationships, family disputes, and setbacks in career and finance, we tend to become more regretful.

当我们一味关注令人遗憾的事情，比如失败的恋情、家庭纷争以及事业和财务上的挫折时，我们往往会变得更加懊悔。

Conversely, when we focus on the things we are grateful for, a greater sense of happiness tends to spread through our lives.

相反，当我们把注意力放在值得感恩的事物上时，更强烈的幸福感往往会渗透到我们生活的方方面面。

And while no one would argue for cultivating a false sense of blessedness, there is mounting evidence that counting our blessings is one of the best habits we can develop to promote mental and physical health.

虽然没有人会主张培养一种虚假的幸福感，但越来越多的证据表明，学会感恩是我们所能养成的、有助于促进身心健康的最佳习惯之一。

E

Gratitude has long enjoyed a privileged position in many of the world's cultural traditions.

在世界诸多文化传统中，感恩长期以来都享有特殊的地位。

For example, some ancient Western philosophers counsel gratitude that is both enduring and complete, and some Eastern thinkers portray it as not merely an attitude but a virtue to be put into practice.

例如，一些西方古代哲学家主张感恩应持久而彻底，一些东方思想家则将其描述为一种不仅停留在态度层面、更应付诸实践的美德。

F

Recent scientific studies support these ancient teachings.

近年来的科学研究印证了这些古老教诲。

Individuals who regularly engage in gratitude exercises, such as counting their blessings or expressing gratitude to others, exhibit increased satisfaction with relationships and fewer symptoms of physical illness.

经常进行感恩练习的人，例如列举自己的福分或向他人表达谢意，在人际关系中会表现出更高的满意度，身体疾病的症状也更少。

And the benefits are not only psychological and physical.

而感恩的好处并不局限于心理和身体层面。

They may also be moral—those who practice gratitude also view their lives less materialistically and suffer from less envy.

还可能体现在道德层面——那些践行感恩的人对生活的物质追求也更淡然，嫉妒心理也更少。

G

There are multiple explanations for such benefits of gratefulness.

关于感恩为何能带来这些益处，有多种解释。

One is the fact that expressing gratitude encourages others to continue being generous, thus promoting a virtuous cycle of goodness in relationships.

其中一个原因是，表达感激会鼓励他人继续保持慷慨，从而在人际关系中形成善意的良性循环。

Similarly, grateful people may be more likely to reciprocate with acts of kindness of their own.

同样，懂得感恩的人可能也更愿意以善意的行为回馈他人。

Broadly speaking, a community in which people feel grateful to one another is likely to be a more pleasant place to live than one characterized by mutual suspicion and resentment.

从整体上看，一个人与人之间相互感激的社区，往往会比一个充斥着相互猜疑和怨恨的社区更令人愉悦。

The beneficial effects of gratitude may extend even further.

感恩的益处可能远不止于此。

For example, when many people feel good about what someone else has done for them, they experience a sense of being lifted up, with a corresponding enhancement of their regard for humanity.

例如，当许多人因别人为自己所做的事而心怀感激时，他们会感到精神为之一振，同时也更加尊重人类。

Some are inspired to attempt to become better people themselves, doing more to help bring out the best in others and bringing more goodness into the world around them.

有些人因此受到激励，努力让自己成为更好的人，更多地帮助他人发挥其最美好的一面，并为周围的世界带来更多善意。

H

Gratitude also tends to strengthen a sense of connection with others.

感恩还往往能增强人与人之间的联结感。

When people want to do good things that inspire gratitude, the level of dedication in relationships tends to grow and relationships seem to last longer.

当人们乐于做那些能激发感恩之事时，人际关系中的投入程度往往会加深，关系也似乎更加持久。

And when people feel more connected, they are more likely to choose to spend their time with one another and demonstrate their feelings of affection in daily acts.

而当人们感到彼此更加亲近时，他们更可能选择共度时光，并在日常举动中表达关爱之情。

I

Of course, acts of kindness can also foster discomfort.

当然，善举也可能带来不安。

For example, if people feel they are not worthy of kindness or suspect that some ulterior motive lies behind it, the benefits of gratitude will not be realized.

例如，如果人们觉得自己不配得到善待，或怀疑善举背后另有隐情，感恩的益处便无法实现。

Likewise, receiving a kindness can give rise to a sense of indebtedness, leaving beneficiaries feeling that they must now pay back whatever good they have received.

同样，接受善意也会让人产生负债感，使受惠者觉得必须回报所受之恩。

Gratitude can flourish only if people are secure enough in themselves and sufficiently trusting to allow it to do so.

只有当人们内心足够安全、足够信任他人时，感恩才能真正滋长。

Another obstacle to gratitude is often called a sense of entitlement.

感恩的另一个障碍通常被称为理应得利感。

Instead of experiencing a benefaction as a good turn, people sometimes regard it as a mere payment of what they are owed, for which no one deserves any moral credit.

人们有时并不把受助视为别人的一番好意，而是将其看作对方在偿还所欠之债，因而无人值得任何道德上的褒奖。

J

There are a number of practical steps anyone can take to promote a sense of gratitude.

任何人都可以采取一些切实可行的做法来培养感恩之心。

One is simply spending time on a regular basis thinking about someone who has made a difference, or perhaps writing a thank-you note or expressing such gratitude in person.

其中之一，就是定期花些时间想想那个曾对自己产生重要影响的人，或者写一封感谢信，抑或当面向对方表达谢意。

Others are found in ancient religious disciplines, such as reflecting on benefactions received from another person or actually praying for the health and happiness of a benefactor.

另一些做法见于古老的宗教修习，比如默想他人给予自己的恩惠，或者切实地为恩人的健康与幸福祈祷。

In addition to benefactions received, it is also possible to focus on opportunities to do good oneself, whether those acted on in the past or hoped for in the future.

除了感恩所受的恩惠，也可以把注意力放在自己行善的机会上，无论这些机会是过去已经把握的，还是将来有望实现的。

Some people are most grateful not for what others have done for them but for chances they enjoyed to help others.

有些人最感激的，不是别人为他们做了什么，而是自己曾有过帮助他人的机会。

In regularly reflecting on the things in his life he is grateful for, Defoe's Crusoe believes that he becomes a far better person than he would have been had he remained in the society from which he originally set out on his voyage.

笛福笔下的鲁滨逊·克鲁索经常反思生活中值得感恩的事物，他相信，与留在当初启程远航时所在的那个社会相比，自己因此成为了远为更好的人。

K

Reflecting on generosity and gratitude, the great basketball coach John Wooden once offered two counsels to his players and students.

伟大的篮球教练约翰·伍登在谈及慷慨与感恩时，曾向他的球员和学生提出两条忠告。

First, he said, "It is impossible to have a perfect day unless you have done something for someone who will never be able to repay you."

他说，第一条是："除非你为某个永远无力回报你的人做些什么，否则你不可能拥有完美的一天。"

In saying this, Wooden sought to promote purely generous acts, as opposed to those performed with an expectation of reward.

说这番话，伍登意在倡导纯粹出于慷慨的行为，而不是那些指望回报才做的行为。

Second, he said, "Give thanks for your blessings every day."

他说，第二条是："每天都要为你的福分感恩。"

L

Some faith traditions incorporate such practices into the rhythm of daily life.

有些信仰传统把这类做法融入日常生活之中。

For example, adherents of some religions offer prayers of thanksgiving every morning before rising and every night before lying down to sleep.

例如，某些宗教的信徒每天早晨起床前和每天晚上入睡前都要做感恩祈祷。

Others offer thanks throughout the day, such as before meals.

另一些人则一天中多次表达感恩，比如在饭前。

Other less frequent special events, such as births, deaths and marriages, may also be heralded by such prayers.

其他不太常见的特殊事件，如出生、死亡和结婚等，也可能伴随着这样的祈祷。

M

When Defoe depicted Robinson Crusoe making thanksgiving a daily part of his island life, he was anticipating findings in social science and medicine that would not appear for hundreds of years.

当笛福笔下的鲁滨逊·克鲁索把感恩作为荒岛日常生活的一部分时，他其实预见了数百年后才出现的社会科学和医学研究发现。

Yet he was also reflecting the wisdom of religious and philosophical traditions that extend back thousands of years.

然而，他也同时反映了绵延数千年的宗教与哲学传统中的智慧。

Gratitude is one of the healthiest and most nourishing of all states of mind, and those who adopt it as a habit are enriching not only their own lives but also the lives of those around them.

感恩是最健康、最有益于心灵滋养的心态之一，那些将其养成习惯的人，不仅在丰富自己的生活，也在丰富身边人的生活。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1447 词 · 22 句

P1

Technology is never a neutral tool for achieving human ends.

技术从来都不是实现人类目的的中立工具。

Technological innovations reshape people as they use these innovations to control their environment.

人们在利用这些创新成果来控制自身环境的同时，技术创新也在重塑着他们。

Artificial intelligence, for example, is altering humanity.

例如，人工智能正在改变着人性本身。

P2

While the term AI conjures up anxieties about killer robots or catastrophic levels of unemployment, there are other, deeper implications.

虽然一提到人工智能，人们便会联想到杀人机器人或灾难性失业水平等令人焦虑的图景，但它还有其他更深层的含义。

As AI increasingly shapes the human experience, how does this change what it means to be human?

随着人工智能日益塑造着人类的经验体验，这将如何改变"何为人类"的内涵？

Central to the problem is a person's capacity to make choices, particularly judgments that have moral implications.

这一问题的核心在于人做出选择的能力，尤其是做出具有道德含义的判断的能力。

P3

Aristotle argued that the capacity for making practical judgments depends on regularly making them—on habit and practice.

亚里士多德认为，做出实践判断的能力取决于经常做出这类判断——取决于习惯与实践。

We see the emergence of machines as substitute judges in a variety of everyday contexts as a potential threat to people learning how to effectively exercise judgment themselves.

我们看到，机器作为替代性裁判出现在各种日常场景中，这可能会威胁到人们学习如何有效行使自身判断力。

P4

In the workplace, managers routinely make decisions about who to hire or fire and which loan to approve, to name a few.

在职场中，管理者例行性地决定录用或解雇谁、批准哪笔贷款等等，不一而足。

These are areas where algorithmic prescription is replacing human judgment, and so people who might have had the chance to develop practical judgment in these areas no longer will.

这些正是算法规定正在取代人类判断的领域，因此，那些原本可能有机会在这些领域中培养实践判断力的人，将不再拥有这样的机会。

P5

Recommendation engines, which are increasingly prevalent intermediaries in people's consumption of culture, may serve to constrain choice and minimize luck.

推荐引擎作为人们文化消费中日益普遍的中介，可能会限制人们的选择、减少偶然性。

By presenting consumers with algorithmically selected choices of what to watch, read, stream and visit next, companies are replacing human taste with machine taste.

企业通过向消费者呈现算法筛选出的下一步观看、阅读、流媒体播放和访问的内容，正以机器的品味取代人的品味。

In one sense, this is helpful.

从某种意义上说，这是有帮助的。

After all, machines can survey a wider range of choices than any individual is likely to have the time or energy to do on their own.

毕竟，机器能够审视的选择范围，比任何个人凭自己的时间和精力可能做到的都要广。

P6

At the same time, though, this selection is optimizing for what people are likely to prefer based on what they've preferred in the past.

然而与此同时，这种筛选依据的是人们过去所偏好的东西，来优化他们未来可能偏好的选择。

We think there is some risk that people's options will be constrained by their past in a new and unanticipated way.

我们认为，人们的可选项有可能会以一种全新而始料未及的方式被自身过去所限定，这存在一定风险。

P7

As machine learning algorithms improve and as they train on more extensive data sets, larger parts of everyday life are likely to become utterly predictable.

随着机器学习算法不断改进，且用更庞杂的数据集加以训练，日常生活的更大部分很可能变得完全可预测。

The predictions are going to get better and better, and they will ultimately make common experiences more efficient and pleasant.

预测会越来越准确，最终将使常见体验变得更高效、更令人愉悦。

P8

Algorithms could soon—if they don't already—have a better idea about which show you'd like to watch next and which job candidate you should hire than you do.

算法可能很快——若非已然如此——就比你自己更清楚你接下来想看什么节目、该录用哪位求职者。

One day, humans may even find a way for machines to make these decisions without some of the biases that humans typically display.

终有一天，人类甚至可能找到办法，让机器做出这些决定，同时避免人类通常表现出的某些偏见。

P9

But to the extent that unpredictability is part of how people understand themselves and part of what people like about themselves, humanity is in the process of losing something significant.

然而，就不可预测性既是人们理解自我的一部分、也是人们喜爱自身之处的一部分而言，人类正在失去某种意义重大的东西。

As they become more and more predictable, the creatures inhabiting the increasingly AI-mediated world will become less and less like us.

随着它们变得越来越可预测，栖居于这个日益由人工智能中介的世界中的生灵，将变得越来越不像我们人类。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2450 词 · 28 句

P1

Phonics, which involves sounding out words syllable by syllable, is the best way to teach children to read.

自然拼读法（即逐个音节拼读单词）是教孩子阅读的最佳方法。

But in many classrooms, this can be a dirty word.

但在许多课堂上，它却成了一个禁忌。

So much so that some teachers have had to sneak phonics teaching materials into the classroom.

以至于有些教师不得不把自然拼读的教学材料偷偷带进课堂。

Most American children are taught to read in a way that study after study has found to be wrong.

大多数美国孩子被教导阅读的方式，一项又一项研究已经证明是错误的。

P2

The consequences of this are striking.

这种情况的后果触目惊心。

Less than half of all American adults were proficient readers in 2017.

2017年，美国成年人中阅读能力达到熟练水平的不足一半。

American fourth graders rank 15th on the Progress in International Literacy Study, an international exam.

在国际读写素养进展研究这一国际考试中，美国四年级学生排名第15位。

P3

America is stuck in a debate about teaching children to read that has been going on for decades.

美国深陷于一场关于儿童阅读教学的争论之中，这场争论已经持续了几十年。

Some advocate teaching symbol-sound relationships (the sound k can be spelled as c, k, ck, or ch), known as phonics.

一些人主张教授符号与发音之间的对应关系（例如/k/这个音可以拼写为c、k、ck或ch），这就是所谓的自然拼读法。

Others support an immersive approach (using pictures of a cat to learn the word cat), known as "whole language".

另一些人则支持沉浸式方法（例如用猫的图片来学习cat这个单词），这就是所谓的“整体语言”教学法。

Most teachers today, almost three out of four according to a survey by the Ed Week Research Centre in 2019, use a mix called "balanced literacy".

如今的大多数教师——据《教育周刊》研究中心2019年的一项调查，近四分之三——采用的是一种被称为“均衡读写”的混合方法。

This combination of methods is ineffective.

这种把各种方法混合在一起的做法并无实效。

"You can't sprinkle in a little phonics," says Tenette Smith, executive director of elementary education and reading at Mississippi's education department.

“你不能只在教学中零星掺入一点自然拼读法，”密西西比州教育部小学教育与阅读执行主任特内特·史密斯说。

"It has to be systematic and explicitly taught."

“它必须系统化，并且明确地教给学生。”

P4

Mississippi, often behind in social policy, has set an example here.

密西西比州在社会政策方面常常落在后面，却在这件事上树立了榜样。

In a state once notorious for its low reading scores, the Mississippi state legislature passed new literacy standards in 2013.

在一个曾因阅读成绩低下而臭名昭著的州，密西西比州议会于2013年通过了新的读写能力标准。

Since then Mississippi has seen remarkable gains.

自那以来，密西西比州取得了显著进步。

Its fourth graders have moved from 49th (out of 50 states) to 29th on the National Assessment of Educational Progress, a nationwide exam.

在全美教育进步评估（一项全国性考试）中，该州四年级学生的排名从第49位（在50个州中）升至第29位。

In 2019 it was the only state to improve its scores.

2019年，它是唯一一个分数有所提高的州。

For the first time since measurement began, Mississippi's pupils are now average readers, a remarkable achievement in such a poor state.

自开始测量以来，密西西比州的学生如今首次达到了平均阅读水平，对于这样一个贫困州而言，这是一项了不起的成就。

P5

Mississippi's success is attributed to implementing reading methods supported by a body of research known as the science of reading.

密西西比州的成功归功于采用了得到一批被称为“阅读科学”的研究支持的阅读教学方法。

In 1997 Congress requested the National Institute of Child Health and Human Development and the Department of Education to convene a National Reading Panel to end the "reading wars" and synthesize the evidence.

1997年，国会要求美国国家儿童健康与人类发展研究院和教育部召集成立一个全国阅读小组，以终结“阅读战争”并综合相关证据。

The panel found that phonics, along with explicit instruction in phonemic awareness, fluency and comprehension, worked best.

该小组发现，自然拼读法配合对音素意识、流利度和理解力的明确教学，效果最好。

P6

Yet over two decades on, "balanced literacy" is still being taught in classrooms.

然而二十多年过去了，“平衡读写教学法”仍在课堂上被使用。

But advances in statistics and brain imaging have disproved the whole-language method.

然而，统计学和脑成像技术的进步已经推翻了全语言教学法。

To the teacher who is a proficient reader, literacy seems like a natural process that requires educated guessing, rather than the deliberate process emphasized by phonics.

对于阅读熟练的教师而言，识字似乎是一个自然而然的过程，需要的是有根据的推测，而不是拼读法所强调的那种刻意的练习过程。

Teachers can imagine that they learned to read through osmosis when they were children.

教师们可能会以为自己小时候是潜移默化地学会阅读的。

Without proper training, they bring this to classrooms.

如果缺乏适当的培训，他们就会把这种观念带进课堂。
