By now, it is generally accepted that regular exercise comes with an assortment of health benefits for people who maintain the habit over time.

如今，人们普遍认为，长期保持运动习惯能给健康带来多方面的益处。

But is it ever too late to start?

但是，开始运动会不会为时已晚呢？

New research looked at people who increased their exercise levels after their 40s beyond levels they had when they were younger and those individuals showed declines in their risk of early mortality which were similar to those of people who exercised consistently throughout their lives.

一项新研究考察了那些在四十多岁后将运动量提升到超过年轻时水平的人群，结果发现，这些人的早亡风险有所下降，其降幅与一生坚持运动的人相当。

This was a drop of 32% to 35% compared to people who did not exercise.

与不运动的人相比，这一降幅为32%至35%。

Those who began exercising in middle age also saw reductions in their incidence of chronic diseases, such as heart disease and cancer, which were similar to the steady exercisers.

那些从中年才开始运动的人，其心脏病和癌症等慢性疾病的发病率也有所下降，降幅与长期坚持运动的人相近。

So why bother exercising consistently throughout your life?

那么，又何必一生都坚持运动呢？

The authors of the study concede that some might interpret the study's findings to mean that they can delay starting an exercise program but this would be a mistake.

该研究的作者承认，有些人可能会据此认为自己可以推迟开始锻炼计划，但这种想法是错误的。

There are other benefits, both mental and physical, to staying consistently active that we should not disregard.

坚持运动还有其他身心方面的好处，我们不应忽视。

These real health benefits include better cognitive functioning and lowered risk of gaining weight.

这些实实在在的健康益处包括更好的认知功能以及降低体重增加的风险。

The latter is particularly important given the global obesity epidemic.

考虑到全球肥胖流行的趋势，后一点尤为重要。

In addition, experts assert that starting younger is better because, if you make a habit of exercising when you are young, you are more likely to keep up those patterns later in life.

此外，专家指出，趁年轻开始运动更好，因为如果在年轻时养成运动习惯，日后更有可能坚持这一模式。

It is ideal, then, for people to start exercising when they are children or adolescents, and it is good to maintain an active lifestyle at all times regardless of your age.

因此，最好从小就养成运动习惯，而且无论年龄大小，始终保持积极的生活方式都是有益的。

However, this study does indicate that, if you have not been active, you can still benefit if you start becoming active in your 40s and 50s.

不过，这项研究确实表明，即使你过去一直不怎么活动，只要在四五十岁时开始积极运动，仍然能从中受益。

A

Like most chefs, Flippy is not afraid of hard work.

和大多数厨师一样，Flippy 不怕辛苦。

Since last summer, that has meant 11 am until 7 pm shifts at Caliburger in Pasadena, California, as well as short assignments at Chick-N-Tots at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles.

从去年夏天起，这意味着要在加州帕萨迪纳的 Caliburger 餐厅从上午11点工作到晚上7点，还要在洛杉矶道奇体育场的 Chick-N-Tots 餐厅完成一些短期任务。

These are long hours of repetitive work, where the boss demands absolute consistency.

这是漫长而重复的工作时间，老板要求绝对的一致性。

But you won't hear Flippy complain or say anything, in fact.

但你不会听到 Flippy 抱怨，事实上，它连一句话都不会说。

For Flippy is a robot — a cloud-connected mechanical arm with 3D thermal scanners for eyes — that can flip burgers or fry 80 baskets of food an hour, monitor that food and even clean up afterwards.

因为 Flippy 是一个机器人——一只接入云端的机械臂，眼睛是三维热成像扫描仪——它每小时能翻汉堡或炸80篮食物，监控食物的状态，甚至还能在事后清理打扫。

B

Flippy was created by Miso Robotics, part of the Cali Group, which is described by its CEO, John Miller, as: "A technology company that happens to sell cheeseburgers."

Flippy 由 Miso Robotics 创造，该公司隶属于 Cali Group，其首席执行官约翰·米勒这样形容这家公司："一家碰巧卖芝士汉堡的科技公司。"

Cali creates new machines that it road-tests in its Caliburger restaurants across the world, into which Flippy will be deployed this year.

Cali 开发新机器，并在其遍布全球的 Caliburger 餐厅进行实地测试，今年 Flippy 将在这些餐厅投入使用。

And Flippy is not alone.

而且 Flippy 并非孤例。

Also in California, Bear Robotics has developed a self-guiding robot, Penny, which has so far served 40,000 diners.

同样在加州，Bear Robotics 开发了一款自主导航机器人 Penny，迄今为止已为四万名顾客提供过服务。

C

Ten years ago, robot-chefs and waiters were pure science fiction.

十年前，机器人厨师和服务员还纯粹是科幻小说中的事物。

Today, they are a reality, and at prices that make them a plausible investment.

如今，它们已成为现实，而且价格合理，是一项值得考虑的投资。

From May, Penny will be shipped on a subscription basis that offers "an immediate return", says Bear Robotics' chief operating officer, Juan Higueros.

Bear Robotics 首席运营官胡安·伊格罗斯表示，从五月起，Penny 将以订阅方式推出，能够"立即带来回报"。

Flippy will cost a reported $60,000 to $100,000.

据报道，Flippy的售价在6万至10万美元之间。

Fully automated burger restaurants managed with minimal human oversight — where customers order at screens, pay electronically, and eat food cooked and delivered by robots — are now a possibility.

几乎无需人工监管的全自动汉堡餐厅如今已不再是幻想——顾客在屏幕上点餐、电子支付，食物由机器人烹饪和送达。

D

The technology exists; it just needs knitting together cost-effectively and in a way diners buy into.

技术已经具备，只是需要以经济高效的方式将各个环节整合起来，并让食客买账。

In an industry keen to slash labor costs and increase profits, further automation seems inevitable.

在这样一个急于削减人力成本、提高利润的行业中，进一步自动化似乎势在必行。

After an eight-month test at Kang Nam Tofu House in California, Bear Robotics credited Penny with driving a 28% increase in sales.

在加州康南豆腐馆（Kang Nam Tofu House）为期八个月的试用之后，Bear Robotics公司称Penny送餐机器人使餐厅销售额增长了28%。

Meanwhile, in US trials, the self-ordering screens that McDonald's is currently installing at its 1,300 UK restaurants yielded a reported 30% rise in order values.

与此同时，在美国的试用中，麦当劳目前正在其1300家英国餐厅安装的自助点餐屏幕，据称使每单消费金额提升了30%。

E

Personalisation is seen as key to the appeal of this technology, alongside its ability to reliably push so-called "upsells" (meal deals, extras, larger drinks) to customers.

这项技术的吸引力被认为关键在于个性化，以及它能够稳定地向顾客推销所谓的"加购项"（套餐、加料、大杯饮料）。

Soon when you log into a restaurant app, it will be able to use your data and purchasing history to recommend dishes to you — factoring in everything from the weather to, if you are ordering at a screen that can read your face, your mood.

不久的将来，当你登录一家餐厅的App时，它就能利用你的数据和购买记录为你推荐菜品——从天气到你的情绪，一切因素都会被纳入考量（如果你是在一台能识别人脸的屏幕前点餐的话）。

F

"Right now people compromise on choice. We're presented with a one-size-fits-all menu. Ultimately, technology can allow us to make personal choices. You love spicy food and chicken? Here's the dish people like you mostly order,"

"目前人们在选择上有所妥协，摆在我们面前的是一份千篇一律的菜单。而技术最终能让我们做出个性化选择。你爱吃辣，喜欢鸡肉？这是和你口味相似的人最常点的菜品。"

says Tom Weaver, CEO at the hospitality tech company Flyt.

餐饮科技公司Flyt的首席执行官汤姆·韦弗（Tom Weaver）说道。

He talks enthusiastically about the Helsinki pop-up Take-In, a full-service restaurant where diners ordered dishes in from various delivery services.

他兴致勃勃地谈起赫尔辛基的快闪餐厅Take-In，那是一家全服务餐厅，顾客可以点选多家外卖平台送来的菜品。

"For the first time in a few hundred years, digital is allowing us to create new versions of restaurants."

"几百年来第一次，数字技术让我们得以创造出全新形态的餐厅。"

G

If high-street fast food is automation-ready, its impact on high-end restaurants is likely to be subtler and slower.

如果说街头快餐已经具备了自动化条件，那么自动化对高端餐厅的影响可能会更微妙、也来得更慢。

"It'll probably be a very long time before we see a five-star robot-chef," says Noel Sharkey, professor of AI and robotics at the University of Sheffield.

谢菲尔德大学人工智能与机器人学教授诺埃尔·夏基说：“我们大概还要等很久，才能看到五星级的机器人厨师。”

"Apart from the AI creativity gap, great cooking involves a subtle understanding of ingredients and delicate cooking that would be enormously challenging for robots. Placing fragile foods on plates would be incredibly slow."

“除了人工智能在创造力上的差距之外，高超的烹饪还涉及对食材的细腻理解和精细烹调，这对机器人来说是极大的挑战。把易碎的食物摆放到盘子里，对它们来说会慢得惊人。”

H

Currently, robots have limited functionality.

目前，机器人的功能还很有限。

A human needs to load Penny with plates that diners lift off themselves.

仍需人工把餐盘装到“佩妮”机器人上，再由食客自行取下餐盘。

At the robot-powered US pizza-delivery company Zume, the more difficult jobs, such as topping pizzas, are still done by humans.

在美国机器人披萨配送公司Zume，难度较大的工作——比如给披萨加配料——目前仍由人工完成。

But Moley, which will launch soon, offers an idea of the sophistication to come.

但即将推出的Moley机器人厨师，让人得以一窥未来技术的精细程度。

Modelled on the movements of a MasterChef contest winner, Tim Anderson, its robotic arms are fixed over a stove and programmed to prepare dishes from raw ingredients.

它以《厨艺大师》比赛冠军蒂姆·安德森的动作作为原型，机械臂固定在炉灶上方，可按照程序从原料开始制作菜肴。

It is designed for domestic use, but a commercial version is planned that opens up the possibility of restaurant kitchens automating even highly technically challenging tasks.

它面向家用，但公司计划推出商用版本，这意味着餐厅厨房甚至有望将技术难度很高的任务也实现自动化。

I

For now, the kitchen technology that QSR Automations supplies to Mitchells & Butlers' pubs or KFC is less glamorous.

目前，QSR Automations公司为米其尔与巴特勒旗下的酒吧或肯德基提供的厨房技术就没那么光鲜了。

Rather than having a chef shouting out diners' orders, the QSR system automatically distributes those orders across screens.

QSR系统不需要厨师高声喊出食客的点单，而是自动把这些订单分发到各个屏幕上。

The kitchen screens then tell chefs when to start cooking each dish, so that your plate of fish, chips and peas comes together at the right time.

厨房屏幕随后会提示厨师何时开始烹饪每道菜，好让你那盘炸鱼、薯条和青豆能在恰当的时间一同上桌。

J

The restaurant industry is currently suffering a severe shortage of skilled chefs — a deficit of 11,000, according to the skills agency People 1st.

餐饮业目前正面临技术娴熟厨师严重短缺的问题——据技能机构 People 1st 称，缺口达 1.1 万名。

"Automation systems designed to make the process more idiot-proof are trying to bridge that gap," says Stefan Chomka, the editor of Restaurant magazine.

"旨在让整个流程更加'傻瓜化'的自动化系统正在试图填补这一缺口，"《餐厅》杂志主编斯特凡·乔姆卡说。

K

In terms of simplifying or enhancing the customer experience, online restaurant booking has been the easily-won success of the internet age.

就简化或提升顾客体验而言，网上订餐早已是互联网时代唾手可得的成功。

Automated at-table ordering and payment is seen as the next landmark.

餐桌自助点餐与自动支付被视为下一个里程碑。

Until then, early adopters, such as the Manchester bar owner Andy Smith, who uses the Ordoo app at his venue 33 Oldham Street, may well experience growing but "lower than expected" acceptance.

在此之前，像曼彻斯特酒吧老板安迪·史密斯这样的早期采用者——他在自己的店铺 33 Oldham Street 使用 Ordoo 应用——很可能面临接受度不断增长却"低于预期"的局面。

L

Embarrassing failures in restaurant tech frequently hit the headlines.

餐饮科技领域令人尴尬的失败屡屡登上新闻头条。

The TGI Fridays drone that injured a Brooklyn Daily photographer; US burger chain Shake Shack having to retreat from plans for entirely cashless ordering sites.

TGI Fridays 的无人机弄伤了《布鲁克林日报》的一名摄影师；美国汉堡连锁品牌 Shake Shack 也不得不放弃全面推行无现金点餐门店的计划。

But, says Chomka, such technology could miss the mark if it only offers "solutions to problems that don't really exist".

但乔姆卡说，如果这类技术只是提供"解决那些并不真正存在的问题的方案"，那它就可能偏离方向。

"Payments being automatic" could be useful, he adds.

"自动付款"可能会有用，他补充道。

But, in an expensive restaurant, "how quick do you need dinner to be?"

但在高档餐厅，"你需要晚餐上得多快呢？"

M

That attachment to the human art of hospitality will be heartening for staff who see automation as a threat to jobs.

对人性化待客之道的这份执着，会让那些将自动化视为就业威胁的员工感到欣慰。

"Unite is concerned that without safeguards, any benefits will bypass workers in low-paying jobs, such as waiters," says the union's London regional officer, Dave Turnbull.

"Unite 联合工会担心，如果没有保障措施，任何好处都不会惠及服务员等低薪岗位的劳动者，"该工会伦敦地区官员戴夫·特恩布尔说。

"We're urging Members of Parliament to ensure automation does not just benefit a wealthy few like previous industrial revolutions."

我们正敦促议会成员确保自动化不会像以往的工业革命一样，仅仅让少数富人受益。

N

The industry principle is that automation will allow it to redeploy staff more effectively.

该行业的原则是，自动化将使其能够更有效地重新调配员工。

Jobs will change, if not immediately decrease in number.

工作岗位将会改变，即便其数量不会立即减少。

"Self-order screens mean more demand for people front-of-house. Last year, we recruited 1,000 new managers,"

"自助点餐屏幕意味着对前厅人员的需求增加。去年，我们新招募了1000名经理，"

says McDonald's COO, Jason Clark.

麦当劳首席运营官贾森·克拉克说道。

Caliburger has a programme to retrain staff as robot engineers or "chef techs".

Caliburger有一个将员工再培训为机器人工程师或"厨师技师"的项目。

O

But ultimately the chance to reduce overheads by employing fewer people or at lower rates will be what attracts big brands to technology.

但归根结底，通过雇佣更少的人或以更低的薪酬来降低运营成本的机会，才是吸引大品牌采用技术的真正原因。

Invariably, over the next 10 years, jobs will go in conventional bricks-built restaurants, not least because automation will boost their rapidly growing rival: delivery.

未来十年间，传统砖砌餐厅的岗位无疑将会减少，尤其是因为自动化会助推其迅速发展的竞争对手——外卖配送。

Companies such as Domino and Just Eat have both experimented with robot and drone delivery, which is already legal in Reykjavik, Iceland.

Domino和Just Eat等公司都已尝试机器人和无人机配送，这种配送方式在冰岛雷克雅未克已经合法。

"Robot food deliveries are the future," says Noel Sharkey.

诺尔·沙基说道："机器人送餐是未来的趋势。"

"Small, 4 mph ground robots are safe and will work 24 hours a day."

"时速4英里的小型地面机器人安全可靠，可以一天24小时工作。"

P

Combined with machine learning of anticipated order volumes and the minute-by-minute cooking data generated by systems such as QSR, that robot fleet could deliver almost instantly.

再加上对预计订单量的机器学习，以及QSR等系统生成的逐分钟烹饪数据，这个机器人车队几乎可以实现即时配送。

"Restaurants will prepare food ahead of orders coming in," predicts Just Eat's director of engineering, Daniel Richardson.

“餐厅会提前备好餐食，等订单一到就直接出餐，”Just Eat 工程总监丹尼尔·理查森预测道。

"I'll be able to say 'Let's have pizza' to my family, my intelligent assistant will hear and 10 minutes later the food will be at my door."

“我只需对家人说一句‘我们吃披萨吧’，我的智能助手就会听到，十分钟后餐食便会送到家门口。”

No one is putting an exact date on it yet.

目前还没有人给出确切的时间表。

But in the dining room and on your doorstep, the robots are coming.

但在餐厅和你家门口，机器人已经悄然而至。

P1

Many see friendships as a comfort blanket: a shoulder to cry on, a reliable soul to confide in.

许多人把友谊看作一张安慰毯：一个可以依靠哭泣的肩膀，一个可以倾诉心声的可靠灵魂。

However, more often than not, in our eagerness to seek individual success, we lose friends along the way and that has become part of the process.

然而，我们往往在急于追求个人成功的过程中，在途中失去了朋友，而这已成为这一过程的一部分。

P2

The chaos and never-ending turmoil that accompanies everyday life is an aggressive obstacle in the pursuit of stable friendships.

日常生活中的混乱与永无休止的动荡，是追求稳定友谊的严重障碍。

As students, we use this excuse to shrug off losing people who we once thought to be loved ones.

作为学生，我们用这种借口来对失去曾经以为是至亲至爱的人不以为然。

P3

Instead of viewing our lives as a race, where everyone is on their own path to some form of success, we must relearn to value the connections we make along the track.

我们不应把生活看作一场竞赛，人人都在各自的道路上奔向某种成功，而必须重新学会珍视我们在途中建立的种种联系。

We must reevaluate the harm of radical, individual freedom.

我们必须重新审视激进的个人自由所带来的危害。

P4

Radical individualism elevates the freedom of the individual over the collective.

激进的个人主义将个人自由置于集体之上。

It argues, for instance, that universal healthcare, an example of collectivism, threatened the dream of many Americans.

例如，它认为全民医疗——一个集体主义的例子——威胁了许多美国人的梦想。

P5

Many of us have fallen victim to an individualistic culture.

我们很多人都已成为个人主义文化的受害者。

Society tends to celebrate personal achievement and commend it based on the assumption that winning or succeeding is a consequence of working harder than the next person.

社会往往颂扬个人成就并加以赞赏，其依据是这样一个假设：获胜或成功是比别人更加努力工作的结果。

P6

As a society, we idolize the concept of "losing friends on the road to success".

作为一个社会，我们把“在通往成功的路上失去朋友”这一观念奉为理想。

I remember hearing in high school, "Don't worry about losing friendships, just focus on study and getting into college. That's all that will matter in the long run,"

我记得上高中时听过这样的话：“别担心失去友谊，只管专心学习、考上大学。从长远来看，那才是唯一重要的事。”

a sentiment that is not unique to my experience.

这一感受并非我的独有经历。

It's preached in many success stories, and it's embedded in the way we see growth.

它被反复宣讲于众多成功故事之中，并深深嵌入我们看待成长的方式。

Motivational speakers will push their audience to be complacent to the inevitable end of losing friends.

励志演讲者会鼓动听众接受一个必然的结局——失去朋友。

Spend too much time with friends?

和朋友相处太久？

You're losing time better utilized on your achievements.

你这是在浪费本可用于成就自我的时间。

Invest time in your relationships?

把时间投入到人际关系中？

Invest time in your work instead.

还不如把时间投入工作。

P7

This constant need to succeed has blinded us from seeing through the whirlwind of change that accompanies our everyday lives.

这种对成功的持续追求，使我们对伴随日常生活而来的种种变迁视而不见。

We are expected to tangibly succeed, earn awards, get higher positions, earn better grades.

我们被期望取得实实在在的成功、获得奖项、登上更高的职位、取得更好的成绩。

In order to succeed, we lose friends, and that has sadly become normalized.

为了成功，我们失去了朋友，而令人悲哀的是，这一切已被视为常态。

P8

We cannot be complacent with losing friendships because they are our anchors.

我们不能对失去友谊听之任之，因为朋友是我们的依靠。

Yes, pursue success.

是的，去追求成功吧。

Challenge yourself.

挑战自我。

Push limits.

突破极限。

But contrary to all the self-proclaimed success experts, doing it alone does not make you more successful or any better than someone who achieved it with the help of others.

但与所有自诩为成功专家的人所宣扬的相反，独自奋斗并不能让你更成功，也不能让你比那些在他人帮助下取得成功的人更优秀。

P9

Put effort into fostering healthy relationships, and maintaining them through your busy schedule that seems like it leaves no time for anyone else.

要用心去培育健康的人际关系，即便你的日程安排看似忙得没有时间陪伴任何人，也要努力维系这些关系。

Although we may be living in a society that treasures the independent pursuit of achievement, having people to support and love you on the way is far more important than any end result.

尽管我们所处的社会崇尚独立追求成就，但在这条路上有人支持你、爱你，远比任何最终成果都更加重要。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2455 词 · 24 句

P1

During his acceptance speech for the Nobel Peace Prize, Martin Luther King Jr. said, "I believe that unarmed truth and unconditional love will have the final word in reality."

在诺贝尔和平奖颁奖致辞中，马丁·路德·金说：“我相信，手无寸铁的真理和无条件的爱终将在现实中胜出。”

P2

More than five decades later, our society is still struggling to separate truth from fantasy, fact from fiction.

半个多世纪过去了，我们的社会仍在艰难地分辨真伪、辨别虚实。

From disputes over the climate change to debates about how slavery is taught, America is at a crossroads.

从关于气候变化的争议到关于奴隶制应如何教授的辩论，美国正处在十字路口。

And the battle for equity is at the heart of these vital conversations.

而争取公平的斗争，正是这些至关重要对话的核心所在。

P3

Study after study shows that inequity has had appalling consequences.

一项又一项研究表明，不公平现象已造成触目惊心的后果。

A 2020 report by Citi indicated that "Black students could have increased their lifetime incomes $90 – $113 billion" if equity issues related to higher education had been adequately addressed over the past two decades.

花旗集团2020年的一份报告指出，如果在过去二十年里高等教育领域相关的公平问题得到妥善解决，“黑人学生的终身收入本可增加900亿至1130亿美元”。

P4

This statistic represents a flood of issues we've failed to confront as a nation.

这一统计数据背后，是我们作为一个国家始终未能正视的大量问题。

It also highlights one truth: We're missing out on valuable opportunities to bridge the differences that divide us.

它也揭示了一个事实：我们正在错失弥合分歧、消除隔阂的宝贵机会。

However, by honoring King's legacy, we can get our country back on the path toward true equity.

然而，只要我们铭记金博士的精神遗产，就能让国家重新走上通往真正公平的道路。

P5

Students — and adults for that matter — simply cannot understand the conflicts we have today without understanding what caused them in the first place.

学生——其实成年人也不例外——如果不了解当今冲突的根源，就根本无法理解我们今天面临的矛盾。

That means we have a responsibility to help them make connections between US history and current events.

这意味着我们有责任帮助他们建立美国历史与当下时事之间的联系。

It also means giving them the tools they need to address inequity and bias in their own lives.

这也意味着要赋予他们必要的工具，让他们能够在自己的生活中应对不公平与偏见。

Adherence to these principles, along with a commitment to truthful dialogue, can help every student better appreciate how far we've come and more clearly see the roads we have yet to travel.

坚守这些原则，并秉持真诚对话的承诺，能帮助每一位学生更好地体会我们已经走了多远，也更清晰地看清我们尚需走完的道路。

P6

With more truthful analysis of the need for more qualified, diverse educators, we can improve our recruiting, training, and retention strategies for schools across the country.

只有更诚实地分析我们对更多高素质、多元化教育工作者的需求，我们才能改进全国各地学校的招聘、培训和留任策略。

P7

Unfortunately, our deeply rooted divisions are delaying these advancements.

遗憾的是，根深蒂固的分歧正延缓着这些进步。

A recent survey found that 77 percent of Americans believe the nation is "more divided than ever before".

最近的一项调查发现，77%的美国人认为这个国家"比以往任何时候都更加分裂"。

But reports of increased polarization shouldn't persuade us to look away from one another.

但是，关于极化加剧的种种报道，不应让我们转过头去、彼此回避。

Instead, they should compel us to address our shared issues head-on.

相反，这些报道应促使我们直面共同的问题。

P8

We are not, as some would suggest, living in a "post-racial society".

我们并没有像一些人所声称的那样，生活在一个"后种族社会"之中。

We simply cannot afford to keep our heads buried in the sand when it comes to racial inequity.

面对种族不平等，我们根本承担不起继续把头埋进沙子的代价。

Unless we get honest about intolerance, bias, and how these social diseases impact the ways we treat one another, injustice will continue to haunt us.

除非我们诚实地正视不宽容与偏见，正视这些社会顽疾如何影响我们彼此相待的方式，否则不公将继续纠缠着我们。

But our children and our grandchildren deserve better.

但我们的子孙后代理应得到更好的未来。

P9

On this Martin Luther King Day and beyond, let's commit to working together to disrupt the status quo.

在这个马丁·路德·金纪念日以及今后的日子里，让我们承诺携手合作，打破现状。

Let's continue to build a better, more inclusive nation; and in the words of Martin Luther King Jr., "A society at peace with itself, a society that can live with its conscience."

让我们继续建设一个更美好、更具包容性的国家；用马丁·路德·金的话说，"一个与自身和平共处的社会，一个能与良知相伴而存的社会。"
