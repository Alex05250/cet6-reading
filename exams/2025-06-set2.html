No beast on Earth is tougher than the tiny tardigrade.

地球上没有哪种动物比小小的水熊虫更顽强。

It can survive being frozen at -272° Celsius, being exposed to the vacuum of outer space and even being blasted with 500 times the dose of X-rays that would kill a human.

它能在-272摄氏度的极寒中存活，能暴露于外太空的真空环境，甚至能承受相当于致死剂量500倍的X射线轰击。

In other words, the creature can endure conditions that don't even exist on Earth.

换言之，这种生物能忍受地球上根本不存在的极端环境。

And researchers are looking to the microscopic animals to learn how to prepare humans and crops to handle the rigors of space travel.

科学家们正寄望于这种微小动物，探索如何帮助人类和农作物做好准备，以应对太空旅行的严酷考验。

The tardigrade's indestructibility stems from its adaptations to its environment—which may seem surprising, since it lives in seemingly comfortable places, like the cool, wet patches of moss that dot a garden wall.

水熊虫的坚不可摧源于它对环境的适应——这或许令人意外，因为它栖息在看似舒适的环境中，比如点缀于花园墙面上的一片片阴凉潮湿的苔藓。

But it turns out that a tardigrade's damp, mossy home can dry out many times each year.

但事实证明，水熊虫那潮湿的苔藓之家每年会多次经历干枯。

Drying is pretty catastrophic for most living things.

脱水对大多数生物而言堪称灾难。

It does damage to cells in some of the same ways that freezing, vacuum and radiation do.

它对细胞造成的损害在某些方面与冷冻、真空和辐射如出一辙。

Tardigrades, however, have evolved special strategies for dealing with these kinds of damage.

然而，水熊虫已经进化出应对这类损伤的特殊策略。

As a tardigrade dries out, its cells produce several strange proteins that are unlike anything found in other animals.

当水熊虫脱水时，其细胞会产生几种奇特的蛋白质，这些蛋白质在其他动物体内绝无仅有。

In water, the proteins are shapeless.

在水中，这些蛋白质毫无固定形态。

But as water disappears, the proteins self-assemble into long fibers that fill the cell's interior.

但随着水分消失，这些蛋白质会自行组装成长纤维，填满细胞内部。

The fibers support the cell's membranes and proteins, preventing them from breaking or unfolding.

这些纤维支撑着细胞的膜和蛋白质，防止它们破裂或解折叠。

Emulating tardigrades could one day help humans colonize outer space.

模仿水熊虫的特性，有朝一日或能帮助人类开拓外太空。

Food crops could be engineered to produce tardigrade proteins, allowing these organisms to grow more efficiently on spacecraft where levels of radiation are elevated compared with on Earth.

可以通过基因工程改造粮食作物，使其产生水熊虫蛋白，从而让这些作物在太空飞行器上更高效地生长，因为太空飞行器上的辐射水平高于地球上。

So if humans ever succeed in reaching the stars, they may accomplish this feat, in part, by standing on the shoulders of the tiny eight-legged endurance specialists in your backyard.

因此，如果人类有朝一日成功抵达群星，那么这一壮举在一定程度上是站在了你家后院里那些微小的八条腿耐力专家的肩膀上。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1194 词 · 60 句

A

As the scale and impacts of climate change become increasingly alarming, meat is a popular target for action.

随着气候变化的规模和影响日益令人担忧，肉类成为减排行动的热门靶点。

Advocates for the protection of the natural environment from destruction or pollution urge the public to eat less meat.

倡导保护自然环境免遭破坏或污染的人士呼吁公众少吃肉。

Some activists have even called for taxing meat to reduce consumption of it.

一些活动人士甚至呼吁对肉类征税以减少消费。

B

A key claim underlying these arguments holds that globally, meat production generates more greenhouse gases than the entire transportation sector.

这些论点背后的一个核心主张是，从全球范围来看，肉类生产所产生的温室气体超过了整个交通运输行业。

However, this claim is demonstrably wrong, as I will show.

然而，正如我将要证明的那样，这一说法显然是错误的。

And its persistence has led to false assumptions about the linkage between meat and climate change.

而这一说法的持续流传，导致人们对肉类与气候变化之间的关联产生了错误的假设。

C

My recent research focuses on ways in which animal agriculture affects air quality and climate change.

我近期的研究聚焦于畜牧业影响空气质量与气候变化的方式。

In my view, there are many reasons for either choosing animal protein or opting for a vegetarian selection.

在我看来，选择动物蛋白或选择素食，都有许多理由。

However, abandoning meat and meat products is not the environmental panacea many would have us believe.

然而，放弃肉类及肉制品并非许多人试图让我们相信的万能环保良方。

And if taken to an extreme, it also could have harmful nutritional consequences.

而且，如果走向极端，还可能带来有害的营养后果。

D

A healthy portion of meat's negative reputation centers on the assertion that livestock is the largest source of greenhouse gases worldwide.

肉类的负面名声有很大一部分源于这样一种断言：牲畜是全球最大的温室气体来源。

For example, an analysis published in 2009 by the World Watch Institute based in Washington, D.C.

例如，2009年总部位于华盛顿特区的世界观察研究所发布的一项分析。

asserted that 51 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions come from rearing and processing livestock.

断言称，全球51%的温室气体排放来自牲畜的饲养与加工。

According to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, the largest sources of U.S. greenhouse gas emissions in 2016 were electricity production (28 percent of total emissions), transportation (28 percent) and industry (22 percent).

据美国环境保护署（EPA）统计，2016年美国温室气体排放的最大来源是电力生产（占排放总量的28%）、交通运输（28%）和工业（22%）。

All of agriculture accounted for a total of 9 percent, but all of animal agriculture contributes less than half of this amount, representing 3.9 percent of the total greenhouse emission in the U.S. That is very different from claiming that livestock represents as much as or more than transportation.

整个农业部门合计占9%，而其中整个畜牧业的贡献还不到这一数字的一半，仅占美国温室气体排放总量的3.9%。这与所谓“畜牧业的排放量与交通运输业相当甚至更多”的说法相去甚远。

E

Why is there such a misconception?

为什么会出现这样的误解？

In 2006 the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) published a study titled "Livestock's Long Shadow," which received widespread international attention.

2006年，联合国粮食及农业组织（粮农组织，FAO）发布了一份题为《牲畜的巨大阴影》的研究报告，在国际上引起了广泛关注。

It stated that livestock produced a staggering 18 percent of the world's greenhouse gas emissions.

报告称，畜牧业产生了高达全球18%的温室气体排放。

The agency drew a startling conclusion that livestock was doing more to harm the climate than all modes of transportation combined.

该机构得出了一个惊人的结论：畜牧业对气候的破坏超过了所有交通运输方式的总和。

This latter claim was wrong, and has since been corrected by Henning Stenfeld, the report's senior author.

后一种说法是错误的，报告的第一作者亨宁·斯滕菲尔德后来已予以更正。

F

The problem was that analysts from the FAO used a comprehensive life-cycle assessment to study the climate impact of livestock, but a different method when they analyzed transportation.

问题在于，粮农组织的分析人员在研究畜牧业的气候影响时采用了全面的生命周期评估方法，而在分析交通运输时却使用了另一套方法。

For livestock, they considered every factor associated with producing meat.

对于畜牧业，他们考虑了与肉类生产相关的每一个环节。

This included emissions from fertilizer production, converting land from forests to pastures, growing feed, and direct emissions from animals (manure as well as expelling of gas from the stomach) from birth to death.

这包括化肥生产、将森林改为牧场、种植饲料所产生的排放，以及牲畜从出生到死亡的直接排放（包括粪便和胃部排气）。

G

However, when they looked at transportation's carbon footprint, they ignored impacts on the climate from manufacturing vehicle materials and parts, assembling vehicles and maintaining roads, bridges and airports.

然而，在计算交通运输的碳足迹时，他们却忽略了制造车辆材料与零部件、组装车辆以及维护道路、桥梁和机场所产生的气候影响。

Instead, they only considered the exhaust smoke emitted by finished cars, trucks, trains and planes.

相反，他们只考虑了成品轿车、卡车、火车和飞机所排放的尾气。

As a result, the FAO's comparison of greenhouse gas emissions from livestock to those from transportation was greatly distorted.

结果，粮农组织对畜牧业温室气体排放量与交通运输温室气体排放量的比较被严重扭曲。

H

I pointed out this flaw during a speech to fellow scientists in San Francisco on March 22, 2010, which led to a flood of media coverage.

2010年3月22日，我在旧金山向同行科学家发表演讲时指出了这一缺陷，此事随后引发了大量媒体报道。

To its credit, the FAO immediately owned up to its error.

值得肯定的是，粮农组织立即承认了自己的错误。

Unfortunately, the agency's initial claim that livestock was responsible for the lion's share of world greenhouse gas emissions had already received wide coverage.

遗憾的是，该机构最初声称畜牧业是全球温室气体排放的主要来源，这一说法已经广为流传。

To this day, we struggle to "unring" the bell.

时至今日，我们仍在努力消除这一错误说法的不良影响。

In its most recent assessment report, the FAO estimated that livestock produces 14.5 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions from human activities.

在其最新评估报告中，粮农组织估计，畜牧业产生的温室气体占全球人类活动温室气体排放量的14.5%。

There is no comparable full life-cycle assessment for transportation.

交通运输方面则没有类似的全生命周期评估。

However, as Stenfeld has pointed out, direct emissions from transportation versus livestock can be compared and amount to 14 versus 5 percent, respectively.

然而，正如斯坦菲尔德所指出的，交通运输与畜牧业的直接排放量是可以比较的，分别占14%和5%。

I

Many people continue to think that avoiding meat as infrequently as once a week will make a significant difference to the climate.

许多人仍然认为，哪怕只是每周少吃一次肉，也会对气候产生显著影响。

But according to one recent study, even if Americans eliminated all animal protein from their diets, they would reduce U.S. greenhouse gas emissions by only 2.6 percent.

但根据最近的一项研究，即使美国人完全从饮食中剔除所有动物蛋白，也只能使美国的温室气体排放量减少2.6%。

According to our research at the University of California, Davis, if the practice of Meatless Monday were to be adopted by all Americans, we'd see a reduction of only 0.5 percent.

根据我们在加州大学戴维斯分校的研究，如果全美推广"无肉星期一"的做法，排放量也只会减少0.5%。

J

Moreover, technological, genetic and management changes that have taken place in U.S. agriculture over the past 70 years have made livestock production more efficient and less greenhouse gas-intensive.

此外，过去70年间美国农业在技术、基因和管理方面发生的变化，使畜牧业生产效率更高，温室气体排放强度更低。

According to the FAO's statistical database, total direct greenhouse gas emissions from U.S. livestock have declined by 11.3 percent since 1961, while production of livestock meat has more than doubled.

据联合国粮农组织统计数据库显示，自1961年以来，美国畜牧业的温室气体直接排放总量下降了11.3%，而畜禽肉产量却增长了一倍以上。

K

Demand for meat is rising in developing and emerging economies, especially in the Middle East, North Africa and Southeast Asia.

发展中和新兴经济体对肉类的需求正在上升，尤其是在中东、北非和东南亚地区。

For example, raising livestock such as goats in Kenya is an important source of food and income for many small-scale farmers and herders.

例如，在肯尼亚饲养山羊等牲畜是许多小规模农户和牧民的重要食物和收入来源。

But meat consumption per person in these regions still lags that of developed countries.

但这些地区的人均肉类消费量仍落后于发达国家。

In 2015, average annual meat consumption per person in developed countries was 92 kilograms, compared to 24 kilograms in the Middle East and North Africa and 18 kilograms in Southeast Asia.

2015年，发达国家人均年肉类消费量为92公斤，而中东和北非为24公斤，东南亚为18公斤。

Still, given projected population growth in the developing world, there will certainly be an opportunity for countries such as the United States to bring their sustainable livestock rearing practices to the table.

尽管如此，鉴于发展中世界预计的人口增长，美国等国家无疑有机会将其可持续畜牧养殖实践推向国际舞台。

L

Removing animals from U.S. agriculture would lower national greenhouse gas emissions to a small degree, but it would also make it harder to meet people's nutritional requirements.

将牲畜从美国农业中移除，会在一定程度上降低全国温室气体排放，但也会使满足人们的营养需求变得更加困难。

Many critics of animal agriculture are quick to point out that if farmers raised only plants, they could produce more pounds of food and more calories per person.

许多反对畜牧业的批评者很快指出，如果农民只种植作物，就能产出更多磅数的食物和更多的人均热量。

But humans also need many essential micro- and macro-nutrients for good health.

但人类要保持健康，还需要许多必需的微量和宏量营养素。

It's hard to make a compelling argument that the United States has a calorie deficit, given its high national rates of adult and child obesity.

鉴于美国成人和儿童肥胖率居高不下，很难令人信服地论证美国存在热量不足的问题。

Moreover, not all plant parts are edible or desirable.

此外，并非所有植物部位都可食用或值得食用。

Raising livestock is a way to add nutritional and economic value to plant agriculture.

饲养牲畜是为种植业增添营养价值和经济价值的一种方式。

M

As one example, the energy in plants that livestock consume is most often contained in cellulose, which is indigestible for humans and many other mammals.

举例来说，牲畜所食用的植物中的能量，大多储存在纤维素中，而纤维素是人类和许多其他哺乳动物无法消化的。

But cows, sheep and other ruminant animals can break cellulose down and release the solar energy contained in this vast resource.

然而，牛、羊及其他反刍动物却能分解纤维素，释放出这一庞大资源中所蕴含的太阳能。

According to the FAO, as much as 70 percent of all agricultural land globally is range land that can only be utilized as grazing land for ruminant livestock.

据联合国粮农组织（FAO）统计，全球高达70%的农业用地属于草原，只能用作反刍牲畜的放牧地。

N

The world population is currently projected to reach 9.8 billion by 2050.

据预测，到2050年世界人口将达到98亿。

Feeding this many people will raise immense challenges.

养活如此众多的人口将带来巨大挑战。

Meat is more calorie-dense per serving than vegetarian options, and ruminant animals largely thrive on feed that is not suitable for humans.

每份肉类的热量密度高于素食选择，而且反刍动物主要靠不适宜人类食用的饲料茁壮成长。

Raising livestock also offers much-needed income for small-scale farmers in developing nations.

饲养牲畜还能为发展中国家的小农户提供急需的收入。

Worldwide, livestock provides a livelihood for 1 billion people.

在全球范围内，牲畜养殖为10亿人提供了生计。

O

Climate change demands urgent attention, and the livestock industry has a large overall environmental footprint that affects air, water and land.

气候变化亟待关注，而畜牧业的整体环境足迹巨大，影响着空气、水和土地。

These, combined with a rapidly rising world population, give us plenty of compelling reasons to continue to work for greater efficiencies in animal agriculture.

这些问题，再加上世界人口的迅速增长，为我们继续努力提高畜牧业效率提供了诸多令人信服的理由。

I believe the place to start is with science-based facts.

我认为，起点应当是以科学为依据的事实。

P1

Why are we so worried about our careers?

我们为何如此担忧自己的事业？

Partly it's to do with money, but there's a psychological aspect to our fears as well.

部分原因与金钱有关，但我们的恐惧也有心理层面的因素。

P2

We worry because we suspect—not wrongly—that the world is full of a frightening sort of person ready to judge us ruthlessly and swiftly: a person we can call a snob.

我们之所以忧心，是因为我们怀疑——而且并非怀疑错了——世上满是这样一种可怕的人：他们随时准备毫不留情、迅速果断地评判我们；这种人可以称作势利者。

P3

A snob is anyone who takes a relatively small part of us and uses it to come to a rigid conclusion about how much of their attention we deserve.

势利者就是那种抓住我们身上相对微小的一部分，便据此对我们应得他们多少关注下定论的人。

In the past, that might be your ancestry and royal connections.

在过去，这一点或许是你的出身和王室关系。

Nowadays, the snob cares about one thing only: what you do for a living.

而如今，势利者只在乎一件事：你是靠什么谋生的。

P4

This explains why the first question we will be asked in any new social context is 'What do you do?

这就解释了，为何在任何新的社交场合，人们问我们的第一个问题总是"你是做什么的？"

' and according to how we answer, snobs will either welcome us with broad smiles, or leave us in the cold.

而根据我们的回答，势利者要么满面笑容地欢迎我们，要么对我们冷眼相待。

P5

And that is why we are fired up by such a desperate urge to achieve and impress.

正因如此，我们才会被一种不顾一切想要成功、想要给人留下深刻印象的强烈冲动所驱使。

P6

Sometimes our behaviour is mistaken for greed and vanity, but it is more than this.

有时我们的行为被误认为是贪婪和虚荣，但其含义不止于此。

A lot of our interest in fancy cars, jobs and houses has nothing to do with materialism.

我们对豪车、名职、豪宅的诸多向往，与物质主义毫无关系。

It has to do with a hunger for the respect and esteem that is only available in our societies through the acquisition of material goods.

它源于一种对尊重和敬意的渴求，而在我们的社会里，唯有获取物质财富才能换来这种尊重和敬意。

It isn't the goods themselves we seek, it is the love we stand to gain through our possession of them.

我们所追求的并非商品本身，而是通过拥有它们所能获得的爱。

The next time we see someone driving a Ferrari, we shouldn't condemn them for their greed, we should pity them for the intensity of their need for love from the world.

下次当我们看到有人开着法拉利时，不该指责他们的贪婪，而应怜悯他们对来自世界的爱有着如此强烈的渴求。

P7

At the root of snobbery is a lack of imagination and confidence about how to decide who in the world is valuable.

势利的根源，在于缺乏想象力和自信，不知道该如何判断世上什么人才是真正有价值的。

The snobs are brutally misguided and slavish in their beliefs about how the superior individuals can be identified.

势利者在如何辨识出类拔萃之人这一问题上，既被严重误导，又盲从迷信。

For snobs, it is the already acclaimed and already successful who are the only ones worthy of respect.

在势利者眼中，唯有那些早已声名显赫、早已功成名就之人才配得上尊重。

There is no room in their timid regimented minds to imagine that someone might be clever, kind or good—and yet somehow have been overlooked entirely by society, their qualities lying hidden beneath an unfamiliar veil, and having as yet discovered no obvious outlet.

他们怯懦而刻板的头脑里，容不下这样的想象：一个人或许聪明、善良、品行端正——却不知何故被社会完全忽视，其品质隐没在一层陌生的面纱之下，至今尚未找到显而易见的施展之处。

P8

The true answer to snobbery is not to say that there is no such thing as a better or worse person, but to insist that better or worse exist in constantly unexpected places and carry none of the outward signs of distinction.

应对势利的真正之道，并非宣称人无所谓优劣之分，而是坚持认为：优劣存在于种种出人意料的地方，且不带任何外在的显赫标志。

And because we are such poor judges of the worth of others, our ultimate duty remains to be kind, good, curious and imaginative about pretty much everyone who ever crosses our path.

正因为我们如此不善于评判他人的价值，我们的终极责任便是对几乎每一个与我们不期而遇的人保持善意、良善、好奇与想象力。

P1

Women have historically been paid less.

在历史上，女性的薪酬一直低于男性。

But in the US in the 1980s, they began to catch up—fast.

但在20世纪80年代的美国，女性的收入开始迅速追赶上来。

During that decade, the gender pay gap closed by about one percentage point a year.

在那十年间，性别薪酬差距每年缩小约一个百分点。

Had that trend continued, the gender wage gap would have been closed by 2017.

如果这一趋势延续下去，到2017年性别薪酬差距本可基本消除。

P2

But the trend didn't continue, and the gap remains yawning.

然而这一趋势并未延续，薪酬差距依然悬殊。

P3

According to a new study from academics at Harvard, the stagnation can be put down, perhaps counterintuitively, to the introduction of state and federal family leave policies.

哈佛大学学者的一项新研究认为，这种停滞或许有悖直觉，却可归因于各州及联邦家庭休假政策的出台。

P4

The academics argue that during the 1990s, as governments began to introduce leave policies, it was mainly women who took advantage of them.

这些学者指出，20世纪90年代各国政府开始推行休假政策时，主要利用这些政策的是女性。

Though the leave policies might have helped those women to stay in the workplace—instead of dropping out to have families—those who returned saw their wages had increased at lower rates than the men.

尽管休假政策可能帮助这些女性留在职场——而不是离职回家生儿育女——但重返岗位后，她们的工资涨幅却低于男性。

P5

After family leave was introduced in the US, in fact, the rate of gender wage convergence fell to just 0.03 percentage points per year, and has remained there ever since.

事实上，美国引入家庭休假制度后，性别薪酬趋同的速度降至每年仅0.03个百分点，并从此一直维持在这一水平。

P6

Those monitoring the process towards salary equity at work have long watched as progress slowed in many countries around the world.

那些关注职场薪酬平等进程的人早就注意到，全球许多国家的进展已趋于放缓。

In fact, that progress began to reverse during the pandemic.

事实上，这一进展在疫情期间开始逆转。

P7

The gender pay gap is one of the most outstanding examples of that lack of parity, and still exists just about everywhere.

性别薪酬差距是这种不平等最突出的表现之一，几乎在所有地方都依然存在。

The motherhood penalty has become a shorthand for describing why: In many places, especially rich countries, women earn the same as men until they reach their childbearing years.

母职惩罚已成为描述其原因的简明说法：在许多地方，尤其是富裕国家，女性在到达生育年龄之前，收入与男性持平。

Women who have children begin to see their salaries slip behind their male counterparts.

生育了孩子的女性开始发现，自己的薪水逐渐落后于男性同行。

P8

Part of this is because women take on more of the unpaid labor at home, which can eat into time available for work and energy for career advancement.

部分原因在于女性承担了更多家庭中的无酬劳动，这会挤占可用于工作的时间和职业发展所需的精力。

But it's also because mothers are passed over for raises and promotion, and because time out of the workplace sets women back, even if that time is taken voluntarily, and supported by company or government policy.

但另一部分原因是，母亲们在加薪和晋升时被跳过，而且脱离职场的时间会使女性落后，哪怕这段时间是自愿休假，并且得到了公司或政府政策的支持。

P9

What would have happened if leave policies hadn't been introduced?

如果当时没有出台休假政策，情况会怎样？

The study doesn't go into that question, other than to say that if the 1980s trend continued, we would have been at parity by now.

该研究并未深入探讨这一问题，只是指出，如果20世纪80年代的趋势得以延续，我们现在已经实现了薪酬平等。

P10

It's possible, however, that the journey towards wage parity would have stalled either way.

然而，无论采取哪种方式，通往薪酬平等的道路都可能陷入停滞。

If women's gains in the 1980s were made through the erasure of things like bias, once those less uncontrollable problems had been addressed, there would still have been an issue with women—who are the ones to bear children and take care of them in the early weeks because of biological factors like the ability to breastfeed, forcing them to take breaks, whether or not those breaks were mandated.

如果女性在20世纪80年代的收入增长是通过消除偏见等问题实现的，那么一旦那些较不可控的问题得到解决，依然会面临一个核心难题——女性由于母乳喂养等生理因素，是生育孩子并在最初几周照料孩子的人，这迫使她们必须中断工作，无论这种中断是否由制度规定。
