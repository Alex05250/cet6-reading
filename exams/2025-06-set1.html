In her second year, Charlene Duong learned of the use of poisonous, synthetic pesticides on her college campus.

大学二年级时，陈良（Charlene Duong）得知学校校园内使用了有毒的合成农药。

Shocked but not surprised, she knew she had to do something.

震惊之余，她并不意外，她知道自己必须做点什么。

Along with a couple of classmates, Charlene did a quick web search and discovered a small but growing movement led by the organization, Herbicide-Free Campus (HFC), to rid college campuses of artificial herbicides.

陈良和几个同学一起迅速在网上搜索，发现了一个规模不大但正在壮大的运动，由"无除草剂校园"（Herbicide-Free Campus，简称HFC）这一组织发起，旨在让大学校园彻底摆脱人工合成除草剂。

They were intrigued.

他们很感兴趣。

Like many, Charlene experiences climate anxiety—a chronic fear of a climate catastrophe—and was, at the time, looking for an outlet.

和许多人一样，陈良也饱受气候焦虑之苦——即对气候灾难的长期恐惧——当时她正想找一个宣泄的出口。

When she discovered the HFC movement, she said she felt she "had found a specific area to focus on that still fit into the larger picture of fighting for a healthier, safer, cleaner environment for all."

她说，当她发现HFC运动时，感觉自己"找到了一个具体的着力点，同时又能融入为所有人争取更健康、更安全、更清洁环境这一更大的事业之中"。

Toxic herbicide use in university land care is not unique.

在大学校园绿地养护中使用有毒除草剂并非个例。

Most institutions of higher education rely on synthetic pesticides and fertilizers to achieve aesthetic goals.

大多数高等院校都依赖合成农药和化肥来达到美化校园的目的。

Having a "beautiful" campus means green and perfectly maintained lawns along with flower beds and paved sidewalks.

拥有一座"美丽"的校园，意味着草坪绿意盎然、修剪得整整齐齐，配以花坛和铺设平整的人行道。

But these conventionally managed campuses can come at a cost: increased cancer risk, contaminated waterways, poisoned wildlife and lifeless soil.

但这些采用常规方式管理的校园是要付出代价的：患癌风险上升、水道污染、野生动物中毒以及土壤丧失生机。

Pesticide use on college campuses also contributes to our global climate crisis.

大学校园使用农药也加剧了全球气候危机。

The use of chemicals to get rid of insects or unwanted plant life can increase indirect emissions as they can include petroleum-based ingredients.

使用化学药剂消灭害虫或清除不需要的植物，会增加间接排放，因为这些药剂可能含有石油基成分。

Pesticide use also decreases the life in soil, hampering the ability of soils to absorb carbon or retain water and thus reducing campuses' ability to recover quickly from climate-related extreme weather events like droughts and floods.

农药的使用还会减少土壤中的生物，削弱土壤吸收碳和保持水分的能力，从而降低校园快速应对干旱、洪涝等气候相关极端天气事件的能力。

Instead of using toxic chemicals, students working with HFC help out with weeding the campus grounds.

HFC 的学生志愿者不用有毒化学品，而是帮助清理校园里的杂草。

"This work reminds me to be in the present moment as I play my role in reducing herbicide use and keeping my campus safe and healthy," says Charlene.

查琳说："这项工作提醒我要专注于当下，在减少除草剂使用、维护校园安全与健康的过程中尽自己的一份力。"

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1217 词 · 49 句

A

Librarians know the value of their community services, and their patrons appreciate their importance as well.

图书馆员深知社区服务的价值，而读者也充分认可其重要性。

But in an increasingly digital world, we see the role of libraries as community and cultural centers at times undervalued.

但在一个日益数字化的世界里，我们发现图书馆作为社区和文化中心的作用有时被低估了。

When shrinking municipal budgets combine with the nonstop technological revolution, public library services that focus on building communities face-to-face, inspiring and educating patrons about art, literature, and music, and helping patrons engage in civil discourse can seem old-fashioned.

当不断缩减的市政预算与无休止的技术革命交织在一起时，那些致力于面对面建设社区、以艺术、文学和音乐启迪和教育读者、并帮助读者参与公民对话的公共图书馆服务，可能会显得有些过时。

But it is precisely those shrinking budgets and the assault of technologically mediated life that make public libraries' cultural and community offerings more important than ever.

然而，恰恰是这些不断缩减的预算和科技化生活的冲击，使得公共图书馆的文化与社区服务比以往任何时候都更加重要。

B

Many people point out the value public libraries bring to their communities.

许多人指出了公共图书馆为其社区带来的价值。

More than just books and banks of computers, libraries are still places where individuals gather to explore, interact, and imagine.

图书馆远不止是书籍和成排电脑的集合，它们仍然是人们汇聚一堂、探索、交流和激发想象力的场所。

Some of the specific ways in which libraries add value to our communities and serve as cultural centers for our patrons are community builders, centers for the arts, and champions of youth.

图书馆为我们社区增值、为读者充当文化中心的一些具体方式包括：建设社区、充当艺术中心、以及支持青少年成长。

Libraries serve in these capacities and are more than just about information.

图书馆在这些方面发挥着作用，其意义远不止于信息的传递。

C

As community builders libraries are engaged in incredible work.

作为社区建设者，图书馆正在从事令人赞叹的工作。

From tiny public libraries to huge city institutions with multiple branches, libraries across the United States are building community and supporting local culture in exciting ways.

从规模很小的公共图书馆到拥有多个分馆的大型城市图书馆，全美各地的图书馆正以令人振奋的方式建设社区、支持本地文化。

These are inspiring and hopefully will encourage librarians interested in community services and cultural outreach to make connections with each other, share ideas, and build partnerships.

这些事例令人鼓舞，有望激励那些对社区服务和文化推广感兴趣的图书馆员相互联系、分享理念、建立合作关系。

Supporters of libraries as community builders claim that unless you are out there changing neighborhoods, you are not completing the work you are to do.

将图书馆视为社区建设者的支持者们声称：除非你切实走出馆外、改变社区面貌，否则你就没有完成自己应尽的工作。

Strengthening neighborhoods and championing the cultural lives of communities are big responsibilities.

加强社区建设、推动社区文化生活，是重大的责任。

D

Place-based economic development stresses the importance of offering attractive, functional, and community-based places, such as libraries, in town squares and depressed neighborhoods.

以地域为基础的经济发展强调在城镇广场和衰落社区中提供有吸引力、功能完善且扎根社区的场所，如图书馆。

Like a major department store in a mall, libraries attract large numbers of people, creating economic opportunities for numerous businesses and organizations in the surrounding area.

图书馆就像购物中心里的大型百货商场，能够吸引大量人流，为周边地区的众多企业和组织创造经济机遇。

Large cities, medium-sized ones, and even small towns have successfully transformed their libraries into the hubs of vibrant neighborhoods.

大城市、中等城市乃至小城镇，都成功地将图书馆转变为充满活力的社区中心。

E

As key municipal agencies, and focal points for community education, libraries are major players in creating livable, environmentally friendly cities and towns.

图书馆作为重要的市政机构和社区教育的核心，是打造宜居、环保型城乡的重要力量。

The Urban Libraries Council released a report detailing the unique ways in which libraries can further sustainability at the local level.

城市图书馆协会发布了一份报告，详细阐述了图书馆在地方层面推进可持续发展的独特方式。

Beyond ensuring that library construction projects consider environmental impact, libraries can take a lead in supporting local foods and craftsmen, like the Peabody Institute Library's partnering with local businesses to pioneer a farmers' market in their courtyard, or the Richmond Public Library's seed lending library which "nurtures locally-adapted plant varieties, and fosters community resilience, self-reliance and a culture of sharing."

除了确保图书馆建设项目考虑环境影响外，图书馆还可以带头支持本地食品和手工艺人，例如皮博迪学院图书馆与当地企业合作在庭院里率先开设农贸市场，或者里士满公共图书馆的种子借阅项目，该项目"培育适应当地环境的植物品种，增强社区的韧性、自力更生能力和分享文化"。

F

Archives preserve historic artifacts, oral histories, digital history projects, and scholarly writings relevant to the community, including minority groups.

档案馆保存着与社区（包括少数族裔群体）相关的历史文物、口述史、数字历史项目和学术著作。

Communities lucky enough to have archivists have a great advantage when it comes to organizing historical records and artifacts.

拥有档案管理员的社区在整理历史记录和文物方面拥有很大的优势。

An organized archive is a place where people can research their ancestry and immigration history, do environmental research, and more.

管理有序的档案馆是人们研究家族血统与移民历史、开展环境研究等的场所。

An archivist is an advocate for preservation who, among other things, coordinates the restoration of maps and paintings, the digitization of vital records, and the creation of oral history projects.

档案管理员是文化遗产的守护者，其职责之一是协调地图和绘画的修复、重要记录的数字化以及口述史项目的创建等工作。

With projects like the Massachussets Memories Road Show and the Veterans History Project, evidence of the importance of archives is everywhere.

随着"马萨诸塞记忆巡回展"和"退伍军人历史项目"等项目的开展，档案重要性的证据随处可见。

G

In the words of Robert Putnam, "People may go to the library looking mainly for information, but they find each other there."

用罗伯特·帕特南的话来说：“人们去图书馆也许主要为查找资料，却在那里遇见了彼此。”

New moms connect at baby story-times; elderly people, often facing difficult life transitions, attend events and find that they make new friends; teenagers meet up in libraries' teen spaces after school; and readers discuss current events in the periodicals room.

新手妈妈们在婴儿故事时间里结识；老年人常面临艰难的人生过渡期，他们参加活动并结交了新朋友；青少年放学后聚集在图书馆的青少年专区；读者们则在期刊阅览室里讨论时事。

In libraries, community-building connections are happening all the time.

在图书馆里，社区纽带的建立无时无刻不在发生。

H

As Keith Richards said, "The public library is the great equalizer."

正如基思·理查兹所言：“公共图书馆是最伟大的公平器。”

Despite the rising costs of concert and theater tickets, public library events (including concerts, author visits, and gallery displays) are often offered free of charge, enabling people of any income level to attend.

尽管音乐会和戏剧门票价格不断上涨，公共图书馆的活动（包括音乐会、作家见面会和画廊展览）往往免费开放，使各个收入水平的人都能参加。

In addition, library book groups allow people to explore and discuss the literary arts, and the Great Stories Club introduces at-risk youth to literature.

此外，图书馆的读书小组让人们得以探讨和交流文学艺术，而“伟大故事俱乐部”则引导处于风险中的青少年走近文学。

The best part: it's all free and open to the public.

最棒的是：这一切都是免费的，并且向公众开放。

I

In a time when education is increasingly expensive, public libraries provide information and educational opportunities free for all people, regardless of their socio-economic status.

在这个教育日益昂贵的时代，公共图书馆不分社会经济地位，向所有人免费提供信息和教育机会。

Offered by libraries across the country, American Library Association's Let's Talk about It programs are wonderful examples of scholar-facilitated learning opportunities in libraries.

美国图书馆协会的“让我们谈谈”项目由全国各地的图书馆承办，是图书馆中学者引导型学习机会的优秀范例。

In addition, many libraries present classes and discussion programs, and some even provide online continuing education courses such as the Universal Class database.

此外，许多图书馆开设课程和讨论项目，有些图书馆甚至提供在线继续教育课程，例如“通用课堂”数据库。

J

Librarians know that patrons aren't just information consumers, they're information producers.

图书馆员知道，读者不仅仅是信息的消费者，更是信息的创造者。

Patrons use the library to gain knowledge in order to create their own new and independent works.

读者利用图书馆获取知识，以便创作出属于自己的全新独立作品。

Increasing numbers of libraries provide spaces and services that meet the needs of people who want to learn how to edit Wikipedia, set up blogs or podcasts, create their own magazines, and so much more.

越来越多的图书馆提供符合人们需求的空间和服务，帮助他们学习如何编辑维基百科、开设博客或播客、创办自己的杂志，乃至更多。

Many libraries offer art or writing workshops and groups, and some provide music practice rooms for patrons.

许多图书馆开设艺术或写作工作坊和兴趣小组，有些还为读者提供音乐练习室。

Programs like ImaginOn in Charlotte, North Carolina, provide exciting models that take community partnership, creativity, and creation to a new level.

北卡罗来纳州夏洛特市的ImaginOn（想象空间）等项目提供了令人振奋的范例，将社区合作、创造力和创作提升到了新的高度。

K

The decline of civil discourse stems in part from the fact that it is so easy for people to watch news about, buy products from, and engage—in both the virtual and real worlds—only with those of similar backgrounds and ideologies.

文明交流的式微，部分源于这样一个事实：无论是在虚拟世界还是现实世界中，人们都很容易只接触与自己背景和意识形态相似的人——只看这些人播报的新闻、只购买这些人的产品、只与这些人交往。

Public libraries, through such programs as The Human Library and Socrates Café, can help build small communities of difference that encourage people to interact with and learn from each other through dialogue.

公共图书馆通过"真人图书馆"（The Human Library）和"苏格拉底咖啡馆"（Socrates Café）等项目，可以帮助构建由差异人群组成的小型社群，鼓励人们通过对话相互交流、彼此学习。

By both actively promoting civil discourse through these programs, and modeling and upholding the principles of free inquiry and expression for all, libraries help individuals rediscover the importance of and increased need for civil discourse in American life.

图书馆一方面通过这些项目积极推动文明交流，另一方面以身作则、维护面向所有人的自由探究与表达原则，从而帮助个人重新认识到文明交流在美国生活中的重要性及其日益增长的必要性。

L

Free tutoring, homework help programs, and summer reading programs for kids and teens help bridge the economic divide that impacts students' academic performance.

面向儿童和青少年的免费辅导、作业辅导和暑期阅读项目，有助于弥合影响学生学业表现的经济鸿沟。

The cost of hiring a private tutor is well beyond what many library patrons can afford, so libraries offer homework help and tutoring online, by phone, in person, and even through social media and homework apps.

聘请私人教师的费用远远超出许多图书馆读者的承受能力，因此图书馆通过线上、电话、面对面，甚至社交媒体和作业辅导应用等方式提供作业辅导和教学服务。

Annual summer reading programs also have a positive impact on student performance and, according to a 2010 study conducted by Dominican University's Graduate School of Library and Information Science, students' reading skills get a boost from these popular nationwide events.

一年一度的暑期阅读项目同样对学生的学业表现有积极影响。根据多米尼加大学图书馆与信息科学研究生院2010年开展的一项研究，这些广受欢迎的全国性活动提升了学生的阅读技能。

M

Through library collections, programs, and physical spaces, children learn to share, to be engaged in their communities, to participate in the arts, and to explore their immediate world and the world at large.

通过馆藏资源、各类项目和实体空间，孩子们学会了分享、融入社区、参与艺术活动，并探索身边的世界和更广阔的天地。

There are surely endless examples of innovative library services for children, including the Middle Country Public Library's Nature Explorium, which engages children in learning about the natural world.

面向儿童的创新图书馆服务无疑不胜枚举，比如中乡村公共图书馆（Middle Country Public Library）的"自然探索园"（Nature Explorium），引导孩子们学习有关自然世界的知识。

N

These examples are just a few of the many amazing things that public libraries around the United States (and the world) are doing to build and maintain strong community connections.

这些例子只是美国（乃至全世界）各地公共图书馆为建立和维系牢固的社区纽带所做的诸多出色工作中的一小部分。

We encourage you to try some of these ideas in your own libraries, and we hope that these ideas will help you be better able to convince your community leaders of the important role that public libraries play in communities large and small.

我们鼓励各位在自己的图书馆中尝试其中一些理念，也希望这些理念能帮助您更有说服力地让社区领导者认识到公共图书馆在大大小小的社区中所发挥的重要作用。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1443 词 · 25 句

P1

Nationally, one in six children miss 15 or more days of school in a year.

从全国范围看，每六个孩子中就有一个一年缺课15天或更多。

Education officials have deplored all this missed instruction.

教育官员对这些错失的教学时间深感痛惜。

P2

These chronically absent students suffer academically because of all the classroom instruction they miss out on.

这些长期缺课的学生因错失了大量课堂教学而在学业上遭受损失。

In 2015, the U.S. secretary of education responded to this crisis, urging communities to support every student to attend every day and be successful in school.

2015年，美国教育部长对这一危机作出回应，敦促各社区支持每一名学生每天到校并顺利完成学业。

His open letter stated that missing 10% of school days in a year for any reason—excused or unexcused—is a primary cause of low academic achievement.

他在公开信中指出，无论出于何种原因——无论是请假还是未请假——一年内缺课天数达到10%，是学业成绩低下的首要原因。

P3

Worrying about whether children attend school makes sense.

担心孩子是否到校上课，这种心情合乎情理。

After all, if students don't show up, teachers can't teach them.

毕竟，学生不到校，老师就无法教他们。

P4

But what if America's attendance crisis is about much more than students missing class?

但美国的出勤危机，会不会远不止学生缺课那么简单？

What if, instead, it is a reflection of family and community crises these students face—such as being ejected from the family apartment, fearing for their safety in their neighborhood or suffering an illness?

它是否反而是这些学生所面临的家庭与社区危机的写照——例如被赶出家门、在所住街区为自身安全担忧，或遭受疾病困扰？

P5

As social scientists we investigated how excused and unexcused absences relate to children's academic achievement.

作为社会科学研究者，我们考察了请假缺课与无故缺课同儿童学业成绩之间的关系。

P6

We find that absences excused by a parent do little to harm children's learning.

我们发现，经家长请假的缺课几乎不会损害儿童的学习。

In fact, children with no unexcused absences—but 15 to 18 excused absences—have test scores equal to their peers who have no absences.

事实上，那些没有无故缺课、但有15至18次请假缺课的儿童，其考试成绩与从不缺课的同龄人相当。

P7

Meanwhile, the average child with even just one unexcused absence does much worse academically than peers with none.

与此同时，平均而言，哪怕只旷课一次（无故缺勤）的孩子，其学业表现也远逊于从未缺勤的同龄人。

P8

We believe unexcused absence is a strong signal of the many challenges children and families face, including economic and medical hardships.

我们认为，无故缺勤是一个强烈信号，反映出儿童及其家庭所面临的种种困境，包括经济和医疗方面的艰难处境。

Unexcused absences can be a powerful signal of how those out-of-school challenges affect children's academic progress.

无故缺勤可以有力地揭示校外的这些困境如何影响儿童的学业进步。

P9

Our evidence suggests unexcused absences are problematic, but for a different reason than people often think.

我们的证据表明，无故缺勤确实是个问题，但其原因与人们通常所想的不同。

Absence from school, and especially unexcused absence, matters mainly as a signal of many crises children and their families may be facing.

缺课，尤其是无故缺勤，其意义主要在于它是一个信号，表明儿童及其家庭可能正面临多重危机。

It matters less as a cause of lower student achievement due to missed instruction.

它作为缺课导致教学内容缺失、进而拉低学业成绩的原因，其重要性要小得多。

P10

How we choose to think of school absences matters for educational policy.

我们如何看待学生缺勤，对教育政策的制定至关重要。

School attendance policies typically hold schools and families accountable for the days children miss, regardless of whether they were excused or unexcused absences.

学校的出勤政策通常要求学校和家庭对孩子缺勤的天数负责，而不论这些缺勤是有正当理由还是无故缺勤。

P11

These policies assume that missing school for any reason harms children academically because they are missing classroom instruction.

这些政策假定，无论出于何种原因缺课都会因错过课堂教学而损害儿童的学业。

They also assume that schools will be able to effectively intervene by reducing student absences.

它们还假定，学校能够通过减少学生缺勤来进行有效干预。

We find neither to be the case.

我们发现，这两种假设都不成立。

P12

As a result, these attendance policies end up disproportionately punishing families dealing with out-of-school crises in their lives and pressuring schools who serve them to get students to school more often.

结果是，这些出勤政策不成比例地惩罚了那些正应对生活中校外危机的家庭，同时给服务于这些家庭的学校施加压力，迫使它们让学生更频繁地到校。

P13

We instead suggest using unexcused absence from school as a signal to channel resources to the children and families who need them most.

我们则建议将无故缺课作为一种信号，借此把资源投向最需要帮助的儿童及其家庭。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2450 词 · 23 句

P1

After earning a bachelor's degree, I was determined to do what I love.

获得学士学位后，我决心从事自己热爱的事业。

I headed straight to graduate school to investigate the social problems that fascinated me.

我直接进入研究生院，研究那些令我着迷的社会问题。

P2

For almost a decade, I told everyone I encountered that they should do the same.

近十年来，我一直告诉遇到的每个人，他们也应该这样做。

"Follow your passion," I counseled.

我劝告别人：“追随你的热爱。”

"You can figure out the employment stuff later."

“至于就业方面的事，以后再想办法解决。”

P3

It wasn't until I began to research this widely accepted career advice that I understood how problematic it really was.

直到我开始研究这一广为接受的职业建议时，我才明白它究竟有多大问题。

P4

As a sociologist, I interviewed college students and professional workers to learn what it really meant to pursue their dreams, which I will refer to here as the passion principle.

作为一名社会学家，我采访了大学生和职场人士，以了解追求梦想对他们究竟意味着什么——我在本文中将其称为“热爱原则”。

I was stunned by what I found out about this principle in the research for my new book.

在我的新书研究过程中，我所了解到的关于这一原则的情况令我大为震惊。

P5

Surveys show the American public has long held the passion principle in high regard as a career decision-making priority.

调查显示，美国公众长期以来一直将“热爱原则”视为职业决策的首要考量。

And its popularity is even stronger among those facing job instability.

而在面临工作不稳定的人群中，这一原则更为流行。

P6

Advocates of the passion principle found it compelling because they believed that following one's passion can provide workers with both the motivation necessary to work hard and a place to find fulfillment.

“热爱原则”的支持者认为它很有说服力，因为他们相信，追随热爱既能为劳动者提供努力工作所需的动机，也能为他们找到成就感提供一个方向。

P7

Yet, what I found is that following one's passion does not necessarily lead to fulfillment, but is one of the most powerful cultural forces perpetuating overwork.

然而，我发现，追随热爱并不一定会带来成就感，反而是在让过度工作长期延续的最强大的文化力量之一。

I also found that promoting the pursuit of one's passion helps perpetuate social inequalities due to the fact that not everyone has the same economic resources to allow them to pursue their passion with ease.

我还发现，倡导追随个人激情的做法会加剧社会不平等，因为并非人人都拥有同样的经济资源，能够从容地追求自己的激情所在。

P8

While the passion principle is broadly popular, not everyone has the necessary resources to turn their passion into a stable, good-paying job.

尽管“激情原则”广受欢迎，但并非人人都有必要的资源，能把激情转化为一份稳定且收入可观的工作。

Passion-seekers from wealthy families are better able to wait until a job they are passionate about comes along without worrying about student loans in the meantime.

出身富裕家庭的追梦者更有条件等待心仪工作的出现，而无须在此期间为助学贷款发愁。

They are also better situated to take unpaid internships to get their foot in the door while their parents pay their rent.

他们也更有条件接受无薪实习以跻身行业，而房租则由父母承担。

And they often have access to parents' social networks to help them find jobs.

而且他们往往还能借助父母的社交网络来求职。

Surveys revealed that working-class and first-generation college graduates, regardless of their career field, are more likely than their wealthier peers to end up in low-paying unskilled jobs when they pursue their passion.

调查显示，工薪家庭出身和第一代大学毕业生，无论从事何种职业领域，在追随激情时，都比家境优越的同龄人更有可能最终落入低薪、无技能的工作岗位。

P9

Colleges, workplaces and career counselors who promote the "follow your passion" path for everyone, without leveling the playing field, help perpetuate socioeconomic inequalities among career aspirants.

高校、职场和职业顾问若在不营造公平竞争环境的情况下，向所有人鼓吹“追随你的激情”这条路，就会助长求职者之间社会经济不平等的延续。

P10

It's not just well-off passion-seekers who benefit from the passion principle.

从激情原则中获益的并不只是家境优渥的追梦者。

Employers of passionate workers do, too.

雇用富有激情员工的雇主同样从中受益。

Potential employers showed greater interest in passionate applicants in part because they believed the applicants would work hard at their jobs without expecting an increase in pay.

潜在雇主对满怀激情的求职者表现出更浓厚的兴趣，部分原因在于他们相信这些求职者会努力工作，而不会要求加薪。

They even sacrifice a good salary, job stability and leisure time to work in a job they love.

他们甚至愿意牺牲优厚的薪酬、稳定的工作和闲暇时间，去从事自己热爱的工作。
