Imagine you and a stranger are sitting on a couch.

想象一下，你和一个陌生人坐在一张沙发上。

How close would you sit to them?

你会坐得离对方多近？

It turns out that how much of a buffer zone you prefer may reveal a lot about your personality.

事实证明，你偏好的缓冲区大小可能会在很大程度上揭示你的性格。

In one study, researchers sought to ascertain how our personality may be linked to our preferred amount of personal space.

在一项研究中，研究人员试图探究我们的性格如何与我们偏好的个人空间大小相关联。

The researchers found that people who scored higher in the trait of agreeableness tended to sit closer to others, as did people who were more open and outgoing.

研究人员发现，宜人性这一特质得分较高的人往往坐得离别人更近，更开放、更外向的人也是如此。

Other individual differences, such as gender and age, can also affect how much personal space we crave.

其他个体差异，如性别和年龄，也会影响我们渴望多少个人空间。

Women tend to prefer more physical distance from strangers or acquaintances and older adults also generally tend to prefer to keep more distance between themselves and others.

女性往往偏好与陌生人或熟人保持更大的身体距离，而年长者通常也倾向于与他人保持更多距离。

Beyond reflecting our personality and demographics, the amount of personal space we prefer is also linked to our cultural backgrounds.

我们偏好的个人空间大小，除了反映我们的性格和人口统计特征外，还与我们的文化背景有关。

In a study involving participants from 42 countries, researchers found that around the world people's desired amount of personal space varied substantially.

在一项涵盖42个国家参与者的跨文化研究中，研究人员发现，世界各地人们所期望的个人空间大小存在显著差异。

For example, in Romania, the average amount of personal space people said they would prefer from a stranger was more than 4 feet, whereas in Argentina it was roughly half that.

例如，在罗马尼亚，人们表示与陌生人之间偏好的平均个人空间距离超过4英尺，而在阿根廷，这一距离大约只有前者的一半。

What might underlie this variation?

这种差异的背后可能是什么原因？

The researchers looked at a number of potential factors, but the only one that was significantly correlated with preferred interpersonal distance was average temperature.

研究人员考察了多种潜在因素，但唯一与偏好的人际距离显著相关的因素是平均气温。

The warmer the place, it seems, the "warmer" the people and the less space they needed between them and others.

地方越温暖，人们似乎也越“热情”，彼此之间的距离需求也越小。

Personal space may also reflect interpersonal attraction.

个人空间也可能反映出人际吸引。

Researchers have found that our chosen physical proximity also appears to reflect how much we like others.

研究者发现，我们所选择的身体上的亲疏程度，似乎也能反映出我们对他人的好恶。

The more people would like to be friends with the other person, the closer they sat to them.

人们越想与对方成为朋友，坐得离对方就越近。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1223 词 · 48 句

A

The cost of a mortgage or rents, meeting monthly utility bills, paying for a new car—all headaches most people get to worry about from their 20s.

房贷或房租、每月缴纳水电燃气费、买一辆新车——这些麻烦事大多从二十多岁起就成了人们的心头之忧。

But while some may struggle to pay their bills after a few too many nights out at the start of the month, others will have a tightly followed plan for their repayments.

不过，有些人月初连着潇洒了几个晚上后就可能捉襟见肘、难以付清账单，另一些人却会严格按照计划还款。

So what is the difference between the shrewd saver and the more reckless spender?

那么，精明的储蓄者和大手大脚的消费者之间究竟有何差别？

B

Experts believe it is all about the age you learn how to spend responsibly.

专家认为，关键在于你是在多大年龄学会理性消费的。

Adult money habits are typically set by the age of seven, according to a large-scale study by the government's Money Advice Service and behaviour experts at Cambridge University.

根据政府的货币咨询服务（Money Advice Service）与剑桥大学行为专家开展的一项大规模研究，成年后的金钱习惯通常在七岁前就已定型。

By then, most children have grasped how to recognise the value of money and are capable of complex functions such as planning ahead, delaying a decision until later and understanding that some choices are irreversible.

到那时，大多数孩子已经懂得认识金钱的价值，并能完成一些复杂的行为，比如提前规划、把决定推迟到以后再做，以及明白有些选择是不可逆的。

C

Tomorrow marks the start of Global Money Week, a worldwide initiative to help children make smart financial decisions and learn that "money matters matter".

明天是全球金钱周（Global Money Week）的开幕日，这是一项全球性活动，旨在帮助儿童做出明智的财务决策，并让他们认识到“理财很重要”。

But for many young people in the UK, it's a matter of too little, too late.

但对英国的许多年轻人来说，这事来得太少、太晚了。

"The habits of mind which influence the ways children approach complex problems and decisions, including financial ones, are largely determined in the first few years of life," says Dr David Whitebread, the co-author of the study.

该研究的合著者戴维·怀特布雷德博士说：“影响孩子处理复杂问题和决策——包括财务决策——方式的思维习惯，主要是在人生最初几年形成的。”

And this core behaviour is very likely to influence their financial decisions when they are adults, he says.

他说，这种核心行为极有可能影响他们成年后的财务决策。

D

The study also urges parents not to underestimate their own good and bad money habits.

研究还呼吁父母不要低估自身金钱习惯的好坏所带来的影响。

"Since young children have few monetary resources they control independently, it is the basic approaches and skills which are modelled, discussed and demonstrated by parents that are likely to be influential levers, breeding efficient habits and practices," it says.

报告写道：“由于年幼的孩子很少有自己能独立支配的金钱资源，父母所示范、讨论和展示的基本方法与技能，才是可能产生影响的关键杠杆，有助于培养高效的习惯与做法。”

E

Parents who choose to help their children learn how to plan ahead, reflect on the past and self-regulate can make a huge difference in promoting good financial behaviour in the future, explains Whitebread.

怀特布雷德指出，选择帮助孩子学会未雨绸缪、反思过去以及自我调节的父母，对孩子未来养成良好的理财行为有着巨大的促进作用。

According to a survey by M&G Investments, the vast majority (83%) of parents recognise the value of teaching their children about money.

据M&G Investments的一项调查显示，绝大多数（83%）的父母都认识到教孩子认识金钱的价值。

However, this concern does not always carry through to practice—one in six do not feel confident enough to do it.

然而，这种意识并不总能落实到行动上——六分之一的父母对此没有足够的信心去实践。

More than one in every four parents think children should take responsibility for understanding money themselves.

超过四分之一的父母认为，孩子应当自己负起了解金钱的责任。

F

"There's joy in the fact that young children are spontaneous and live life in the moment, unrestrained by adult problems. But teaching children about money when they are young is a good way for parents to help them learn to delay satisfaction, so that the child thinks: 'I'd like to do this but I'm not going to'. Hard evidence suggests, if you want your child to do well in life, the earlier you can start trying to teach them inhibition and self-discipline, the better,"

“小孩子天真随性、活在当下、不受成人烦恼的束缚，这本身就令人欣喜。但趁孩子年幼时教他们认识金钱，是帮助他们学会延迟满足的好办法，让孩子能够想到：‘我很想做这件事，但我还是忍住不做。’确凿的证据表明，如果你想让孩子在人生中有所成就，越早开始培养他们的克制力和自律能力，效果就越好。”

says Whitebread, pointing to research that has shown people in prison frequently have poor impulse control, a personality trait that can affect relationships and long-term health.

怀特布雷德说道，他援引的研究表明，监狱中的服刑人员往往自控力较差，而这种性格特征会影响人际关系和长期健康。

G

But how should a parent try to teach their child to spend responsibly?

但是，父母应该如何教孩子理性消费呢？

One simple way is to give them pocket money and then help them to analyse and learn from any foolish spending decisions.

一种简单的方法是给孩子零花钱，然后帮助他们分析愚蠢的消费决定并从中吸取教训。

H

Another is through board games which can help teach financial responsibility.

另一种方法是借助有助于培养理财责任感的桌面游戏。

Pop to the Shops, a shopping board game for five-to-nine-year-olds, has been one of Orchard Toys' top 10 bestselling board games every year for the past 15 years.

《Pop to the Shops》是一款面向五至九岁儿童的购物主题桌面游戏，过去15年来每年都是Orchard Toys十大畅销桌游之一。

To win, players must use fake coins to purchase everyday grocery items from various shops around the board, and give the other players who visit their shop the correct change.

为了获胜，玩家必须使用游戏币在棋盘上的各家商店购买日常杂货商品，并在其他玩家光顾其店铺时找零。

If they don't have enough money, they must purchase a cheaper item.

如果钱不够，他们就必须购买更便宜的商品。

"It acts as a conversation starter about money—parents can fill in the gaps," says Orchard Toys product manager Rachael Sutcliffe.

“它起到了开启金钱话题的作用——家长可以补充讲解，”Orchard Toys产品经理雷切尔·萨特克利夫说道。

I

There are also some pocket-money-tracking and payment apps, such as RoosterMoney and goHenry, which are designed to be used by under-sevens, and which boast that they can help children develop good money habits.

此外还有一些零花钱记录与支付类应用，例如RoosterMoney和goHenry，专为七岁以下儿童设计，并宣称能帮助孩子养成良好的金钱习惯。

This is dependent on the children receiving enough pocket money to need an app to track their spending, which research suggests is not always the case.

但这取决于孩子拿到的零花钱是否多到需要用应用来记录支出，而研究表明事实并非总是如此。

J

Surprisingly, given the research about habit-forming in young children, there are very few gaming apps that can help under-sevens—Pigby's Fair, developed by NatWest, being a notable exception.

令人意外的是，尽管已有大量关于幼儿习惯养成的研究，专门帮助七岁以下儿童的益智类游戏应用却少之又少——由NatWest开发的Pigby's Fair是一个显著的例外。

In this, children who solve basic puzzles will earn virtual money selling what they have made at different stalls in a fair, then spend or save it.

在游戏中，孩子们通过解开基础谜题赚取虚拟钱币，在集市的不同摊位上出售自己的作品，然后选择花掉或存起来。

But the need to shift virtual money around, and take into account interest payments and pocket money bonuses, makes it a complex financial education tool.

然而，虚拟钱币的流转操作、利息收益以及零花钱奖励等因素，使它成为一个相当复杂的金融教育工具。

K

There are plenty of online games which focus on coin recognition and counting with money, such as the Igloo Shopping game or Top Marks' Coins Game.

网上有许多侧重于钱币辨认和用钱计数的游戏，例如Igloo Shopping游戏和Top Marks的Coins Game。

So what if children find such games boring?

那么，如果孩子觉得这类游戏很无聊怎么办？

An exciting (and free) real-world option for parents to use is a visit to Metro Bank.

家长不妨带孩子去Metro Bank走一走，这是一个既令人兴奋又免费的现实选择。

Although, like most banks, it does not allow children to open an account until they turn 11, it specifically tries to offer young children a chance to learn about money inside its branches by inviting kids to use the "magic money machines" to count their coins.

虽然和大多数银行一样，它不允许11岁以下儿童开户，但它专门在网点内为年幼的孩子提供学习金钱知识的机会，邀请他们使用“魔法数币机”来清点硬币。

Children who accurately guess how much was in their piggy bank get a prize, and free candies are handed out when the coins are exchanged for banknotes.

准确猜出储蓄罐里有多少钱的孩子会得到奖品，硬币兑换纸币时还会免费分发糖果。

The bank does not take a commission from the transaction.

银行不从这类交易中收取手续费。

L

At six years old, my daughter Flora is getting to grips with her finances early.

我六岁的女儿弗洛拉很早就在学习如何管理自己的财务了。

She tested out a number of games aimed at teaching children her age the value of money.

她试玩了几款旨在教导她这个年龄段的孩子认识金钱价值的游戏。

She enjoyed them all, but best, in her view, is Money Match Cafe, a board game where she must work out the value of different denominations of coin in her imaginary restaurant, her "customers" being her stuffed toys.

这些游戏她都喜欢，但在她看来最好的一款是《金钱匹配咖啡馆》（Money Match Cafe），这是一款棋盘游戏，她必须在自己的模拟餐厅里算出不同面值硬币的价值，她的“顾客”就是她的毛绒玩具。

"It was really fun because you get to serve food to your own toys on a little tray with a napkin on it, and then you can hug them," she says.

她说：“真的很好玩，因为你可以用铺着餐巾的小托盘给自己的玩具上菜，然后还可以抱抱它们。”

M

"If you're six and you play this game, you'll learn that you might not have the right amount of money for what you want to buy. You just have to cross your fingers and hope. It's a good challenge, and I enjoyed adding the coins up to see how much they made. But I don't like the name. Money Match Cafe is not a good name for a cafe. I would have liked it more if it had been Flora's Cafe or Fantastically Baked Lunches."

“如果你六岁玩这个游戏，你会学到自己想要买的东西可能钱不够。你只能祈祷走运。这是个很好的挑战，我很喜欢把硬币加起来看看一共是多少。但我不喜欢这个名字。Money Match Cafe 不是个好咖啡馆的名字。如果叫‘弗洛拉咖啡馆’或者‘奇妙烘焙午餐’，我会更喜欢。”

N

Seven out of ten teachers think children are now faced with financial decisions earlier.

十分之七的教师认为，如今孩子们更早就要面临与财务相关的决定。

On Wednesday, Lifesavers—a new project which promotes financial education in primary schools—is hosting a conference to encourage teachers to join its programme and get free classroom resources and training.

周三，Lifesavers——一个在小学推广金融教育的新项目——将举办一场会议，鼓励教师加入其计划，免费获取课堂教学资源和培训。

Almost two-thirds of millennials wish they had received more money advice at a younger age, according to a Santander survey.

桑坦德银行（Santander）的一项调查显示，近三分之二的千禧一代希望在更年轻的时候能得到更多理财方面的建议。

O

Only a third of parents involve their children in discussions about their household finances, even though less than half of young people aged seven to seventeen receive financial education at school, another survey shows.

另一项调查显示，只有三分之一的父母会让孩子参与家庭财务的讨论，尽管不到一半的七至十七岁年轻人在学校接受过金融教育。

P

Most adults believe their parents have had the biggest influence on their money behaviour.

大多数成年人认为，父母对自己理财行为的影响最大。

So when should they start trying to instil good habits?

那么，他们应该从什么时候开始培养良好的理财习惯呢？

As early as possible before the age of seven, research suggests.

研究表明，越早越好，最迟不要晚于七岁。

Three artists have brought a lawsuit against Stability AI, DeviantArt, and Midjourney, alleging that the text-to-image AI tools have infringed the rights of thousands of artists.

三名艺术家已对Stability AI、DeviantArt和Midjourney提起诉讼，指控这些文生图AI工具侵犯了数千名艺术家的权利。

P2

The lawsuit claims that the Stable Diffusion tool used by Stability AI, DeviantArt, and Midjourney was trained on billions of copyrighted images downloaded from the internet and used by the companies "without compensation or consent from the artists."

诉讼称，Stability AI、DeviantArt和Midjourney所使用的Stable Diffusion工具，是用从互联网上下载的数十亿张受版权保护的图片训练而成，这些公司“未经艺术家补偿或同意”便使用了这些图片。

P3

The artists are represented by The Joseph Saveri Law Firm and Matthew Butterick.

这些艺术家由Joseph Saveri律师事务所和Matthew Butterick代理。

P4

"If Stable Diffusion and similar products are allowed to continue to operate as they do now, the foreseeable result is they will replace the very artists whose stolen works power these AI products with whom they are competing," the law firm wrote in a press release.

该律师事务所在新闻稿中写道：“如果Stable Diffusion及类似产品被允许继续照常运营，可以预见的结果是，它们将取代那些被盗作品为这些AI产品提供动力、并与之竞争的艺术家本身。”

"AI image products are not just an infringement of artists' rights; they will eliminate 'artist' as a viable career path. In addition to obtaining compensation for the wrongful conduct, this lawsuit seeks to prevent that outcome and ensure these products follow the same rules as any other new technology that involves the use of massive amounts of intellectual property. If streaming music can be accomplished within the law, so can AI products."

“AI图像产品不仅是对艺术家权利的侵犯，它们还将使‘艺术家’这一职业不再可行。除了为不当行为获得赔偿外，本诉讼还旨在防止这一结果，并确保这些产品遵守与任何其他涉及大量知识产权使用的新技术相同的规则。如果流媒体音乐能够在法律框架内实现，AI产品同样可以。”

P5

Since text-to-image generators have increased in popularity in the past year, many artists have been vocalizing their opposition to AI art after seeing their own distorted signatures appear in AI art and finding out that their work was used to train the tools without their permission.

过去一年里，文生图生成器日益流行，许多艺术家在看到自己的签名被扭曲地出现在AI艺术作品中、并得知自己的作品未经许可就被用于训练这些工具后，纷纷公开表达对AI艺术的反对。

P6

Not only are many artists furious at the possible copyright violations of AI art, but they have also been frustrated by how AI tools are able to sidestep the labor and art processes they are required to go through.

许多艺术家不仅对AI艺术可能侵犯版权感到愤怒，也对AI工具能够绕过他们必须经历的劳动和艺术创作过程感到沮丧。

As a result, many artists have been trying to ban AI art from art-sharing platforms.

因此，许多艺术家一直在努力推动在艺术分享平台上封禁AI艺术。

P7

As AI innovation continues at a rapid speed, it seems that artists and lawmakers are trying to put a brake on further developments before it's too late.

随着AI创新以迅猛的速度持续推进，艺术家和立法者似乎正试图在为时已晚之前踩下刹车。

P8

Many tech enthusiasts are still rallying around the promise of AI tools to create detailed images cheaply and quickly—anonymous tech supporters have already published a response to Butterick and Joseph Saveri's lawsuit on a website, using the "fair use" clause as the basis for why copyrighted images could be used by the tool.

许多科技爱好者仍然拥护AI工具的前景，即能够廉价、快速地生成精细图像——匿名的科技支持者已在一个网站上发布了对Butterick和Joseph Saveri诉讼的回应，以“合理使用”条款为依据，主张该工具可以使用受版权保护的图片。

P9

Fair use, a legal doctrine that allows for a limited usage of copyrighted materials without permission by the original creators for purposes such as teaching and news sharing, is what a lot of generative model creators claim that their training data is covered by.

合理使用是一项法律原则，允许在未经原作者许可的情况下，为教学和新闻分享等目的有限度地使用受版权保护的材料，许多生成式模型的创建者声称其训练数据受此条款保护。

However, since fair use applies differently to each case, it's hard to make a blanket statement about all works in a dataset as being applicable.

然而，由于合理使用在每个案件中的适用情况各不相同，很难对数据集中的所有作品是否适用一概而论。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2448 词 · 26 句

P1

On a warm October morning, Daniel Agok woke up to another disappointing day at his school in Wanyjok, South Sudan.

十月里一个温暖的早晨，丹尼尔·阿戈克醒来，又将在南苏丹万约克的学校迎来令人失望的一天。

The classes he had planned teaching computer skills to aspiring young professionals were canceled, due to an unreliable diesel generator and the absence of a municipal power grid in his area.

他原计划为有志成为专业人才的年轻人教授计算机技能，但由于柴油发电机时断时续，当地又没有市政电网，这些课程只能取消。

Now, he would be losing frustrated customers as well as income.

这下，他不仅会流失那些失望的客户，还会损失收入。

Agok is just one of the hundreds of millions of people in sub-Saharan Africa, many of whom are business owners, who still do not have dependable electricity.

在撒哈拉以南非洲，数亿人至今仍用不上可靠的电力，其中不乏企业主，阿戈克只是其中之一。

This inequitable access to energy continues to put their livelihood and future at risk.

这种能源获取上的不平等，持续威胁着他们的生计与未来。

P2

The current system keeps a lid on the economic talents of people like Agok and his students.

现行体制压制了阿戈克及其学生这类人的经济才能。

We must instead unleash their abilities and let Africa reach its true economic potential.

我们应当转而释放他们的潜能，让非洲充分发挥其经济潜力。

That means wiring Africa with clean, dependable energy.

这就意味着要用清洁、可靠的能源为非洲通电。

P3

Historically, national governments on the continent have led electrification efforts, but fewer than half of the people surveyed say that the national grid is providing a reliable supply of electricity.

从历史上看，非洲大陆各国政府一直是电气化建设的主导者，但受访者中不到一半的人表示国家电网提供了可靠的电力供应。

P4

This has led to expensive and harmful temporary "solutions".

这催生了代价高昂且有害的临时“解决方案”。

In Nigeria alone experts estimate people spend $13 billion every year on fuel for toxic, polluting generators.

仅在尼日利亚，专家估计人们每年就要花130亿美元购买燃油，为有毒、污染环境的发电机供能。

That number will only grow as the continent's population hits 2.5 billion people by 2050.

随着非洲大陆人口到2050年将增至25亿，这一数字还只会继续增长。

Deploying modern grid infrastructure with speed and at scale is essential to replacing these temporary solutions with reliable and affordable alternatives.

要以可靠且价格可负担的替代方案取代这些临时解决方案，就必须快速、大规模地部署现代化电网基础设施。

P5

The rapidly growing private power sector in Africa holds the key to achieving the speed and scale necessary to close the continent's energy access gap.

非洲快速增长的私营电力部门掌握着关键，唯有凭借其所需的速度与规模，才能弥合非洲大陆的能源获取缺口。

Private companies today are building world-class infrastructure and driving forward economic growth with sustainable business models.

如今，私营企业正在建设世界一流基础设施，并以可持续的商业模式推动经济增长。

In fact, many governments agree and have begun to embrace the private sector as partners in providing reliable power at scale.

事实上，许多政府对此表示认同，并已开始把私营部门视为合作伙伴，共同提供大规模的可靠电力。

In South Africa, President Cyril Ramaphosa has streamlined licensing requirements and enlisted the private sector to play a key role.

在南非，总统西里尔·拉马福萨简化了许可要求，并让私营部门发挥关键作用。

P6

South Africa is just one example.

南非只是其中一个例子。

Across the continent, the private sector has delivered power faster and more reliably, and governments are increasingly turning to private partners.

在整个非洲大陆，私营部门供电更快也更可靠，各国政府正越来越多地寻求与私营伙伴合作。

P7

With the right funding and structure in place, the private sector will come together to provide this essential service.

只要资金到位、架构得当，私营部门就会汇聚起来，提供这项必不可少的服务。

After all, electrifying sub-Saharan Africa is a $350 billion opportunity.

毕竟，让撒哈拉以南非洲实现电气化是一个价值3500亿美元的机遇。

P8

Today, Daniel Agok is steadily growing his business in South Sudan.

如今，丹尼尔·阿戈克正在南苏丹稳步发展自己的生意。

His town of Wanyjok was electrified by a private company, providing clean, reliable, and affordable power to his business and his neighbors.

他所在的万尼乔克镇由一家私营公司通了电，为其生意和邻居们提供了清洁、可靠且价格可负担的电力。

Agok now operates his growing computer skills training business smoothly.

阿戈克如今经营着他不断壮大的计算机技能培训业务，运转顺利。

His classes are no longer affected by the smell and the constant noise of the diesel generator, and he saves money with more affordable power.

他的课堂不再受柴油发电机的气味和持续噪声的影响，而且电费更加实惠，帮他省下了开支。

His students are developing hardware and software skills.

他的学生们正在锻炼硬件和软件方面的技能。
