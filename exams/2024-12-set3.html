About 23% of the global population lives in absolute poverty.

全球约有23%的人口生活在绝对贫困之中。

In developing countries there is a low life expectancy, a high infant mortality rate, high levels of unemployment and illiteracy, nutritional levels below acceptable standards and widespread disease with very little or poor quality medical assistance.

发展中国家普遍预期寿命偏低、婴儿死亡率居高不下、失业和文盲率高企、营养水平低于可接受标准，疾病肆虐，而医疗救助极少或质量低劣。

Others live incredibly wealthy and luxurious lives and so the wealth is distributed in a very unequal manner.

另一些人则过着极其富裕奢华的生活，财富的分配因此极为不均。

这些是慈善问题的核心所在，不过慈善还涵盖许多其他领域，比如帮助老年人。

当富豪们承诺捐出大部分身家时，我们无不为之喝彩，但他们通常仍留有数百万留给后人，这仍远远超过大多数人一生所能期望挣到的钱。

Even comparatively moderate salaries in the west are very high when compared globally.

即便西方国家中相对中等的收入，放在全球范围内看也相当高。

Someone on £ 20,000 could easily afford to give away £ 2,000 per year and still have plenty to live on plus some luxuries.

一个年收入两万英镑的人，完全可以每年捐出两千英镑，剩下的钱仍足够维持生活并享受一些奢侈品。

大多数人都认同捐赠慈善在道德上值得赞扬，但慈善行为往往被视为超出义务要求的额外之举。

Some argue, however, that charitable behaviour is morally required.

然而，也有人认为慈善行为是道德上的必需。

This means that to fail to behave charitably would be wrong.

这意味着，不行善举即为错误。

The majority of arguments in this vein refer to giving aid to poorer nations but they may also refer to giving time within one's immediate community.

持此类观点的论据大多着眼于向较贫穷国家提供援助，但也可指在自己所在的社区中奉献时间。

Arguments tend to place different requirements on people in different income brackets and exclude entirely people who are merely making ends meet.

这类论据往往对不同收入阶层的人提出不同的要求，而完全将那些仅能勉强维持生计的人排除在外。

Some argue that people should all contribute a certain percentage of their earnings.

有人主张，每个人都应将收入的一定比例捐献出来。

尽管人们对于这一道德义务究竟应到何种程度存在很大分歧，我仍会把所有主张人们在道德上有义务向慈善事业捐款的论点归为一类。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1205 词 · 57 句

A

Trade is one of the policy areas where the hostility that exists between populists and classical liberals is most visible.

贸易是民粹主义者与古典自由主义者之间的敌意表现得最为明显的政策领域之一。

Free-traders point to the undeniable good that tariff-free trade has done for consumers across the world and to the observable alleviation of poverty in corners of the world where previously closed markets have been opened up.

自由贸易论者指出，免关税贸易为全球消费者带来了无可否认的益处，而且在那些此前封闭的市场被打开之后，世界某些角落的贫困状况得到了肉眼可见的缓解。

Protectionists point to the domestic producers who've paid the price for this globalized economy in the form of lost livelihoods and hollowed-out communities.

贸易保护主义者则指出，国内生产者为这个全球化经济付出了代价——生计丧失，社区凋敝。

B

The ongoing conservative civil war often degenerates into content-free tribal warfare, but trade is a rare exception.

这场持续不断的保守派内讧往往沦为毫无实质内容的部落式混战，但贸易是一个少有的例外。

There are substantial, thought-out policy proposals on both sides of the argument.

争论双方都有实质性、经过深思熟虑的政策提案。

C

Consequently, trade as a topic of discussion provides an opportunity for liberals and populists to have a real meeting of minds.

因此，贸易作为讨论话题，为自由主义者和民粹主义者提供了一个真正达成思想共识的机会。

Fruitful debates might actually take place in this area, as opposed to the familiar ritual we've become accustomed to of condemnation met with counter-condemnation.

在这个领域，富有成效的辩论或许真能展开，而不只是我们早已习以为常的那套谴责与反谴责的例行仪式。

D

Strange as it might sound, the problem with trade in the modern world isn't a matter of dollars and cents.

听起来或许有些奇怪，当今世界贸易的问题并非关乎金钱得失。

It's a matter of false consciousness.

而是关乎错误意识。

This observation is bound to set Marxist alarm bells ringing in the minds of some readers, but it was first made by Alexis de Tocqueville in 1840.

这一观察必然会令一些读者心中响起马克思主义的警钟，但它最早是由托克维尔在1840年提出的。

E

Tracking the economic development of mankind from primitive to modern societies, Tocqueville observed a paradox unfolding over the centuries as economic realities and human experience of those realities strayed further and further from each other.

托克维尔在追溯人类经济从原始社会到现代社会的发展历程时，发现了一个延续数个世纪的悖论：经济现实与人类对这些现实的体验越来越彼此背离。

In primitive societies, he notes, the division of labor was as yet undeveloped for the most part, requiring each person, family, or tribe to be relatively independent when it came to meeting their own basic material needs.

他指出，在原始社会中，劳动分工在很大程度上尚未发展，每个人、每个家庭或每个部落在满足自身基本物质需求时都相对独立。

Men built their own dwellings, farmed their own land, tended to their own livestock.

人们自己建造住所，耕种自己的土地，照料自家的牲畜。

This is not to deny that basic forms of trade took place, but, for the most part, our primitive ancestors lived fairly self-reliant, if crushingly poor, lives.

这并不是要否认当时已有基本的贸易形式，但在大多数情况下，我们的原始祖先过着相当自给自足的生活，尽管穷得令人窒息。

F

However, the exclusively local and face-to-face nature of economic and political organization in the ancient and prehistoric worlds constantly impressed upon these primitive peoples the uncontrollable reality of others and their needs.

然而，在古代和史前世界，经济与政治组织完全是地方性的、面对面的，这不断使这些原始人真切地意识到，他人及其需求是自己无法控制的现实。

As Tocqueville notes, "as soon as a man begins to deal with common affairs in public, he begins to perceive that he is not so independent of his fellow men as he had first imagined, and that in order to obtain their support he must often lend them his cooperation."

正如托克维尔所指出的："一个人一旦开始在公共场合处理公共事务，就会开始意识到，他并不像最初想象的那样独立于同胞，而为了获得他们的支持，他往往必须与他们合作。"

G

At the advent of the modern world, the division of labor spread further and further throughout society.

随着现代世界的来临，分工在社会中日益广泛地扩展。

Each person became more and more dependent on others for their basic needs.

每个人在基本需求上越来越依赖他人。

And yet, robbed of the engagement with our neighbors and with our local communities that our ancestors were forced into by circumstance, we feel ourselves to be more and more independent of one another.

然而，我们的祖先因环境所迫而不得不与邻里和地方社区打交道，如今我们失去了这种交往，反倒觉得自己越来越不依赖彼此。

As we become more and more dependent on others, we become less and less conscious of our dependence on others.

我们对他人越来越依赖，却越来越意识不到这种依赖。

This is the paradox of trade in the modern world.

这就是现代世界中贸易的悖论。

H

The false consciousness that this paradox generates causes havoc on the debates we have about free trade.

这一悖论所催生的错误意识，严重扰乱了我们关于自由贸易的辩论。

There is scarcely a single commodity in any American household that isn't dependent for its manufacture and sale, through one supply chain or another, on scores of different people spread out across the entire globe.

在任何一个美国家庭中，几乎找不到一件商品，其生产和销售不是通过这样或那样的供应链，依赖于散布在全球各地的数十名不同的人。

But as Tocqueville already foresaw in 1840, we do not feel dependent on these strangers for our way of life.

但正如托克维尔早在1840年就预见的那样，我们并不觉得自己的生活方式依赖于这些陌生人。

No sense of the dependence of our own material welfare on their work ever strikes our national consciousness.

我们的国民意识从未意识到，自身的物质福祉有赖于他人的劳动。

We rarely contemplate the globalized avenues of free trade with gratitude.

我们很少怀着感恩之心去审视自由贸易所开辟的全球化通道。

I

There are two reasons for this.

这背后有两个原因。

The first, to put it bluntly, is money.

第一个原因，直白地说，就是金钱。

Money allows us to purchase the work of others without giving any thought to them as human beings.

金钱让我们得以购买他人的劳动，而无需把他们当作人来考虑。

Unlike our ancestors in their primitive townships, we rarely have to meet face-to-face the people who've invented, built, shipped, or supplied our goods.

不同于生活在原始城镇中的祖先，我们很少需要与那些发明、制造、运输或供应商品的人面对面接触。

No relationship has to be built before an exchange can take place.

在交易发生之前，不必建立任何关系。

Simply agree on a price, and you can have any goods you wish without taking a second thought for the human being involved on the other side of the transaction.

只要商定一个价格，你就能得到任何想要的商品，而不会再多想交易另一端的那个人。

In this way, money makes us feel more independent than we actually are.

如此一来，金钱让我们感觉自己比实际更加独立。

Each of us senses the hold that it has over our fellows.

我们每个人都感受到金钱对他人的掌控力。

We know that if we bid highly enough we can buy ourselves out of the time-consuming labor of building relationships.

我们知道，只要出价足够高，就能花钱让自己免于建立关系这件耗时的苦差。

Money is kind of like magic in that way.

就此而言，金钱有点像魔法。

It gives us a set of rituals to perform and promises that if we do so we'll be able to wield power over others.

它为我们提供了一套需要遵行的仪式，并承诺只要照做，我们就能驾驭他人、掌控他人。

The illusion is created that having enough money to buy something is the equivalent of knowing how to make it yourself.

一种错觉由此产生：有钱买到某样东西，就等于懂得如何亲手把它制造出来。

Gratitude for the anonymous men and women who make up the supply chain rarely makes its way into our consciousness.

对于构成供应链的那些无名男女，我们很少心存感激，也很少意识到他们的存在。

J

Anonymity, in fact, is the second root cause of the free-trade paradox.

事实上，匿名性是自由贸易悖论的第二个根本原因。

Modernity has emancipated everyone from the limits of location and community.

现代性把每个人从地域与社群的局限中解放了出来。

By and large, when we trade, we trade with strangers; when we vote, we vote for strangers; when we watch, read, or listen to stories, the tellers of the tales are strangers.

总体而言，我们交易时，交易的对象是陌生人；投票时，投票的对象是陌生人；观看、阅读或聆听故事时，讲故事的人同样是陌生人。

As opposed to the ancestors Tocqueville compares us to, we do not know the people with whom we have to do, in either the economic or the political sphere.

与托克维尔用来和我们作比较的先辈不同，无论在经济领域还是政治领域，我们都不认识那些与我们打交道的人。

This is simply the shadow side of the miracle of markets, which, for the first time in history, have allowed strangers to look after each other.

这不过是市场奇迹的阴暗面；正是市场，在历史上第一次让陌生人得以彼此关照。

They've also allowed each of us to live more and more of our lives exclusively as strangers to other people.

然而，市场也让我们每个人越来越多地只是以陌生人的身份度过自己的生活。

This is how Tocqueville—rather pessimistically—describes us: Each, standing apart, is like a stranger to the destiny of others; his children and personal friends forming for him the entire human race.

托克维尔颇为悲观地这样描述我们：每个人各自独立，对他人的命运如同陌生人；他的子女和私交好友便构成了他眼中全部的人类。

As for the remainder of his fellow citizens, he is beside them, but he does not see them.

至于其余的同胞公民，他虽与他们比肩而立，却看不见他们。

He touches them, but he does not feel them.

他触碰到他们，却感觉不到他们的存在。

He exists only in and for himself.

他只为自身而存在，也仅存在于自身之中。

K

The last sentence but one is as apt a summary as one could hope to come by of how each of us functions in the modern economy: "He touches them, but he does not feel them."

倒数第二句话是对我们每个人在现代经济中如何运作的最佳概括，正如人们所能期待的那样恰如其分："他触碰了它们，却感受不到它们。"

L

This is the greatest challenge facing defenders of free trade.

这是自由贸易捍卫者面临的最大挑战。

It's exceedingly difficult for human beings to feel gratitude toward strangers, and the global marketplace that has made us so rich has also made us strangers to one another.

人类极难对陌生人怀有感激之情，而使我们变得如此富足的全球市场，同时也让我们彼此形同陌路。

Our brains are hardwired for tribal life, and tribes do not take kindly to strangers.

我们的大脑为部落生活而生，而部落对陌生人向来并不友善。

Impressing a sense of dependence upon and gratitude toward foreign strangers is therefore an uphill task.

因此，让人们对外国陌生人产生依赖感和感激之情，是一项极其艰难的任务。

M

If free-traders are going to win policy arguments in the future, they'll have to find a way of forging bonds of affection between American consumers and foreign producers.

如果自由贸易者希望在未来赢得政策辩论，他们就必须找到一种方式，在美国消费者与外国生产者之间建立起情感纽带。

Only by de-anonymizing the men and women who supply us with the goods and services we enjoy from overseas and by creating a sense of solidarity and relationship across borders that transcends economic interest can free trade win the day.

只有将那些为我们提供海外商品和服务的男男女女从匿名状态中还原出来，并在跨国界层面建立起一种超越经济利益的团结感和联系，自由贸易才能赢得胜利。

Otherwise, the inborn biological upper hand that protectionists have in the form of nationalist solidarity is bound to win the day at the ballot box.

否则，保护主义者凭借民族主义团结这一先天的生物学优势，势必会在投票箱前赢得最终胜利。

P1

There are hundreds of personality quizzes online that assert they can ascertain whether the right or left half of your brain is dominant.

网上有数百种人格测试，声称能够判定你是右脑还是左脑占主导。

Left-brained people are supposedly logical and excel at language and math while right-brained people are more imaginative, emotionally intelligent and skilled with spatial reasoning.

据称，左脑型人擅长逻辑，在语言和数学方面表现出色，而右脑型人更具想象力、情商更高，空间推理能力也更强。

There's just one problem: That's not how brains work.

但问题只有一个：大脑并非如此运作。

P2

Popular science enthusiasts sort of took this idea and ran with it, and it's become woven in popular culture now, and it's not going away.

科普爱好者多少把这个观点奉为圭臬并加以传播，如今它已融入流行文化，而且不会销声匿迹。

P3

Despite this enduring belief, there's no such thing as being "right-brained" or "left-brained".

尽管这种观念根深蒂固，但人根本不存在所谓“右脑型”或“左脑型”。

Whether you're someone who tends more towards creativity or logic has nothing to do with one hemisphere of your brain being dominant over the other.

一个人更偏重创造力还是逻辑思维，与大脑某个半球是否占主导毫无关系。

But the actual science of how the two halves of our brains work together is sometimes stranger than fiction.

然而，关于大脑两半球如何协同工作的真实科学，有时比虚构的故事还要离奇。

P4

The human brain is divided into two hemispheres, the left and right.

人脑分为左右两个半球。

In all vertebrate animals, the right hemisphere controls the left side of the body and vice versa.

在所有脊椎动物中，右半球控制身体左侧，左半球控制身体右侧。

And scientists have long known, thanks to the behaviors of patients who suffered brain injuries, that different areas of the brain do different things.

科学家早就知道，大脑的不同区域各司其职，这一点是从脑损伤患者的行为表现中获知的。

P5

But many scientists struggled with this idea, because the very suggestion that the left and right halves of the brain operate differently disrupted the idea that nature tends toward perfect symmetry.

但许多科学家曾对这一观点感到困惑，因为只要承认大脑左右两半运作方式不同，就动摇了自然界趋向完美对称这一观念。

P6

Work by neuroscientists has revealed the importance of different hemispheres of the brain for different activities.

神经科学家的研究揭示了大脑不同半球在不同活动中的重要作用。

However, their research quickly saw some misinterpretations in the general public: Some presumed creative people must be right-brained and logical people left-brained.

然而，他们的研究很快便发现公众中存在一些误解：有人认为，有创造力的人必定是右脑型，而讲逻辑的人则是左脑型。

It is proven that not only is personality unrelated to the different halves of the brain, but people aren't really right- or left-brained to begin with.

事实已证明，性格不仅与大脑左右半球无关，而且人们原本就并非真正的右脑型或左脑型。

The idea that we have left-dominant people and right-dominant people, and that this is related to personality, is categorically false.

所谓存在左脑主导型和右脑主导型的人，且这与性格相关，这种说法完全是错误的。

That's never been supported in the neuroscience community.

这一说法从未得到神经科学界的认可。

Neuroscientists don't believe that and never have.

神经科学家从不相信这一点，过去也从未相信过。

P7

What scientists learned is that there are really important differences between the left hemisphere and the right hemisphere.

科学家所了解到的是，大脑左半球和右半球之间确实存在重要差异。

It's just that they have nothing to do with personality or whether cognitive strategy is more logical or free spirited or creative.

只不过这些差异与性格无关，也与认知策略是更偏向逻辑、更自由随性还是更具创造力无关。

While researchers have shown the limitations of how the hemispheres of our brains influence our lives, it's not difficult to understand the appeal of such ideas.

虽然研究人员已经揭示了大脑两半球对人们生活影响的局限性，但这类观点为何如此具有吸引力，也并不难理解。

People are endlessly fascinated by themselves and their friends, and the subtle differences in how people think about the world are really meaningful to them.

人们对自己和朋友有着无穷的好奇，而人们思考世界的细微差异对他们而言确实意义重大。

When you come up with an online quiz that tells us something about ourselves, we're drawn to that.

当你推出一个能让我们更了解自己的在线测试时，我们就会被它吸引。

It's irresistible.

这是难以抗拒的。

But you have to take it with an enormous grain of salt.

但你必须对此抱以极大的怀疑态度。

P1

One hundred thirty-five students, four teachers, one giant classroom: This is what 9th grade looks like at Westwood High School, in Mesa, Arizona's largest school system.

135名学生、4位老师、一间巨型教室：这就是亚利桑那州最大校区梅萨市韦斯特伍德高中九年级的日常景象。

There, an innovative teaching model has taken hold, and is spreading to other schools in the district and beyond.

在那里，一种创新教学模式已经落地生根，并正向该学区及其他地区的学校推广开来。

P2

Five years ago, faced with high teacher turnover and declining student enrollment, Westwood's leaders decided to try something different.

五年前，面对教师流失严重和学生人数下滑的困境，韦斯特伍德的校领导决定另辟蹊径。

Working with professors at Arizona State University's teachers college, they piloted a classroom model known as team teaching, which allows teachers to dissolve the walls that separate their classes across physical or grade divides.

他们与亚利桑那州立大学教育学院的教授合作，试点了一种被称为协同教学的课堂模式，让教师们打破班级之间的壁垒，不再受学科或年级界限的束缚。

P3

The teachers share large groups of students—sometimes 100 or more—and rotate between group instruction, one-on-one interventions, small study groups, or whatever the teachers as a team agree is a priority that day.

教师们共同负责大批学生——有时多达100人甚至更多——并在集体授课、一对一辅导、小组研讨等方式之间轮换，具体安排由教师团队根据当天的重点共同商定。

What looks at times like chaos is in fact a carefully orchestrated plan: Each morning, the Westwood teams meet to hammer out a personalized program for every student the team will focus on that day.

乍看之下有时像一团混乱，实则是精心编排的计划：每天早晨，韦斯特伍德的各教师团队都会开会，为当天需要重点关注的每一位学生制定个性化方案。

P4

By giving teachers more opportunity to collaborate, Mesa's administrators hoped to fill staffing gaps and boost teacher morale and retention.

通过给教师提供更多协作机会，梅萨的管理者希望以此填补师资缺口，提升教师士气并留住人才。

Initial research suggests the gamble could pay off.

初步研究显示，这一大胆尝试可能正在见效。

P5

"Teachers are doing fantastic things, but it's very rare a teacher walks into another room to see what's happening," said Andi Fourlis, superintendent of Mesa Public Schools.

"老师们在做着了不起的事，但一位老师走进另一间教室看看别人在做什么，这种情况非常少见，"梅萨公立学校学监安迪·福利斯说道。

"Our profession is so slow to advance because we are working in isolation."

"我们这个行业之所以进步如此缓慢，就是因为我们各自孤立地工作。"

P6

Of course, overhauling teaching approaches can't fix all the frustrations teachers have, such as low pay, but early results from Mesa show team teaching may be helping to reverse low morale.

当然，改革教学方式无法解决教师面临的所有困扰，比如薪酬偏低等问题，但梅萨的早期成果表明，协同教学可能有助于扭转士气低落的局面。

In a survey of hundreds of the district's teachers, researchers found those who worked on teams reported greater job satisfaction, more frequent collaborations with colleagues, and more positive interactions with students.

在对学区数百名教师的调查中，研究人员发现，参与团队教学的教师工作满意度更高、与同事协作更频繁、与学生的互动也更为积极。

P7

Another benefit of teams, teachers say, is that they can help each other improve their instruction.

教师们说，团队的另一个好处是成员可以互相帮助提升教学水平。

During one planning session, English teacher Jeff Hall shared a performance appraisal with a science teacher: Her recent lecture on something she called "the central dogma of biology" had bewildered him and their other teammates.

在一次集体备课中，英语教师杰夫·霍尔向一位科学教师展示了一份教学评估：她最近讲授的关于她所谓的“生物学中心法则”的课程令他和团队其他成员感到困惑。

P8

"If the science is too confusing for me, can you imagine the frustration you feel as kids?"

“如果这门科学对我而言都太难理解，你们可以想象孩子们会有多沮丧。”

Hall said.

霍尔说道。

But the science teacher, he said, wouldn't have known about the confusion on her own.

但他说，那位科学教师自己并没有意识到这种困惑。

P9

The model is not for everyone.

这种模式并不适合所有人。

Some teachers approached about volunteering for a team have said they prefer to work alone.

一些被邀请自愿加入团队的教师表示，他们更喜欢独立工作。

Team teaching can also be a scheduling nightmare, especially at schools like Westwood where only some staff work in teams.

协同教学在排课上也可能是一场噩梦，尤其是在像韦斯特伍德这样只有部分教职员工参与团队合作的学校。

There are also thorny questions like how to evaluate four teachers on the performance of 135 students.

还有一些棘手的问题，比如如何根据135名学生的表现来评价四位教师的工作。

But for the time being, it seems to be working.

但就目前而言，这似乎行之有效。
