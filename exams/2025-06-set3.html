The slow progress of women in elective office is frustrating for some political observers and experts—including myself.

女性在民选公职中的缓慢进展，令一些政治观察家和专家感到沮丧——我本人也不例外。

Achieving gender equality in Congress is an important goal.

实现国会中的性别平等是一个重要目标。

This is because the number of women involved in legislative decisions has significant consequences for all the policies that governments enact.

原因在于，参与立法决策的女性数量，对政府制定的所有政策都有重大影响。

Female legislators are more likely than men to introduce, speak about and work to pass policies that disproportionately affect women and girls, such as paid family leave, pay equity and gender-based violence.

与男性相比，女性立法者更有可能提出、讨论并推动通过那些对女性和女童影响尤为突出的政策，如带薪家庭假、同工同酬以及针对基于性别的暴力的政策。

Having more women in Congress also fosters a greater sense of connection between female voters and government.

国会中有更多女性，还能增强女性选民与政府之间的归属感和联结感。

In addition, it bolsters women's sense that government cares about their concerns and inspires young women to become more politically engaged.

此外，这有助于增强女性对政府关心其诉求的信心，并激励年轻女性更多地参与政治。

While women are underrepresented in governments around the globe, it is a particularly formidable problem in the United States.

尽管全球各国政府中女性的代表比例普遍偏低，但这一问题在美国尤为严峻。

Currently, the U.S. ranks 73rd in the world when it comes to female representation in government.

目前，在政府中女性代表比例方面，美国在全世界排名第73位。

But the reason women are so dramatically underrepresented in U.S. government is not because they face resistance from voters or struggle to raise money.

然而，女性在美国政府中严重缺乏代表性，并不是因为她们遭遇选民的抵制或筹款困难。

On the contrary, decades of research shows that female candidates raise as much money and win as often as male politicians with similar credentials.

恰恰相反，数十年的研究表明，资历相当的女性候选人筹集的资金与获胜的频率，与男性政治家不相上下。

Rather, the slow progress of women in politics is a tale of two political parties.

真正的原因在于，女性在政治中进展缓慢，其实是一个关乎两党的故事。

In the next Congress, there will be 107 female Democratic lawmakers and 42 female Republican lawmakers in the Senate and House combined.

在下一届国会中，参众两院合计将有107名民主党女性议员和42名共和党女性议员。

In order for women to gain half of the seats in Congress, more women need to run, especially on Republican tickets.

若要让女性占据国会一半的席位，就需要有更多女性参选，尤其是在共和党的候选人名单上。

This will require the Republican Party as a whole to prioritize recruiting women—and not just for one election cycle, but in a sustained way.

这要求共和党全党把招募女性作为优先事项——而且不是只在一个选举周期内，而是持之以恒地推进。

A

I can be a reluctant socialiser.

我可能是个不情愿的社交者。

I'm sometimes secretly pleased when social plans are called off.

有时社交计划取消了，我暗自高兴。

I get restless a few hours into a hangout.

聚会进行几个小时后，我就开始坐立不安。

I even once went on a free 10-day silent meditation retreat—not for the meditation, but for the silence.

我甚至参加过一次为期十天的免费静修——不是为了冥想，而是为了那份安静。

B

So I can relate to author Anneli Rufus, who wrote in *Party of One: The Loners' Manifesto*: "When parents on TV shows punished their kids by ordering them to go to their rooms, I was confused. I loved my room. Being there behind a locked door was a treat. To me a punishment was being ordered to play games with my cousin Louis."

所以我能理解作家安妮莉·鲁弗斯，她在《独行者：孤独者的宣言》中写道：“电视节目里，父母惩罚孩子的方式是让他们回房间反省，我对此很困惑。我喜欢我的房间。把自己关在门后对我来说是一种享受。在我看来，惩罚应该是被叫去跟表弟路易斯玩。”

C

Unsocial tendencies like these are often far from ideal.

像这样不合群的倾向往往远非理想。

Abundant research shows the harms of social isolation, considered a serious public health problem in countries that have rapidly ageing populations (though talk of a 'loneliness epidemic' may be exaggerated).

大量研究表明了社交孤立的危害，在人口迅速老龄化的国家，它被视为一个严重的公共卫生问题（尽管所谓‘孤独流行病’的说法可能被夸大了）。

In the UK, the Royal College of General Practitioners says that loneliness has the same risk level for premature death as diabetes.

在英国，全科医师学院表示，孤独导致过早死亡的风险水平与糖尿病相当。

Strong social connections are important for cognitive functioning, motor function and a smoothly running immune system.

牢固的社会联系对认知功能、运动机能以及免疫系统的正常运转都很重要。

D

This is especially clear from cases of extreme social isolation.

极端社交孤立的案例尤其能说明这一点。

Examples of people kept in captivity, children kept isolated in abusive orphanages, and prisoners kept in solitary confinement all show how prolonged solitude can lead to hallucinations and other forms of mental instability.

被囚禁者的经历、在虐待孤儿院中被隔离的孩子，以及被单独监禁的囚犯，都表明长期的孤独可能导致幻觉和其他形式的精神失常。

E

But these are severe and involuntary cases of aloneness.

但这些是严重而非自愿的独处情形。

For those of us who just prefer plenty of alone time, emerging research suggests some good news: there are upsides to being alone—for both our work lives and our emotional well-being.

对于我们这些只是喜欢拥有大量独处时间的人来说，最新研究带来了一些好消息：独处有其益处——无论对我们的工作生活还是情感健康而言。

F

Solitude is a time for reflection, for thinking about things more deeply.

独处是反思的时刻，是更深入思考事物的时刻。

In a world increasingly focused on speed and competitiveness, solitude is precious and something to be treasured.

在一个日益注重速度和竞争的世界里，独处弥足珍贵，值得珍惜。

And there are benefits in learning to love your solitude.

而且，学会享受独处也有诸多好处。

G

One key benefit of solitude is improved creativity.

独处的一个关键好处是提升创造力。

Gregory Feist, who focuses on the psychology of creativity at California's San Jose State University, has defined creativity as thinking or activity with two key elements: originality and usefulness.

加州圣何塞州立大学专注于创造力心理学研究的格雷戈里·费斯特将创造力定义为包含两个关键要素的思维或活动：原创性和实用性。

He has found that personality traits commonly associated with creativity are openness (receptiveness to new thoughts and experiences), self-confidence, and autonomy—which may include "a lack of concern for social norms" and "a preference for being alone".

他发现，通常与创造力相关的人格特质包括开放性（对新思想和新体验的接纳）、自信和自主性——后者可能包括“对社会规范的漠视”和“偏好独处”。

In fact, Feist's research on both artists and scientists shows that one of the most prominent features of creative folks is their lesser interest in socialising.

事实上，费斯特对艺术家和科学家的研究都表明，富有创造力的人最显著的特征之一就是他们对社交的兴趣较低。

H

One reason for this is that such people are likely to spend sustained time alone working on their craft.

原因之一在于，这类人往往会花大量时间独自钻研自己的技艺。

Plus, Feist says, many artists "are trying to make sense of their internal world and a lot of internal personal experiences that they're trying to give expression and meaning to through their art."

此外，费斯特说，许多艺术家“正试图理解自己的内心世界和大量内在的个人体验，并试图通过艺术来表达这些体验并赋予其意义”。

Solitude allows for the reflection and observation necessary for that creative process.

独处为这一创作过程提供了必要的反思和观察空间。

I

A recent justification of these ideas came from University at Buffalo psychologist Julie Bowker, who researches social withdrawal.

纽约州立大学布法罗分校研究社交退缩的心理学家朱莉·鲍克最近的研究为这些观点提供了佐证。

Social withdrawal is usually categorised into three types: shyness caused by fear or anxiety; avoidance, from a dislike of socialising; and unsociability, from a preference for solitude.

社交退缩通常分为三种类型：因恐惧或焦虑引起的害羞；因厌恶社交而产生的回避；以及因偏好独处而导致的不合群。

A paper by Bowker and her colleagues was the first to show that a type of social withdrawal could have a positive effect—they found that creativity was linked specifically to unsociability.

鲍克及其同事的一篇论文首次表明，某种社交退缩可能具有积极影响——他们发现创造力与不合群这一类型有特定关联。

They also found that unsociability had no correlation with aggression (shyness and avoidance did).

他们还发现，不合群与攻击性没有相关性（而害羞和回避则有）。

This was significant because while previous research had suggested that unsociability might be harmless, Bowker and colleagues' paper showed that it could actually be beneficial.

这一点意义重大，因为此前的研究虽曾暗示不合群可能无害，但鲍克及其同事的论文表明它实际上可能有益。

Unsociable people are likely to be "having just enough interaction," Bowker says.

鲍克说，不合群的人很可能“拥有恰到好处的社交互动”。

"They have a preference for being alone, but they also don't mind being with others."

“他们偏好独处，但也不介意与他人相处。”

J

Still, it turns out that solitude is important for more than creativity.

不过，事实证明，独处的重要性不仅限于创造力。

It's commonly believed that leaders need to be sociable.

人们普遍认为，领导者需要善于社交。

But this depends, among other things, on the personalities of their employees.

但这在很大程度上取决于其下属的性格等因素。

One 2011 study showed that in branches of a pizza chain where employees were more passive, outgoing bosses were associated with higher profits.

2011年的一项研究表明，在某披萨连锁店的分店中，若员工较为被动，外向的老板往往与更高的利润相关联。

But in branches where employees were more active, introverted leaders were more effective.

但在员工较为积极主动的分店，内向的领导者则更为有效。

One reason for this is that introverted people are less likely to feel threatened by strong personalities and suggestions.

原因之一在于，内向者不太容易因强势的个性和建议而感到受威胁。

They're also more likely to listen.

而且他们也更愿意倾听。

K

Since ancient times, meanwhile, people have been aware of a link between isolation and mental focus.

与此同时，自古以来，人们就意识到孤独与精神专注之间存在联系。

After all, cultures with traditions of religious hermits believe that solitude is important for enlightenment.

毕竟，那些有宗教隐士传统的文化都相信，独处对于开悟至关重要。

Recent research has given us a better understanding of why.

近期的研究让我们对其中的原因有了更深入的理解。

One benefit of unsociability is the brain's state of active mental rest, which goes hand-in-hand with the stillness of being alone.

不合群的一个好处是大脑处于积极的精神休憩状态，这种状态与独处时的宁静相辅相成。

When another person is present, your brain can't help but pay some attention.

当有他人在场时，你的大脑难免会分出一部分注意力。

This can be a positive distraction.

这种分心可能是有益的。

But it's still a distraction.

但它终究还是一种分心。

L

Daydreaming in the absence of such distractions activates the brain's default-mode network.

在没有这类干扰的情况下做白日梦，能够激活大脑的默认模式网络。

Among other functions, this network helps to consolidate memory and understand others' emotions.

除了其他功能外，这个网络还有助于巩固记忆和理解他人的情绪。

Giving free rein to a wandering mind not only helps with focus in the long term but strengthens your sense of both yourself and others.

让思绪自由漫游，不仅有助于长期的专注力，还能增强你对自己和他人的感知。

Paradoxically, therefore, periods of solitude actually help when it comes time to socialise once more.

因此，矛盾的是，孤独的时光实际上有助于日后再次进行社交。

And the occasional absence of focus ultimately helps concentration in the long run.

而偶尔的注意力分散，长远来看反而有助于提升专注力。

M

A more recent advocate of thoughtful and productive solitude is Susan Cain, author of *Quiet: The Power of Introverts in a World That Can't Stop Talking* and founder of Quiet Revolution, a company that promotes quiet and introvert-friendly workplaces.

深思熟虑、富有成效的独处的更近一位倡导者是苏珊·凯恩，她是《安静：内向性格的竞争力》（*Quiet: The Power of Introverts in a World That Can't Stop Talking*）的作者，也是Quiet Revolution公司的创始人，该公司倡导安静且适合内向者的工作环境。

"These days, we tend to believe that creativity emerges from a decidedly unreserved socialising process, but in fact it requires sustained attention and deep focus," she says.

她说："如今，我们倾向于认为创造力源自一种明显外放的社交过程，但实际上创造力需要持续的注意力和深度专注。"

"Also, humans are such absorbent social beings that when we surround ourselves with others, we automatically take in their opinions and aesthetics. To truly chart our own path or vision, we have to be willing to isolate ourselves, at least for some period of time."

"此外，人类是如此善于吸收的社交性存在，以至于当我们置身于人群之中时，会不自觉地吸收他人的观点和审美。要真正规划自己的道路或愿景，我们必须要愿意让自己独处，至少在一段时间内。"

N

Still, the line between useful solitude and dangerous isolation can be blurry.

不过，有益的独处与危险的孤立之间的界限有时并不清晰。

"Almost anything can be adaptive and maladaptive, depending on how extreme they get," Feist says.

费斯特说："几乎所有事物都可能具有适应性和非适应性，取决于它们发展到何种极端程度。"

A disorder has to do with dysfunction.

所谓障碍，与功能失调有关。

If someone stops caring about people and cuts off all contact, this could point to an irrational neglect of social relations.

如果一个人不再关心他人，切断一切联系，这可能表明其对社会关系存在不合理的漠视。

But creative unsociability is a far cry from this.

但创造性的不合群与此截然不同。

In fact, Feist says, "there's a real danger with people who are never alone."

事实上，费斯特说："那些从不独处的人反而存在真正的危险。"

It's hard to examine one's own thoughts, feelings and motives, to be self-aware, and fully relaxed unless you have occasional solitude.

如果不偶尔独处，人就很难审视自己的思想、情感和动机，难以做到自我觉察和充分放松。

In addition, introverts tend to have fewer but stronger friendships—which has been linked to greater happiness.

此外，内向者往往拥有较少但更深厚的友谊——这与更高的幸福感相关联。

O

As with many things, quality reigns over quantity.

和许多事情一样，质量重于数量。

Nurturing a few solid relationships without feeling the need to constantly populate your life with endless chatting ultimately may be better for you.

悉心经营几段扎实的关系，而不觉得有必要让生活始终充斥着没完没了的闲聊，最终可能对你更为有益。

P

Thus, if your personality tends toward unsociability, you shouldn't feel the need to change.

因此，如果你的性格偏向不合群，你大可不必觉得自己非得改变。

Of course, that comes with qualifications.

当然，这一点是有前提的。

But as long as you have regular social contact, you are choosing solitude rather than being forced into it, you have at least a few good friends and your solitude is good for your well-being or productivity, there's no point agonising over how to fit a square personality into a round hole.

但只要你的社交往来保持规律、独处是你主动的选择而非被迫为之、你至少有几个好朋友，而且独处有利于你的身心健康或工作效率，那就没有必要为如何将方形的性格硬塞进圆孔里而苦恼了。

Simulators are most often utilized within industries such as nuclear power, aviation and surgery where failure results in disastrous consequences.

模拟器最常用于核电、航空和外科等领域，因为在这些领域中，一旦失败便会带来灾难性的后果。

To maximize the value from a simulation learning experience, participants should immediately and directly apply their learning to a specific intervention within their organization.

为了从模拟学习体验中获得最大价值，参与者应当立即将所学直接应用于组织内部的具体干预举措中。

P2

Most organizations aspire to deploy significant change programs, only to find them nearly impossible to implement.

大多数组织都希望推行重大的变革计划，结果却发现这些计划几乎无法落地实施。

That is largely because successful change requires more than a vision, it requires a workforce that not only doesn't resist change, but embraces it.

这主要是因为，成功的变革不仅仅需要愿景，还需要一支不仅不抵制变革、而且主动拥抱变革的员工队伍。

P3

To achieve success, an organization must build a transformation program that will allow change to be rapidly pulled across its departments and throughout its layers.

要想取得成功，组织必须构建一套转型方案，使变革能够迅速渗透到各个部门和各个层级。

Regardless of the level of senior management commitment, unless key thought leaders at all levels embrace the change, the initiative will wither and die.

无论高层管理者多么投入，除非各层级的关键意见领袖都拥护变革，否则这项举措终将枯萎消亡。

To create this kind of widespread passion, learning leaders must expose the workforce to what could be, which will enable them to rethink their mental models, enable them to break free from their deep-rooted paradigms and embrace the opportunity to learn.

要营造这种普遍的热情，学习领导者必须让员工看到未来的各种可能性，从而使他们能够重新审视自己的心智模式，挣脱根深蒂固的思维范式的束缚，并欣然迎接学习的机会。

P4

Allowing participants to enter a simulated environment provides them with the opportunity to experience alternative realities which can prompt them to rethink their current beliefs.

让参与者进入模拟环境，能为他们提供体验不同现实的机会，从而促使他们反思自己现有的信念。

P5

Behavioral change is not easy for most adults.

对于大多数成年人而言，行为改变并非易事。

Lectures, training programs and workshops can explain the intellectual elements of transformation, but they are seldom effective at getting to the behavioral aspects that lie at the heart of a significant change initiative.

讲座、培训课程和研讨会可以阐明转型在认知层面的要素，但很少能够有效触及重大变革举措的核心——行为层面。

Further, under normal working conditions, managers rarely see the full effect of their employee development efforts.

此外，在正常的工作条件下，管理者很少能看到他们在员工发展方面所做努力的全部效果。

As such, an intervention like a simulation can provide the stimulus for change.

因此，像模拟这样的干预手段可以为变革提供推动力。

P6

An effective simulation can be better than experience as a learning tool because it accelerates time, compresses space, and unlike reality, is specifically designed to maximize participant learning.

有效的模拟作为学习工具，可以胜过实际经验，因为它能压缩时间、浓缩空间，并且与现实不同的是，它专门设计用于最大化参与者的学习效果。

Simulations provide an immersive learning experience where skills, processes and knowledge all can be highlighted in a way reality cannot.

模拟提供了一种沉浸式的学习体验，能够以现实所无法实现的方式突出技能、流程和知识。

The ability to explore, experiment and repeatedly apply new knowledge in unlimited, risk-free models is what makes simulation one of the most productive forms of learning.

在无限且无风险的模型中探索、试验并反复运用新知识的能力，正是使模拟成为最高效学习形式之一的原因。

P7

Well-designed simulations can enable individuals and groups to develop a deep level of understanding about how their decisions and intuitive responses to business stimuli affect their fellow participants and the organization as a whole.

设计良好的模拟能够使个人和团队深入理解，他们对商业刺激所作的决策和本能反应如何影响身边的同伴以及整个组织。

To reap the benefits, however, simulations must feel like reality.

然而，要想从中获益，模拟必须让人有身临其境之感。

At the end of the successful simulation, participants must declare "this is us."

在成功的模拟结束时，参与者必须说出"这就是我们自己"。

If they don't, they will view the experience as a game, which can be difficult to apply on the job, or worse, irrelevant to everyday work tasks.

如果他们做不到这一点，就会把这段经历当作一场游戏，觉得难以应用到实际工作中，甚至认为它与日常工作任务毫无关联。

To maximize benefits from simulation, participants should immediately apply the learning from the experience to forge a smooth link between learning and doing.

为了最大化模拟学习的收益，参与者应立即将这段经历中所学到的东西付诸实践，从而在学习与行动之间建立起顺畅的联系。

P1

GDP growth is not a good indicator of how well a country is performing, and should not be the primary goal of governments.

国内生产总值（GDP）的增长并不能很好地衡量一个国家的运行状况，也不应成为政府的首要目标。

Unlimited growth is not sustainable, and economic thinking is moving toward the idea that we should aim for sustainability in our economic models.

无限增长是不可持续的，经济学界的思维正在转向这样一种理念：我们的经济模式应当以实现可持续发展为目标。

But while a sustainable economy is vital to our future, it is a means to an end, not an end in itself.

然而，尽管可持续经济对我们至关重要，它只是一种手段，而非最终目的。

P2

The idea that governments should focus on happiness has its critics.

政府应当关注民众幸福感这一主张，也招致了不少批评。

There are concerns about how happiness can be measured.

人们担忧幸福感究竟如何衡量。

Is happiness not a fleeting and subjective psychological state?

幸福难道不是一种转瞬即逝、因人而异的心理状态吗？

Don't different people experience different levels of happiness?

不同的人感受到的幸福程度难道不是各不相同吗？

Even on the broadest interpretation of 'happiness' as prosperity or 'life satisfaction', people want different things.

即便将'幸福'作最宽泛的解释，理解为繁荣或'生活满意度'，人们想要的东西也各不相同。

P3

Of course, governments cannot impose life satisfaction on citizens.

当然，政府无法把生活满意度强加给公民。

But our happiness relies on collaborative efforts as a society.

但我们的幸福感依赖于整个社会的共同努力。

A government's obligation lies in creating conditions that promote prosperity.

政府的职责在于创造能够促进繁荣的条件。

And there is good reason to suppose that such conditions exist, are globally applicable, and are discoverable through research.

而且有充分理由认为，这样的条件是存在的，具有全球普遍性，并且可以通过研究加以发现。

P4

In a recently published article, philosopher Julian Baggini suggests we should focus on 'real wealth' for citizens, which does not depend on GDP growth.

在最近发表的一篇文章中，哲学家朱利安·巴吉尼提出，我们应该关注公民的“真实财富”，而这种财富并不依赖于GDP增长。

Access is key: people do not need to own, but rather access things that enable them to live well.

获取是关键：人们不需要拥有，而是需要获取那些能让他们生活得好的东西。

Technological advances and changes in social behavior enable us to make more efficient use of the assets that we already have.

技术进步和社会行为的变化使我们能够更高效地利用已有的资产。

And focusing on access to the resources people need to live better lives could help reduce inequality.

而关注获取改善生活所需的资源，有助于减少不平等。

P5

As far as it goes, this has much in common with proposals tabled by 'happiness' advocates.

就其本身而言，这一点与“幸福”倡导者提出的建议有许多共同之处。

But it sets the bar far too low for what governments can and should be doing for their citizens.

但它对政府能够且应该为公民所做的事，标准定得实在太低了。

P6

For example, it's not clear how a 'real wealth' economy would remedy the epidemic of mental ill-health that plagues our society.

例如，尚不清楚“真实财富”经济将如何解决困扰我们社会的心理疾病流行问题。

In Western countries, at least—poor mental health is more detrimental to wellbeing than poverty.

至少在西方国家——心理健康不佳对福祉的危害甚于贫困。

Over and above a vastly improved provision of therapeutic mental healthcare, there are preventative measures for improving mental health that governments could and should adopt.

除了大幅改善心理治疗服务之外，政府还有能够且应该采取的预防性措施来改善心理健康。

The WHO recommends establishing institutions that facilitate community participation—educational programs and interventions that provide skills for promoting mental wellbeing.

世界卫生组织建议建立促进社区参与的机构——即提供促进心理健康技能的教育项目和干预措施。

It says a lot, however, that the WHO feels the need to appeal to the economic benefits of improving mental health to persuade governments that the cost of taking proposed measures is justified.

然而，世卫组织需要诉诸改善心理健康带来的经济效益来说服政府相信采取这些措施的成本是值得的，这本身就说明了很多问题。

As long as the economy is their priority, governments need go no further than ensuring citizens' continued productivity.

只要经济仍是政府的优先事项，它们需要做的不过是确保公民持续保持生产力。

P7

To demand that governments set the 'happiness' of citizens as their highest priority is to demand that they view citizens as ends in themselves.

要求政府把公民的“幸福”置于最高优先位置，就是要求政府把公民本身视为目的。
