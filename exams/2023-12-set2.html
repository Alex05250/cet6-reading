Our brains respond to language expressing facts differently than they do to words conveying possibility, scientists at New York University have recently found.

纽约大学的科学家近期发现，我们的大脑在处理表达事实的语言时，其反应方式与处理表达可能性的词语时有所不同。

Their work offers new insights into the impact word choice has on how we differentiate between statements expressing what is real versus what is merely possible.

他们的研究为措辞如何影响我们区分「陈述的是真实情况」还是「仅仅是可能情况」提供了新的见解。

The researchers assert their findings are important because we are presented with false information all the time.

研究者指出，他们的发现意义重大，因为我们时时刻刻都在接触到虚假信息。

Some of this is deliberate, as is the case with deceptive advertisements, but the problem is aggravated by individuals who believe they are sharing correct information.

有些虚假信息是蓄意为之，比如欺骗性广告；而那些自认为在传播正确信息的人，更使问题雪上加霜。

Thus, it is more important than ever to separate the factual from the possible or merely speculative in how we communicate.

因此，在我们的交流中，区分事实性内容与可能性或纯属推测的内容，比以往任何时候都更为重要。

This is especially true as the study makes clear that information presented as fact evokes special responses in our brains, which are distinct from when we process the same content with clear indicators of uncertainty.

这一点尤为真切，因为该研究清楚表明，以事实形式呈现的信息会在我们的大脑中引发特殊反应，而这种反应有别于我们在处理带有明确不确定性标记的相同内容时的反应。

In their new study, the scientists intended to ascertain how the brain computes possibilities as expressed by words such as "may," "might," and "if."

在这项新研究中，科学家们旨在探明大脑是如何处理由「可能」「也许」「如果」等词语所表达的可能性的。

The researchers compared brain responses to statements expressing factual scenarios and those expressing possibility.

研究者比较了大脑对表达事实性情景的陈述和表达可能性的陈述的不同反应。

"There is a monster under my bed" exemplifies a factual statement.

「我的床底下有一个怪物」就是一个事实性陈述的例子。

"I will stay home," is also factual.

「我会待在家里」也是事实性陈述。

This is opposed to statements that express possibility, like "There might be a monster under my bed," or "If it rains, I will stay home."

这与表达可能性的陈述形成对照，例如「我的床底下可能有一个怪物」或「如果下雨，我就待在家里」。

The results of the study showed that factual language activated a rapid increase in brain activity, with the brain responding more powerfully and showing more engagement with factual phrases compared to those communicating possibility.

研究结果显示，事实性语言引发了大脑活动的快速增强；与表达可能性的语句相比，大脑对事实性语句的反应更为强烈，参与度也更高。

Thus, facts rule when it comes to the brain.

因此，就大脑而言，事实占据主导地位。

Brain regions involved in processing discourse rapidly distinguish facts from possibilities.

参与语篇加工的脑区能迅速区分事实与可能性。

Further, these regions respond in a much more robust fashion to factual statements.

此外，这些脑区对事实性陈述的反应要强烈得多。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1221 词 · 55 句

A

Most visitors come to Cape Canaveral, on the northeast coast of Florida, for the tourist attractions.

大多数游客来到佛罗里达州东北海岸的卡纳维拉尔角，都是为了这里的旅游景点。

It's home to the second-busiest cruise ship port in the world and is a gateway to the cosmos.

这里是全球第二繁忙的邮轮港口，也是通往宇宙的门户。

Nearly 1.5 million visitors flock here every year to watch rockets, spacecraft, and satellites blast off into the solar system from Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex.

每年有近150万游客蜂拥至此，从肯尼迪航天中心游客中心观看火箭、航天器和卫星发射升空，飞向太阳系。

Nearly 64 kilometers of undeveloped beach and 648 square kilometers of protected refuge fan out from the cape's sandy shores.

近64公里长的未开发海滩和648平方公里的保护区，从这片海角的沙质海岸向外铺展开来。

B

Yet some of Cape Canaveral's most legendary attractions lie unseen, wedged under the sea's surface in mud and sand, for this part of the world has a reputation as a deadly ship trap.

然而，卡纳维拉尔角一些最具传奇色彩的景点却隐匿不见，被埋藏在海面之下的泥沙之中——因为这片海域有着“致命船舶陷阱”的恶名。

Over the centuries, dozens of majestic Old World sailing ships smashed and sank on this irregular stretch of windy Florida coast.

几个世纪以来，数十艘雄伟的旧大陆帆船在这段崎岖多风的佛罗里达海岸上撞毁沉没。

They were vessels built for war and commerce, crossing the globe carrying everything from coins to cannons, boxes of silver and gold, chests of jewels and porcelain, and pearls from the Caribbean.

这些船只为战争和贸易而建，航行于全球各地，运载着从硬币到大炮、成箱的金银、装满珠宝和瓷器的箱子，以及来自加勒比海的珍珠等各种货物。

C

Cape Canaveral contains one of the greatest concentrations of colonial shipwrecks in the world.

卡纳维拉尔角拥有世界上殖民时期沉船遗存最为密集的区域之一。

In recent years, advances in radar, diving, detection equipment, computers, and GPS have transformed the hunt.

近年来，雷达、潜水、探测设备、计算机和GPS的进步彻底改变了搜寻工作。

The naked eye might see a pile of rocks, but technology can reveal the precious artifacts that lie hidden on the ocean floor.

肉眼看到的可能只是一堆岩石，但技术能够揭示隐藏在海底的珍贵文物。

D

As technology renders the seabed more accessible, the hunt for treasure-filled ships has drawn a fresh tide of salvors and their investors—as well as marine archaeologists wanting to bring to light the lost relics.

随着技术让海床变得更易抵达，对满载宝藏船只的搜寻引来了一股新的打捞者及其投资者浪潮——还有希望让失落遗物重见天日的海洋考古学家。

But of late, when salvors have found vessels, their rights have been challenged in court.

但近来，当打捞者发现沉船时，他们的权利却在法庭上受到了挑战。

The big question: who should have control of these treasures?

核心问题在于：这些珍宝的掌控权究竟该归谁？

E

High-stakes fights over shipwrecks pit archaeologists against treasure hunters in a vicious cycle of accusations.

围绕沉船的高额利益争夺战让考古学家与寻宝者针锋相对，陷入相互指责的恶性循环。

Archaeologists regard themselves as protectors of history, and they see salvors as careless destroyers.

考古学家自视为历史的守护者，而把打捞者看作粗枝大叶的破坏者。

Salvors feel they do the hard work of searching for ships, only to have them stolen from under them when discovered.

打捞者则觉得，他们辛辛苦苦寻找沉船，可一旦发现，船就被别人从眼皮底下夺走。

This kind of clash inevitably takes place on a grand scale.

这类冲突不可避免地会演变成大规模的对抗。

Aside from the salvors, their investors, and the maritime archaeologists who serve as expert witnesses, the battles sweep in local and international governments and organizations like UNESCO that work to protect under-water heritage.

除了打捞者、他们的投资人以及出庭作证的水下考古专家之外，这场争夺还牵涉到各国政府、国际组织，以及联合国教科文组织等致力于保护水下遗产的机构。

The court cases that ensue stretch on for years.

随之而来的官司往往一打就是好几年。

Are finders keepers, or do the ships belong to the countries that made them and sent them sailing centuries ago?

究竟是谁发现谁拥有，还是这些船应当归属于几个世纪前建造并派遣它们出海的国家？

Where once salvors and archaeologists worked side by side, now they belong to opposing, and equally contemptuous, tribes.

打捞者与考古学家曾经并肩合作，如今却分属对立的两个阵营，彼此同样心存轻蔑。

F

Nearly three million vessels lie wrecked on the Earth's ocean floor—from old canoes to the Titanic—and likely less than one percent have been explored.

地球的海底散布着近三百万艘沉船——从古老的独木舟到泰坦尼克号——而被探查过的可能还不到百分之一。

Some—like an ancient Roman ship found off Antikythera, Greece, dated between 70 and 60 BC and carrying astonishingly sophisticated gears and dials for navigating by the sun—are critical to a new understanding of our past.

其中一些沉船——比如在希腊安提基特拉岛附近发现的一艘古罗马船只，年代介于公元前70年至60年之间，船上载有用于根据太阳导航的、精巧得令人惊叹的齿轮和刻度盘——对于我们重新认识过去至关重要。

No wonder there is an eternal stirring among everybody from salvors to scholars to find them.

难怪从打捞者到学者，人人都始终怀着发现这些沉船的热望。

G

In May 2016, a salvor named Bobby Pritchett, president of Global Marine Exploration (GME) in Tampa, Florida, announced that he had discovered scattered remains of a ship buried a kilometer off Cape Canaveral.

2016年5月，一位名叫鲍比·普里切特的打捞者——佛罗里达州坦帕市环球海洋勘探公司（GME）总裁——宣布，他在卡纳维拉尔角外海一公里处发现了埋藏在海底的一艘船的散落残骸。

Over the prior three years, he and his crew had obtained 14 state permits to survey a nearly 260-square-kilometer area off the cape; they worked 250 days a year, backed by investor funds of, he claims, US $4 million.

在此前的三年里，他和他的船员获得了14份州政府许可，对该岬角附近近260平方公里的海域进行勘测；他们一年工作250天，据他称，背后有400万美元的投资者资金支持。

It was hard work.

那是一份苦差事。

Crew members were up at dawn, dragging sensors from their expedition vessels back and forth, day in and day out, year after year, to detect metal of any kind.

船员们黎明即起，日复一日、年复一年地拖着探测仪在勘探船上往返作业，以探测任何种类的金属。

Using computer technology, Pritchett and his crew created intricate, color-coded maps marked with the GPS coordinates of thousands of finds, all invisible under a meter of sand.

借助计算机技术，普里切特和船员们绘制出精细的彩色编码地图，标注了数千处发现物的GPS坐标，而这些东西全都埋在一米深的沙子之下，肉眼无法看见。

H

One day in 2015, the magnetometer picked up metal that turned out to be an iron cannon; when the divers blew the sand away, they also discovered a more precious bronze cannon with markings indicating French royalty and, not far off, a famous marble column carved with the coat of arms of France, known from historical paintings.

2015年的一天，磁力仪探测到金属信号，结果是一门铁炮；潜水员吹开沙子后，还发现了一门更为珍贵的青铜炮，上面带有法国王室的标记；不远处，一根著名的大理石柱映入眼帘，上面刻有法国国徽，与历史绘画中的图案一致。

The discovery was cause for celebration.

这一发现值得庆贺。

The artifacts indicated the divers had likely found the wreck of La Trinité, a 16th-century French vessel that had been at the center of a bloody battle between France and Spain that changed the fate of the United States of America.

这些文物表明，潜水员很可能找到了"三一"号（La Trinité）的沉船残骸——那是一艘16世纪的法国船只，曾在一场改变美利坚合众国命运的法西血战中处于核心位置。

I

And then the legal storm began, with GME and Pritchett pitted against Florida and France.

随后法律风暴骤起，GME和普里切特与佛罗里达州和法国对簿公堂。

The Sunken Military Craft Act of 2004, a US federal act, protects any vessel that was on a military mission, allowing the originating country to claim their ship even centuries later.

2004年的《沉没军舰法》是美国的一项联邦法案，保护任何执行军事任务的船只，允许其所属国即使在数百年后也能主张对本国船只的所有权。

In 2018, two long years after Pritchett's discovery, the federal district court ruled in favor of France.

2018年，在普里切特发现沉船整整两年之后，联邦地区法院作出了有利于法国的裁决。

For Pritchett, the decision was devastating.

对普里切特来说，这一判决是毁灭性的。

Millions of dollars of investor funding and years of labor were lost.

数百万美元的投资者资金和多年的心血付诸东流。

J

But this is far from the first time a salvor has lost all rights to a discovery.

但这远非打捞者首次因一项发现而丧失全部权利。

In 2012, for instance, Spain won a five-year legal battle against Odyssey Marine Exploration, which had hauled 594,000 gold and silver coins from a Spanish wreck off the coast of Portugal across the Atlantic to the United States.

例如，2012年，西班牙在与奥德赛海洋探索公司的五年法律诉讼中胜诉。该公司曾从葡萄牙海岸附近一艘西班牙沉船中打捞出59.4万枚金银硬币，并将其运过大西洋带到了美国。

"Treasure hunters can be naive," says attorney David Concannon, who has had several maritime archaeologists as clients and represented two sides in the battles over the Titanic for 20 years.

"寻宝者往往过于天真，"律师戴维·康坎农说道。他曾代理过数位海洋考古学家，并在围绕"泰坦尼克"号的争夺战中代表双方长达20年。

"Many treasure hunters don't understand they are going to have to fight for their rights against a government that has an endless supply of money for legal battles that treasure hunters are likely to lose."

"许多寻宝者不明白，他们将不得不与政府争夺自己的权利，而政府拥有取之不尽的诉讼资金，寻宝者很可能败诉。"

K

Putting an inflated price on artifacts rather than viewing them as cultural and historical treasures that transcend any price is what irritates many archaeologists.

给文物标上虚高的价格，而不是将其视为超越任何价格的文化和历史瑰宝，这正是令许多考古学家恼火之处。

For the archaeologist, everything in a wreck matters—hair, fabric, a fragment of a newspaper, rat bones-all things speak volumes.

在考古学家看来，沉船中的一切都意义重大——毛发、织物、报纸残片、老鼠骨骼——所有这些东西都能说明很多问题。

Archaeologists don't want artifacts ending up in a private collection instead of taking humanity on a journey of understanding.

考古学家不希望文物最终落入私人收藏，而不是带领全人类踏上一段理解的旅程。

L

George Bass is one of the pioneers of under-water archaeology, and a researcher at Texas A&M University.

乔治·巴斯是水下考古学的先驱之一，也是得克萨斯农工大学的研究员。

He has testified in court against treasure hunters, but says archaeology is not without its own serious problems.

他曾在法庭上作证反对寻宝者，但也表示考古学本身并非没有严重问题。

He believes archaeologists need to do a better job themselves instead of routinely criticizing treasure hunters.

他认为，考古学家应该做好自己的本职工作，而不是一味地批评寻宝者。

"Archaeology has a terrible reputation for not publishing enough on its excavations and finds," he says.

"考古学在发掘和发现成果的发表方面做得远远不够，名声很差，"他说。

Gathering data, unearthing and meticulously preserving and examining finds, verifying identity and origin, piecing together the larger story, and writing and publishing a comprehensive paper or book can take decades.

收集资料、发掘并精心保护和检验出土文物、核实其身份与来历、拼凑还原事情的全貌，再到撰写并出版一篇全面的论文或一部专著，这一切可能耗时数十年。

A bit cynically, Bass describes colleagues who never published because they waited so long they became ill or died.

带着几分愤世嫉俗的口吻，巴斯谈到一些同行：他们一味苦等，等到病倒甚至离世，最终也未能发表任何成果。

Who is more at fault, Bass asks, the professional archaeologist who carefully excavates a site and never publishes on it or the treasure hunter who locates a submerged wreck, salvages part, conserves part, and publishes a book on the operation?

巴斯问道：到底谁更应受到责难？是那位一丝不苟地发掘一处遗址却始终不发表任何成果的专业考古学家，还是那个找到一艘沉没的船只、打捞出一部分、修复保存一部分，并就整个行动写书出版的寻宝者？

M

Pritchett concedes that his find deserves careful excavation and preservation.

普里切特承认，他的发现值得得到细致的发掘与妥善的保护。

"I think what I found should go in a museum," he says.

"我认为我找到的东西应该放进博物馆，"他说。

"But I also think I should get paid for what I found."

"但我也认为，我应该为自己找到的东西获得报酬。"

Indeed, it's a bit of a mystery why governments, archaeologists, and treasure hunters can't work together—and why salvors aren't at least given a substantial finder's fee before the original owner takes possession of the vessel and its artifacts.

的确，政府、考古学家和寻宝者为何不能通力合作，实在令人费解；而打捞者为何不能在原主收回船只及其文物之前，至少获得一笔丰厚的发现者酬金，同样是个谜。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1449 词 · 16 句

P1

Could you get by without using the internet for four and a half years?

你能做到四年半不上网吗？

That's exactly what singer and actress Selena Gomez has done in a bid to improve her mental health.

这正是歌手兼演员赛琳娜·戈麦斯所做的，她以此来改善自己的心理健康。

P2

She has spoken extensively about the relationship between her social media usage and her mental wellbeing, recalling feeling like "an addict" when she became Instagram's most followed user in 2016.

她曾大量谈及自己使用社交媒体与心理健康之间的关系，回忆说2016年成为Instagram上粉丝最多的用户时，感觉自己像个“瘾君子”。

"Taking a break from social media was the best decision that I've ever made for my mental health", says she.

她说：“暂时远离社交媒体是我为自己的心理健康做过的最佳决定。”

"The unnecessary hate and comparisons went away once I put my phone down."

“一旦我放下手机，那些无谓的谩骂和攀比就都消失了。”

P3

Ditching the web at large, however, is a far more subtle and complicated prospect.

然而，彻底告别互联网则是一件微妙得多、也复杂得多的事情。

The increasing digitisation of our society means that everything from paying a gas bill to plotting a route to a friend's house and even making a phone call is at the mercy of your internet connection.

社会的日益数字化意味着，从缴燃气费、规划去朋友家的路线，甚至打电话，一切都取决于你的网络连接。

Actively opting out of using the internet becomes a matter of privilege.

主动选择不使用互联网，变成了一种特权。

P4

Ms Gomez's multi-millionaire status has allowed her to take the "social" out of social media, so she can continue to leverage her enormous fame while keeping the trolls at bay.

戈麦斯女士的千万富翁身份让她得以把社交媒体中的“社交”部分去掉，从而在抵挡网络喷子的同时，继续利用自己巨大的名气。

The fact that she's still the second most-followed woman on Instagram suggests it's entirely possible to maintain a significant web profile to promote various projects—by way of a dedicated team—without being exposed to the cruel comments, hate mail and rape or death threats.

她仍是Instagram上粉丝第二多的女性，这一事实表明，完全可以通过专人团队来维持一个颇具影响力的网络形象以宣传各类项目，同时不必面对那些恶毒评论、仇恨邮件以及强奸或死亡威胁。

P5

It goes without saying that this is fundamentally different from how the rest of us without beauty deals and films to publicise use the likes of Instagram, TikTok and Twitter, but even the concept of a digital detox requires having a device and connectivity to choose to disconnect from.

不言而喻，这与我们这些没有美妆代言和电影需要宣传的人使用Instagram、TikTok和Twitter的方式有着根本不同，但即便是“数字排毒”的概念，也需要先拥有设备和网络连接，才能选择断开。

P6

The UK's digital divide has worsened over the past two years, leaving poorer families without broadband connections in their homes.

过去两年里，英国的数字鸿沟进一步扩大，导致贫困家庭家中没有宽带连接。

Digital exclusion is a major threat to wider societal equality in the UK, so witnessing companies like Facebook championing the metaverse as the next great frontier when school children are struggling to complete their homework feels particularly irritating.

数字排斥是英国社会平等面临的一大威胁，因此，当学童们还在为完成作业而挣扎时，看到脸书这样的公司把元宇宙鼓吹为下一个伟大前沿领域，就让人格外恼火。

P7

Consequently, it's worth bearing in mind that while deleting all social media accounts will undoubtedly make some feel infinitely better, many other people benefit from the strong sense of community that sharing platforms can breed.

因此，值得记住的是，虽然注销所有社交媒体账号无疑会让一些人感觉好上无数倍，但也有许多人从分享平台所孕育的强烈社区归属感中受益。

P8

Internet access will continue to grow in importance as we edge further towards web 3.0, and greater resources and initiatives are needed to provide the underprivileged with the connectivity they desperately need to learn, work and live.

随着我们进一步迈向Web 3.0，互联网接入的重要性将持续上升；我们需要投入更多资源和举措，为弱势群体提供他们学习、工作和生活所迫切需要的网络连接。

It's crucial that people who feel that social media is having a detrimental effect on their mental health are allowed to switch off and for those living in digital exclusion to be able to switch on in the first place.

至关重要的是，那些感到社交媒体正在对自身心理健康造成不良影响的人应当能够断开连接，而那些被数字排斥在外的人首先应当能够接入网络。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2444 词 · 17 句

P1

Psychologists have long been in disagreement as to whether competition is a learned or a genetic component of human behavior.

心理学界长期以来一直存在分歧：竞争究竟是后天习得的，还是人类行为中一种由基因决定的成分。

Whatever it is, you cannot but recognize the effect competition is exerting in academics and many other areas of contemporary life.

不管属于哪一种，你不得不承认竞争在学术领域以及当代生活的许多其他方面所产生的影响。

P2

Psychologically speaking, competition has been seen as an inevitable consequence of human drives.

从心理学角度来看，竞争一直被视为人类内驱力的必然产物。

According to Sigmund Freud, humans are born screaming for attention and full of organic drives for fulfillment in various areas.

按照西格蒙德·弗洛伊德的观点，人生来就会大喊着寻求关注，并且充满了在各个方面获得满足的本能驱力。

Initially, we compete for the attention of our parents.

起初，我们为争夺父母的关注而竞争。

Thereafter, we are at the mercy of a battle between our base impulses for self-fulfillment and social and cultural norms which prohibit pure indulgence.

此后，我们便受制于一场争斗——一方是追求自我满足的原始冲动，另一方是禁止肆意放纵的社会与文化规范。

P3

Current work in anthropology has suggested, however, that this view of the role of competition in human behavior may be incorrect.

然而，当前人类学领域的研究表明，这种关于竞争在人类行为中所起作用的看法可能是错误的。

Thomas Hobbes, one of the great philosophers of the seventeenth century, is perhaps best remembered for his characterization of the "natural world," that is, the world before the imposition of the will of humanity, as being "nasty, brutish, and short."

托马斯·霍布斯是十七世纪伟大的哲学家之一，他最广为人知的或许是他对“自然世界”（即人类意志尚未施加影响之前的世界）的描述——“肮脏、野蛮而短暂”。

This image of the pre-rational world is still widely held, reinforced by Charles Darwin's highly influential work, The Origin of Species, which established the doctrine of natural selection.

这种关于前理性世界的形象至今仍被广泛接受，并因查尔斯·达尔文极具影响力的著作《物种起源》而得到强化；该书确立了自然选择学说。

This doctrine, which takes for granted that those species best able to adapt to and master the natural environment in which they live will survive, has suggested that the struggle for survival is an inherent human trait which determines a person's success.

这一学说理所当然地认为，那些最能适应并驾驭其所处自然环境的物种方能存活；据此，生存竞争被视为人的一种固有特质，决定着一个人的成功。

Darwin's theory has even been summarized as "survival of the fittest"—a phrase Darwin himself never used—further highlighting competition's role in success.

达尔文的理论甚至被概括为“适者生存”——这个说法达尔文本人从未使用过——进一步凸显了竞争在成功中的作用。

As it has often been pointed out, however, there is nothing in the concept of natural selection that suggests that competition is the most successful strategy for "survival of the fittest."

然而，正如人们经常指出的那样，自然选择这一概念本身并无任何内容表明，竞争是实现“适者生存”最有效的策略。

Darwin asserted in The Origin of Species that the struggles he was describing should be viewed as metaphors and could easily include dependence and cooperation.

达尔文在《物种起源》中断言，他所描述的生存斗争应当被视为隐喻，其中也完全可以包含依赖与合作。

P4

Many studies have been conducted to test the importance placed on competition as opposed to other values, such as cooperation—by various cultures, and generally conclude that Americans uniquely praise competition as natural, inevitable, and desirable.

许多研究都曾检验过不同文化对竞争相较于合作等其他价值的重视程度，研究普遍认为，美国人格外推崇竞争，将其视为自然的、不可避免的、值得追求的。

In 1937, the world-renowned anthropologist Margaret Mead published Cooperation and Competition among Primitive Peoples, based on her studies of several societies that did not prize competition, and, in fact, seemed at times to place a negative value on it.

1937年，世界著名人类学家玛格丽特·米德出版了《原始民族中的合作与竞争》一书，该书基于她对若干不崇尚竞争、甚至有时对竞争持负面看法的社会的研究。

One such society was the Zuni Indians of Arizona, and they, Mead found, valued cooperation far more than competition.

其中一个社会就是亚利桑那州的祖尼印第安人，米德发现，他们对合作的重视远胜于竞争。

After studying dozens of such cultures, Mead's final conclusion was that competitiveness is a culturally created aspect of human behavior, and that its prevalence in a particular society is relative to how that society values it.

在研究了数十个这样的文化之后，米德得出的最终结论是：竞争意识是人类行为的一个文化塑造的面向，其在某一特定社会中的盛行程度取决于该社会对它的重视程度。
