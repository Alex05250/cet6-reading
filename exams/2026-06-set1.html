Two proposals are before the state legislature that look to put some limits on ever-increasing rent prices, which some lawmakers say are contributing to rising homelessness rates.

州议会目前有两项提案，旨在对不断上涨的租金价格加以限制，一些议员认为，租金上涨正在加剧无家可归率的上升。

The proposals impose caps on rent increases, preventing landlords in the state from raising rent more than the rate of inflation in most cases.

这两项提案对租金涨幅设定上限，在大多数情况下，禁止该州房东将租金提高到超过通货膨胀率的水平。

豁免情形包括楼龄不足10年的建筑、公共住房、超出日常维护范围的改善工程以及困难减免。

租金上涨对某些特定人群，例如少数族裔，产生的不利影响更为严重。

Representative Alex Ramel explained why he thought that this kind of legislation was so important, telling the story of a constituent struggling to live in the community.

众议员亚历克斯·拉梅尔阐述了他认为这类立法为何如此重要，讲述了一位选民在社区艰难生活的经历。

拉梅尔指出租金两位数上涨所造成的毁灭性影响，并表示自己正是在与一名年轻人交谈时决定提出这项立法的，那是在贝灵厄姆。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1194 词 · 44 句

A

For those "citizens of the world", the office can be anything from a beach hut in Brisbane to a Starbucks in Seattle, thanks to the growing prevalence of remote-based work.

对于这些“世界公民”来说，由于远程办公日益普及，办公室可以是布里斯班的一间海边小屋，也可以是西雅图的一家星巴克。

But roaming the globe from café to café is not without its challenges.

然而，穿梭于全球各地、从一家咖啡馆辗转到另一家，也并非没有挑战。

B

When the average traveller lands in a city like Bangkok, Berlin, or Buenos Aires, their first step might be to drop their bags at their hotel and to log on to TripAdvisor to find out the hours of a popular tourist attraction or the best rooftop bar for happy hour.

普通旅客抵达曼谷、柏林或布宜诺斯艾利斯这样的城市时，第一步或许是先把行李放到酒店，然后登录猫途鹰，查询某个热门景点的开放时间，或是寻找适合欢乐时光时段的最佳露台酒吧。

C

But people like Pieter Levels are not your average traveller.

但皮特·莱弗斯这样的人并非普通旅客。

A Dutch website developer, Levels has been living and working nomadically in places like Singapore, Hong Kong, Tokyo and Manila for two years.

莱弗斯是一名荷兰网站开发者，两年来他一直以游牧的方式在新加坡、香港、东京和马尼拉等地生活和工作。

With just a carry-on backpack, he chooses his location at will, based on a variety of factors—Wi-Fi speed, average temperature, exotic food and cost of living—that have little bearing on the work he actually does.

仅凭一个随身背包，他便随心所欲地选择落脚之地，依据的是各种与他实际从事的工作并无多大关系的因素——网速、平均气温、异域美食和生活成本。

D

Somewhere in his nomadic journey, Levels noticed that there was a growing need to create an online infrastructure for people like himself.

在游牧般的旅途中，莱弗斯注意到，像他这样的人越来越需要一套在线基础设施。

That's when he created #nomads, a membership-based chat system which runs on the team communication platform known as Slack.

于是他创建了 #nomads，一个基于会员制的聊天系统，运行在名为 Slack 的团队沟通平台上。

The channel allows users to ask people in their current city the types of practical questions that a tourist would unlikely need to ask.

该频道允许用户向身处当前城市的人提出各类实际问题——这些问题是普通游客几乎不会去问的。

E

Members of the geographically dispersed subculture that Levels is a part of call themselves "digital nomads".

莱弗斯所属的这一地理分布广泛的亚文化群体自称“数字游民”。

They are borderless, mostly self-employed remote workers who interact with cities in a way that's entirely different from a tourist, but still doesn't quite reach the status of resident.

他们是没有国界、大多为自雇人士的远程工作者，他们与城市互动的方式与游客截然不同，但又尚未达到居民的地位。

A relatively new phenomenon, there are no hard statistics on the prevalence of digital nomads or location independent workers.

作为一种相对较新的现象，目前尚无确切统计数据说明数字游民或不受地域限制的工作者的普遍程度。

But thanks to the growing prevalence of freelance livelihoods, a global sharing economy and numerous digital tools, the number of people who are able to live this life is growing quickly.

不过，随着自由职业日益普遍、全球共享经济兴起以及各种数字工具的涌现，能够过上这种生活的人正迅速增多。

And in a rapidly urbanising world, where a major city in Asia increasingly offers the same comforts as one in Europe, many people are reaching the realisation they don't need to stay in one place.

而在这个快速城市化的世界里，亚洲的大城市所能提供的舒适条件日益与欧洲大城市不相上下，许多人渐渐意识到，自己不必固守一地。

F

So how does a nomad differ from a tourist or resident?

那么，游牧者与游客或常住居民究竟有何不同？

In many ways, Levels says, you can think of a digital nomad like a "short-term independent expat".

莱韦尔斯说，从许多方面来看，可以把数字游民看作是“短期的独立外籍人士”。

Unlike a resident expat, they don't work for a large multinational company in an air conditioned office building and they generally enter a country on a tourist visa, simply renewing or moving on when it expires.

与常驻的外籍人士不同，他们不在装有空调的写字楼里为大型跨国公司工作；他们通常持旅游签证入境，签证到期时便续签或转往他处。

But unlike a tourist, they don't arrive in a foreign city without any responsibilities.

但与游客不同，他们来到一座异国城市时并非毫无责任。

Travel isn't necessarily required for them to do their jobs, but neither is staying in one place.

他们工作未必需要四处旅行，但也未必非得待在一个地方。

G

Janet Brent is an independent ebook designer.

珍妮特·布伦特是一名独立电子书设计师。

She says many people she knows assume she travels for work, but in reality her international destinations are largely up to her, and driven by cost.

她说，她认识的许多人都以为她是因工作而出行，但实际上她去哪些国家主要由自己决定，并由成本驱动。

"Contrary to what some people might think, I don't get paid to travel—I simply choose the lifestyle. Rather than rent in one given city, I choose to travel and do short-term, temporary stays around the world, and my travel decisions are literally based on what makes the most sense financially for me,"

“与有些人想的相反，我并不是靠旅行挣钱——我只是选择了这种生活方式。我不在某一座城市租房，而是选择旅行，在世界各地作短期、临时的停留；我决定去哪里，完全取决于怎样在经济上对我最划算，”

Brent said.

布伦特说。

"It's completely different from vacationing—you really sink into the city life and get your feet wet in the communities."

“这和度假完全是两回事——你会真正沉浸于城市生活，深入当地社区之中。”

H

Bryce Adams is another such nomad.

布莱斯·亚当斯就是另一位这样的游牧者。

He works as a developer for a South African company WooThemes, which has a workforce that's more than 80% remote-based.

他在一家南非公司WooThemes担任开发人员，该公司超过80%的员工都在远程办公。

Though Adams frequently posts pictures on Instagram of his enviable cooking adventures from Seoul to Bangkok, he says nomad life in foreign cities has its downsides, including finding adequate Wi-Fi for the bandwidth-hungry work his livelihood depends on.

尽管亚当斯经常在Instagram上发布他在首尔到曼谷之间令人艳羡的烹饪之旅的照片，但他说，在异国城市过游牧生活也有其弊端，其中就包括为他赖以为生的、需要高带宽的工作寻找足够快的Wi-Fi。

Contrary to the perception that he works under a mango tree in a tropical haven, Adams says the reality of this life means that he often ends up staying in more western style places in globalised cities—ones that are sure to have air conditioning and a decent connection—rather than exotic beachfront cafés.

与人们想象的他在热带天堂的芒果树下工作的场景相反，亚当斯说，这种生活的现实意味着他往往最终会待在全球化城市中更西式的地方——这些地方肯定配有空调和稳定的网络——而非异国情调的海滨咖啡馆。

I

"A fellow nomad posted quite a funny but honest message on Twitter a few days ago that stuck with me: 'It's funny that digital nomads spend 16 hours to fly to the other side of the world to sit in Starbucks,' and it's completely true," Adams said.

亚当斯说：“几天前，一位数字游民伙伴在Twitter上发了一条既好笑又诚实的留言，令我印象深刻：‘有趣的是，数字游民花16个小时飞到地球的另一端，却只是为了坐在星巴克里。’这话完全属实。”

"When you're a digital nomad, you don't actually have as much freedom as most people think you do. We have deadlines, we all have people relying on us and a mouth (or two) that needs to be fed. It's nice to be able to work anywhere in the world but it's not without its challenges."

“当你是一名数字游民时，你其实并没有大多数人以为的那么自由。我们有截止日期，有需要依靠我们的人，还有一张（或两张）等着喂的嘴。能够在全球任何地方工作固然很好，但也并非没有挑战。”

J

But these challenges can often provide an opportunity for nomads to see a side of a city they may not have if they were staying in a luxury hotel for a long weekend.

然而，这些挑战往往能让游民看到一座城市的另一面——如果他们只是在一座城市度过一个豪华的长周末，可能根本无缘得见。

However inconvenient, a trip to a local doctor, immigration office or laundry—or a conflict with a local Airbnb host (the accommodation style of choice for most nomads)—can be far more culturally instructive than a trip to the national museum.

去看当地医生、去移民局、去洗衣店——或者与当地的Airbnb房东（大多数游民偏爱的住宿方式）发生摩擦——无论多么不便，都远比去一趟国家博物馆更具文化启发性。

And because nomads often have to stay where the strongest internet connection is, they tend to spend more time in developing cities compared to the average tourist, who often flees to more touristy destinations soon after landing at the airport.

而且，由于游民往往不得不待在网络信号最强的地方，与普通游客相比，他们在一个城市停留的时间更长，而普通游客通常一下飞机就奔向更热门的旅游目的地。

K

The nomad life is, by all accounts, one built out of privilege.

据所有人说，游牧生活是一种建立在特权之上的生活。

You not only need a relatively powerful passport that makes tourist visas easy to attain, but also a knowledge economy job and access to the technology and devices that make remote working possible.

你不仅需要一本实力相对较强的护照，使你能够轻松获得旅游签证，还需要一份知识经济类的工作，以及接触使远程办公成为可能的技术和设备的条件。

But nomads like Adams say that while they are aware of this privilege, they believe living this way elevates what they're able to offer, by giving them a broader perspective on what they're building, writing, or designing and expanding their world view.

但亚当斯这样的游民表示，尽管他们意识到了这种特权，他们相信这种生活方式提升了他们的能力，使他们能够以更广阔的视角看待自己正在构建、撰写或设计的内容，并拓展了他们的世界观。

L

"Being able to travel and get around makes it easy to stay inspired," he added.

"能够四处旅行、走南闯北，让人很容易保持灵感，"他补充道。

"I truly believe that it makes me a more sensible human being, as I tend to see and appreciate the creative solutions people come up with in other parts of the world, even though many tend to have far more constraints than we have at home."

"我真心相信，这让我成为一个更通情达理的人，因为我往往会看到并欣赏世界各地的人们想出的创造性解决方案，尽管他们面临的限制往往远比我们在国内面临的多。"

M

So when you're constantly on the road and don't own any property or have a fixed address, which city do you call home?

那么，当你常年在路上，没有房产、没有固定住址时，你会把哪座城市称为家呢？

It's no surprise that many nomads are dual citizens or "third culture kids," who have grown up with a notion of identity that's not tied to nationalism or staying in one place.

难怪许多游牧者都是双重国籍人士或"第三文化孩子"，他们的成长伴随着一种不与民族主义或定居一地挂钩的身份认同观念。

Levels says that the nomad life has not only changed his notions of citizenship, but the people he chooses to spend time with as well.

Levels说，游牧生活不仅改变了他对公民身份的看法，也改变了他选择与之相处的人。

"Saying you're a 'citizen of the world' is a super cliché, right? But it's becoming quite true,"

"说自己是'世界公民'，这话超级俗套，对吧？但它正变得相当真实，"

Levels said.

Levels说道。

"This global awareness has been the reason I started Nomad List and it's affected who my friends are. It's normal to have dinner now and sit with a Korean girl, a British-Singaporean guy, a Mexican girl and a Nigerian guy. As much as that sounds like a politically correct TV advertisement, it's great that that's the time we're in now."

"这种全球意识正是我创办Nomad List的原因，它也影响了我的交友圈。现在和一位韩国姑娘、一位英裔新加坡小伙、一位墨西哥姑娘和一位尼日利亚小伙同桌吃饭，已是家常便饭。尽管这听起来像是一则政治正确的电视广告，但能生活在这样一个时代，真的很棒。"

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1447 词 · 21 句

P1

Children's use of social media is a problem.

儿童使用社交媒体是个问题。

That doesn't mean it should be stopped.

但这并不意味着就该禁止他们使用。

While younger children should not be on platforms where 13 is the minimum age, it is neither practical nor desirable to imagine a world in which teenagers are prevented from accessing the platforms and messaging systems that the adults around them use to organise their lives.

虽然低龄儿童不应使用那些规定最低年龄为13岁的平台，但要设想一个让青少年无法接触周围成年人用来安排生活的平台和通讯系统的世界，既不现实也不可取。

P2

But the pleasure and stimulation that people of all ages find on their screens and smartphones must not blind us to the harm that spending time in this way can cause.

然而，各年龄段的人在屏幕和智能手机上获得的愉悦和刺激，绝不能蒙蔽我们，让我们忽视以这种方式消磨时间所带来的危害。

The finding that the alarmingly high rate of depression in British teenage girls is closely correlated to time spent on social media is extremely concerning.

有研究发现，英国少女的抑郁率高得惊人，且与使用社交媒体的时间密切相关，这一发现令人极为担忧。

Research found that two in five girls are on social media for at least three hours a day—with half as many boys engaged to the same extent.

研究发现，五分之二的女孩每天使用社交媒体至少三小时——达到这一程度的男孩人数只有女孩的一半。

More than a third of depressed girls have experienced online harassment.

超过三分之一的抑郁女孩曾遭受网络骚扰。

About half of affected girls suffered disrupted sleep, compared with 20% of depressed boys.

约半数受抑郁影响的女孩睡眠受到影响，而抑郁男孩中这一比例为20%。

P3

Although the research does not prove that social media causes depression, the importance of the finding that there is a connection should not be underestimated.

尽管该研究并未证明社交媒体会导致抑郁，但两者存在关联这一发现的重要性不容低估。

Schools have done important work in communicating key messages about the risks of online abuse.

学校在传播有关网络侵害风险的关键信息方面做了重要工作。

But when it comes to broader questions about "screen time", many professionals as well as parents are confused and uncertain.

但涉及到有关“屏幕时间”的更广泛问题时，许多专业人士和家长都感到困惑和不确定。

P4

The health secretary, Matt Hancock, wants official guidelines on time and age limits.

卫生大臣马特·汉考克希望出台有关使用时长和年龄限制的官方指南。

This idea has the virtue of simplicity, though some experts question its effects and call for a greater degree of differentiation, arguing that time online by itself is not a bad thing and it all depends on what you are doing.

这一理念的优点在于简单明了，尽管一些专家质疑其效果，主张应有更大的区分度，并指出上网时间本身并非坏事，关键在于你在网上做什么。

Also due this year is information commissioner Elizabeth Denham's Age Appropriate Design Code, covering the use of children's personal data.

今年还将实施的是信息专员伊丽莎白·登厄姆提出的《适龄设计规范》，涉及儿童个人数据的使用。

Both initiatives are welcome.

这两项举措都值得欢迎。

But the government is playing catch-up.

但政府一直在亡羊补牢。

Statutory regulation of the digital media giants is long overdue.

对数字媒体巨头实施法定监管早已迫在眉睫。

The failure to protect children from data harvesting and targeted advertising thus far is as shocking as the lack of concern shown by the companies themselves with regard to children's psychological health.

迄今为止未能保护儿童免受数据收割和定向广告的侵害，其令人震惊的程度不亚于这些公司自身对儿童心理健康问题所表现出的漠不关心。

P5

Of course, social media companies are not the source of all our ills, and can offer a too-easy target for politicians keen to divert attention from inadequately resourced schools and health services.

当然，社交媒体公司并非所有弊病的根源，它们也容易成为政客们的靶子——这些人急于将公众注意力从资金不足的学校和医疗服务上转移开。

There are other reasons for the rising incidence of depression in young people, with increasing pressure to succeed in education and in life more broadly—to be famous, beautiful, rich—often cited as a source of unhappiness or anxiety which can, if aggravated by other factors and not managed, tip into ill health.

年轻人抑郁率上升还有其他原因，比如在教育乃至更广泛的人生领域中日益加剧的成功压力——成名、貌美、富有——常被视为不快乐或焦虑的来源，而这些情绪若因其他因素加剧且得不到疏导，就可能恶化为疾病。

Safeguarding means more than the avoidance of physical threat.

保护儿童不仅仅是避免身体受到威胁。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2443 词 · 20 句

P1

About a decade ago, the G-20, a forum of the world's biggest economies, agreed to crack down on multinational corporations' abusive use of tax havens.

大约十年前，二十国集团（G-20）——由全球最大的经济体组成的论坛——同意严厉打击跨国公司滥用避税天堂的行为。

This resulted in a plan that aimed to curb practices that shielded a large chunk of corporate profits from tax authorities.

为此出台了一项计划，旨在遏制那些使大量企业利润得以逃税务部门监管的做法。

P2

But, instead of reining in the use of tax havens—countries such as the Bahamas and Cayman Islands with very low or no effective tax rates—the problem has only gotten worse.

然而，非但没有遏制避税天堂的使用——即巴哈马、开曼群岛这类实际税率极低甚至为零的国家和地区——问题反而愈演愈烈。

P3

In a new study, we measured the excessive profits reported in tax havens that cannot be explained by ordinary economic activity such as employees, factories and research in that country.

在一项新研究中，我们测算出避税天堂所报告的、无法用该国员工、工厂和研发等正常经济活动来解释的超额利润。

Our findings show a striking pattern of artificial shifting of paper profits to tax havens by corporations, which has been relentless since the 1980s.

我们的研究结果揭示了一个触目惊心的模式：企业将账面利润人为转移至避税天堂，且自20世纪80年代以来这种做法从未停歇。

P4

We found that the world's biggest multinational businesses shifted 37% of the profits—$969 billion—they earned in other countries to tax havens in 2019.

我们发现，2019年，全球最大的跨国企业将其在其他国家赚取的利润中的37%（9690亿美元）转移到了避税天堂。

The figure was less than 2% in the 1970s.

而在20世纪70年代，这一比例还不到2%。

The main reasons for the large increase were the growth of the tax avoidance industry in the 1980s and U.S. policies that made it easier to shift profits from high-tax countries to tax havens.

大幅增长的主要原因，是20世纪80年代避税产业的兴起，以及美国的政策使企业更容易将利润从高税率国家转移到避税天堂。

P5

We estimate that the amount of corporate taxes lost as a result reached 10% of total corporate revenue in 2019, up from less than 0.1% in the 1970s.

我们估计，由此导致的企业税收损失在2019年达到了企业总收入的10%，而在20世纪70年代还不到0.1%。

In 2019, the total government tax loss globally was $250 billion.

2019年，全球政府税收损失总额达2500亿美元。

U.S. multinational corporations alone accounted for about half of that, followed by the U.K. and Germany.

仅美国跨国公司就占了其中约一半，其次是英国和德国。

P6

How do policymakers fix this?

政策制定者该如何解决这一问题呢？

So far, the world as a whole has been trying to solve this problem by cutting or scrapping corporate taxes in a very gradual way.

迄今为止，世界各国一直试图通过渐进式削减或废除企业所得税来解决这一问题。

In the past 40 years, the global effective corporate tax rate has fallen from 23% to 17%.

在过去40年间，全球实际企业所得税率已从23%降至17%。

At the same time, governments have relied more heavily on consumption taxes.

与此同时，各国政府更加依赖消费税。

P7

But the root cause of profit-shifting is the incentives involved, such as generous or lenient corporate tax rates in other countries.

但利润转移的根本原因在于其中的利益驱动，例如其他国家实行的优厚或宽松的企业税率。

If countries could agree on a global minimum corporate tax rate of, say, 20%, the problem of profit-shifting would largely disappear, as tax havens would simply cease to exist.

如果各国能就全球最低企业所得税率达成一致，比如20%，利润转移问题就将基本消失，因为避税天堂将不复存在。

P8

This type of mechanism is exactly what more than 130 countries signed onto in 2021, with implementation of a 15% minimum tax set to begin soon in the EU, U.K., Japan, Indonesia and many other countries.

这正是2021年130多个国家签署认可的机制类型，欧盟、英国、日本、印度尼西亚及许多其他国家即将开始实施15%的最低税率。

The U.S. has notably not been able to pass this legislation.

美国显然未能通过这项立法。

P9

Our research suggests implementing this type of tax reform is necessary in reversing the shift of ever-greater amounts of corporate profits to tax havens—instead of being taxed by the governments where they operate and create value.

我们的研究表明，实施此类税制改革对于扭转越来越多的企业利润流向避税天堂的趋势是必要的——这些利润本应由其开展经营并创造价值所在国的政府征税。
