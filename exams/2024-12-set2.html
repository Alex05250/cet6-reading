Ever been talking about something important with someone you care about and found the discussion frustrating instead of fruitful?

你是否曾与在乎的人谈论重要话题，结果却发现交流令人沮丧而非富有成效？

Negative energy can then cast a shadow over your efforts to build a shared understanding.

这样一来，负面情绪就会给努力建立共识的过程蒙上阴影。

In fact collaborative problem-solving conversations require skills.

事实上，协作解决问题的对话需要技巧。

Strong listening skills are essential.

良好的倾听能力至关重要。

Sprinkling in positivity via appreciation, affectionate smiles, and warm comments helps.

适时穿插积极的表达——如表达欣赏、报以亲切的微笑、说些温暖的话语——会很有帮助。

So does the essential ability to keep your emotional tone in the calm zone.

同样重要的是，要能始终保持冷静的语气。

If the tone of a dialogue heats up and anger erupts, the dialogue flips from collaborative to adversarial, and progress is likely to come to an immediate halt.

如果对话的气氛升温、愤怒爆发，对话就会从协作转向对抗，进展很可能立即中断。

In addition, effective dialogue generally has another important characteristic: symmetry.

此外，有效的对话通常还有一个重要特征：对等性。

Symmetry in dialogue refers to the balance of how much each partner talks.

对话中的对等性是指双方说话量的平衡。

Are you each getting equal airtime?

你们各自拥有同等的发言机会吗？

When one participant does most of the talking, this asymmetry or lack of balance tends to become annoying to one or both of you.

当一方占据大部分发言时间时，这种不对等或失衡往往会让一方或双方感到厌烦。

The silent partner typically tires of only listening, while the talker can feel overburdened.

一直只做倾听的一方通常会感到厌倦，而滔滔不绝的一方则可能觉得不堪重负。

Usually, both would prefer more equal give and take.

通常，双方都希望彼此的付出与回报更加对等。

Different rates of speech and levels of voice volume can erode symmetry as well.

语速的快慢和音量的高低差异也会破坏这种平衡。

The faster or louder partner can easily begin taking up more airtime.

语速更快或声音更大的一方很容易占据更多的发言时间。

The slower-talking or softer-voiced one can have trouble getting the floor.

语速较慢或声音较轻的一方则可能难以获得发言机会。

Conversations also lose balance when one partner's opinions count more than the other's.

当一方的意见比另一方的更有分量时，对话也会失去平衡。

One person's input may carry more sway because their style of expression is more vigorous.

一个人的发言可能更具影响力，因为其表达方式更为有力。

One viewpoint may tend to get lost because it is expressed more tentatively.

某种观点可能会被忽视，因为它表达得较为委婉试探。

If either partner fairly consistently predominates or evaporates, modifying the pattern will be helpful.

如果其中一方长期占据主导地位或总是沉默退让，那么改变这种模式将大有裨益。

The dialogue will feel more productive, and at the same time, the relationship overall is likely to feel more positive.

对话会因此更有成效，同时，整体关系也往往会变得更加融洽。

Section B · 长篇阅读（信息匹配）1218 词 · 52 句

A

Blue moons, rose moons, supermoons.

蓝月亮、粉红月亮、超级月亮。

For some reason all the websites seem to think you really want to know all about these moons.

不知为何，所有网站似乎都认定你真的很想了解关于这些月亮的一切。

"Catch This Weekend's AMAZING SUPERMOON," one headline will announce.

“别错过本周末惊人的超级月亮”——某个标题会这样大声宣告。

"The Supermoon Isn't Actually A Big Deal And You're All Ruining Astronomy," another will complain.

“超级月亮其实没什么大不了的，你们都把天文学给毁了”——另一个标题会这样抱怨道。

B

The latest example is the super blood wolf moon eclipse which sounds like the name of an emotional song I would have performed a modern dance routine to in 9th grade, but is, in fact, an astronomical event set to occur on January 20.

最新的例子是“超级血狼月食”，这名字听起来像一首情绪化的歌名，九年级时我会伴着它跳上一段现代舞，但它其实是一场定于1月20日发生的天文现象。

As we'll explain below, there is nothing truly mysterious or otherwise special about this moon, and it will not look like a bloody wolf head in the sky or anything wild like that.

正如下文将要解释的，这次的月亮并没有什么真正神秘或特别之处，它不会在天空中看起来像一颗血淋淋的狼头，也不会有任何那般离谱的样子。

We get these super lunar events by smashing together all the qualifiers we've historically used to keep track of full moons throughout the year, and in the age of the internet we can get a little too enthusiastic.

这些超级天象，是我们把历史上用来区分全年各次满月的所有修饰语一股脑儿拼在一起造出来的；而在互联网时代，我们有时热情得过了头。

C

Consider this your go-to resource for all moon-gazing news.

请把本文当作你了解一切赏月新闻的首选指南。

Here's what you need to know about the latest lunar event.

关于这次最新的天象，以下是你需要知道的内容。

D

Look, it's okay if you don't know.

听着，你不知道也没关系。

There are probably loads of folks who walk around pretending they totally know why that thing in the sky seems to get bigger and smaller at regular intervals but who totally do not.

恐怕有很多人整天装作完全明白天上那个东西为何会定期变大变小，其实他们压根儿就不明白。

E

The moon orbits Earth, and it's tidally locked—that means it always shows us the same face, instead of spinning around like our planet does.

月球绕地球运行，并且被潮汐锁定——这意味着它始终以同一面朝向我们，而不会像我们的星球那样旋转不停。

That's why you can always see the man on the moon (or the moon rabbit, depending on your cultural preferences) even as it spins around us.

这就是为什么，无论月亮如何在我们周围旋转，你总能看到月亮上的那个男人（或者是玉兔，取决于你的文化偏好）。

But while the moon is big and bright in the sky when it's full, that's only because it's reflecting light from the sun.

不过，满月时月亮虽然又大又亮，那只是因为它在反射太阳的光。

But the moon is always moving, so it's getting hit with sunlight at different angles.

但月亮始终在移动，因此阳光照射它的角度也在不断变化。

It's invisible to us during the new moon, because our satellite is parked right between us and the sun; the so-called dark side of the moon is lit up like Las Vegas, but the side we can see is in shadow.

新月时我们看不到它，因为此时月球正好位于我们和太阳之间；所谓的月球背面被照得像拉斯维加斯一样亮，而我们能看到的一面却处在阴影中。

A full moon happens when the earth is right between the sun and the moon, so sunlight hits the part we can see.

满月发生在地球恰好位于太阳和月亮之间时，此时阳光照在我们能看到的那一面。

And all the other phases are just the transition from one of those extremes to the other.

而月相的所有其他变化，不过是这两种极端状态之间的过渡。

F

The moon isn't always exactly the same distance from Earth, because its orbit isn't perfectly circular.

月亮与地球的距离并非始终完全相同，因为它的轨道并不是完美的圆形。

We call the closest point perigee, and the most distant point is apogee.

我们把最近的点称为近地点，最远的点称为远地点。

2018's closest perigee and most distant apogee both happened in January, and the difference was about 30,000 miles.

2018年最近的近地点和最远的远地点都出现在一月，两者相差约三万英里。

G

The reason you care about this ordinary change in distance is that it turns a moon super.

你之所以要在意这个再寻常不过的距离变化，是因为它能让月亮变“超级”。

When a full moon happens close to perigee, it's going to look a bit bigger.

当满月出现在接近近地点时，月亮看上去会稍大一些。

Honestly, the difference is not that profound, but if you're in a position to photograph the supermoon next to something that shows the slight increase in scale, it can look pretty cool.

说实话，差别并没有那么明显，但如果你能拍到超级月亮，并在旁边放一个可以衬托出其尺寸略微增大的参照物，照片看起来会相当酷。

January's super blood wolf moon eclipse is super because the date lines up with the closest the moon will get to us during January, but the moon won't actually be at its closest for the year until February's supermoon, which you can expect plenty of undue excitement over.

一月的超级血狼月之所以“超级”，是因为这一天正好是月亮在一月里离我们最近的时候；但月亮全年真正离我们最近的一次，其实要等到二月的超级月亮，届时想必又会掀起一阵不必要的热潮。

H

Blood moons only occur during total lunar eclipses (which can happen a few times a year in any given location).

血月只在月全食期间出现（月全食在任何地方一年都可能发生几次）。

When the moon slips through our shadow, we give it a reddish coloring.

当月亮穿过我们的影子时，我们便给它染上了一层微红的色彩。

The moon can also look orange whenever it's rising or setting, or if it hangs low in the horizon all night—the light bouncing off of it has to travel through a thicker atmosphere there, which scatters more blue light away.

月亮在升起或落下时，或整夜低垂在地平线附近时，看起来也可能是橙色的——从月亮反射回来的光在这些位置需要穿过更厚的大气层，大气层将更多蓝光散射掉了。

But you'll probably only see that deep, sinister red during an eclipse.

但那种深沉而阴森的红色，恐怕只有在月食时才能见到。

I

A lot of headlines about moons are just ridiculous (you do not need to be particularly excited about a blue moon, it just looks like a regular full moon), but you should definitely roll out of bed to look at a blood moon if one is going to be visible in your region, even though they're just lunar eclipses and not evidence of bloody battles between the sky gods.

关于月亮的许多新闻标题简直荒唐可笑（你实在不必为蓝月格外激动，它看上去就跟平常的满月一样），但如果血月将在你所在地区可见，你绝对应该爬下床去看一看——尽管它们不过是月全食，并不是天神之间血腥大战的证据。

J

In March of 2018, we had our second "blue moon" of the year, to much acclaim.

2018年3月，我们迎来了当年的第二次“蓝月”，备受瞩目。

And while that's not necessarily special in an oh-gosh-get-out-and-look-at-it way, it's certainly special: a blue moon is a nickname for when two full moons fall in the same calendar month, and we hadn't previously had two in one year since 1999.

虽然这未必值得“天啊快出去看看”那种兴奋，但它确实非同寻常：所谓蓝月，是指同一个日历月内出现两次满月，而上一次一年之内出现两次蓝月，还要追溯到1999年。

We won't have it happen again until 2037.

下一次再出现要等到2037年。

Astronomer David Chapman explained that this is merely a peculiarity of our calendar; once we stopped doing things based on the moon and started trying to follow the sun and the seasons, we stopped having one reliable full moon per month.

天文学家戴维·查普曼解释说，这不过是历法的一个特殊现象；一旦我们不再依据月亮来安排事务，转而追随太阳和四季，就不再保证每个月都有一次固定的满月了。

The moon cycle is 29.53 days long on average, so in most months we still end up with a single new moon and a single full one.

月相周期平均为29.53天，因此在大多数月份里，我们仍然只有一次新月和一次满月。

But every once in a while, one month steals a full moon from another.

但偶尔也会出现这样的情况：某个月从别的月份那里“偷”走了一次满月。

In 2018 (and in 1999, and again in 2037) both January and March stacked full moons on the first and last nights of the month, leaving February in the dark.

在2018年（以及1999年，还有2037年），一月和三月的月初与月末都恰好各有一次满月，唯独二月没有。

K

Getting two blue moons a year is rare, but we have individual blue moons every few years.

一年出现两次蓝月亮实属罕见，但每隔几年我们就能见到一次单独的蓝月亮。

Also, fun fact: not actually blue.

另外，一个有趣的事实是：它其实并不是蓝色的。

A moon can indeed take on a moody blue color, but this only happens when particles of just the right size disperse through the sky—and it has nothing to do with the moon's status as "blue".

月亮确实可以呈现出忧郁的蓝色，但这只有在大小恰到好处的颗粒散播于天空时才会发生——而且这与月亮被称为“蓝月亮”毫无关系。

Big clouds of ash from volcanic eruptions or fires can do the trick, but it doesn't happen often, and the stars would certainly have to align for two such rare instances to occur at once.

火山爆发或大火产生的大量火山灰可以达到这种效果，但这并不常见，而且要让这两种罕见现象同时发生，简直是天时地利缺一不可。

L

You may have heard that the super special second blue moon of 2018 was also a Paschal moon.

你可能听说过，2018年那个极其特别的第二个蓝月亮同时也是复活节月亮。

This is true!

确实如此！

That just means it was the first full moon of spring, which is often used to determine the date of Easter Sunday.

这仅仅意味着它是春季的第一个满月，而这个满月常被用来确定复活节星期日的日期。

All of this is just calendar nonsense and we refuse to go into it further.

所有这些不过是历法上的文字游戏，我们拒绝进一步深究。

M

Sometimes you'll see a headline that promises a moon with so many qualifiers it makes your head spin.

有时你会看到这样的标题，承诺给你一个修饰语多到让人头晕目眩的月亮。

A super blue blood worm moon, perhaps?

比如，超级蓝血蠕虫月亮？

Or a super blood wolf moon?

还是超级血狼月亮？

Lots of websites will tell you that "wolf moon" is the traditional name of the first full moon of the year in "Native American" cultures, which is kind of a weird thing to claim given that there are 573 registered Tribal Nations in the US alone today, not to mention historically.

许多网站会告诉你，“狼月”是美洲原住民文化中一年中第一次满月的传统名称，这种说法颇为奇怪，因为仅美国境内如今登记在册的部落民族就有573个，更不用说历史上还有更多了。

The idea that hungry, howling wolves were such a universal constant in January that all of North America with its different cultures, geographies, and languages spontaneously came up with the same nickname is illogical.

认为饥饿嚎叫的狼在一月份如此普遍，以至于整个北美大陆上不同文化、不同地理环境、不同语言的人们不约而同地给满月起了同一个绰号，这种想法毫无道理。

N

Many cultures have traditional names for the full moon in a given month or season, so there's quite a list to draw from if you're trying to really juice up a story on a slightly-bigger-than-average view of the moon.

许多文化都为特定月份或季节的满月赋予了传统名称，所以如果你真想把一次略大于平时的月面景观渲染成故事，可供选用的名称相当多。

But these are all based on human calendars and activities and folklore; you will not go outside and see a pink moon in April, though I wish it were so.

但这些名称全都基于人类的历法、活动和民间传说；四月你走到屋外并不会看到一轮粉色的月亮，尽管我倒希望如此。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 1450 词 · 26 句

P1

With population increases and global urbanisation ever accelerating, much attention is focused on the sustainability of our cities and scarce attention is paid to the countryside.

随着人口增长和全球城市化进程不断加快，人们的目光大多聚焦于城市的可持续发展，却鲜少关注乡村。

P2

Rural life is associated with closely-knit communities, sense of belonging, and a simple, tranquil life.

乡村生活让人联想到紧密团结的社区、归属感以及简朴宁静的生活。

Yet whilst some or all of these elements exist in the countryside, so do conservatism and a lack of employment opportunities.

然而，尽管乡村中确实存在上述部分或全部元素，同样存在的还有保守观念和就业机会的匮乏。

And it is mostly due to the latter that rural communities are suffering.

而乡村社区所遭受的困境，主要正是源于后者。

Attracted by the improved economics of urban areas, country-dwellers across the world are moving out, contributing to the excessive crowdedness of densely populated cities, and leaving the countryside in desolation.

受城市地区经济改善的吸引，世界各地的农村居民纷纷外迁，既加剧了人口稠密城市的过度拥挤，也使乡村日渐荒凉。

P3

So how can the countryside ensure its survival among these changes; or should it at all?

那么，在这些变迁之中，乡村该如何确保自身的存续？又或者，它是否真的应当存续下去？

Due to both the distance between residences and facilities and often intermittent public transport, those living in the countryside are heavier automobile users.

由于住所与各类设施之间距离较远，加之公共交通往往时断时续，乡村居民对汽车的依赖程度更高。

Rural dwellers also use more energy maintaining their mostly detached buildings.

乡村居民在维护其以独栋为主的建筑时，消耗的能源也更多。

We may be piled up like boxes in the city, but this brings energy efficiency that the countryside cannot match.

我们在城市里或许像箱子一样被堆叠在一起，但这带来的能源利用效率却是乡村无法比拟的。

Rural energy requirements result in higher carbon emissions per person than in the city, so it is actually irresponsible to endorse such a lifestyle choice.

乡村的能源需求导致人均碳排放高于城市，因此支持这样的生活方式选择实属不负责任。

P4

Protecting the countryside is a hot topic.

保护乡村是一个热门话题。

Those who have made their fortunes in the city often buy second homes in the countryside, visiting intermittently for a sample of a supposedly simple life.

那些在城市里发了财的人，常常在乡村购置第二套住宅，偶尔前来小住，体验一番据称简朴的生活。

The side-effect of this is that house prices are pushed up due to increased demand, leaving them unaffordable for local people.

由此产生的副作用是，需求增加推高了房价，使当地人无力负担。

A solution has yet to be found for this challenge, for market forces are further forcing residents out of the countryside.

对于这一挑战尚无解决之道，因为市场力量正进一步迫使居民离开乡村。

For those who believe in rural preservation, this is deplorable.

对于那些主张乡村保护的人来说，这实在令人痛心。

P5

Whilst an idealised view of the countryside is common, the reality is that rural life has continuously evolved.

虽然人们对乡村的理想化想象很普遍，但现实是乡村生活一直在不断演变。

Industrialisation brought an increase in divisions of labour, changing social links between rural people.

工业化带来了劳动分工的增加，改变了乡村居民之间的社会联系。

The internet has introduced once foreign cultures into the countryside.

互联网将曾经陌生的文化引入了乡村。

Improved travel connections and affordability have made the world more accessible, creating a "global village".

出行条件的改善和交通费用的降低使人们更容易抵达世界各地，造就了一个"地球村"。

Considering these changes, surely an evolving countryside is inevitable.

考虑到这些变化，乡村的不断演变无疑是不可避免的。

P6

Perhaps the countryside should also prepare itself for massive population increases in the future.

或许乡村也应为未来人口的大幅增长做好准备。

With cities across the world growing rapidly, how will the current urban generation respond to the inevitable social changes of mass urbanisation?

随着全球各大城市迅速扩张，当代城市一代将如何应对大规模城市化所带来的必然社会变革？

Will the longing for a simpler life become stronger, fueling a rural renaissance?

对更简单生活的向往是否会变得更加强烈，从而推动一场乡村复兴？

P7

As the world continues to urbanise, the voices calling for rural preservation may well grow louder.

随着世界继续城市化，呼吁保护乡村的声音很可能会越来越响亮。

But change is inevitable.

然而，变化是不可避免的。

We know that the world is going to see new cities emerging from the dust and current cities growing into megacities, but the future of the countryside is less clear.

我们知道，世界将迎来一座座从尘土中崛起的新城市，现有的城市也将发展为超级大城市，但乡村的未来却尚不明朗。

Section C · 仔细阅读 Passage 2446 词 · 27 句

P1

Statements, like "beauty is in the eye of the beholder", are rarely questioned.

“情人眼里出西施”这类说法，很少有人质疑。

They've become so embedded in our consciousness that people think it's absurd to think otherwise.

这些说法已深深植根于我们的意识之中，以至于人们认为持有不同看法是荒谬的。

It might be useful, however, to at least push back on this assumption because people evaluate environment, situations, and people aesthetically.

然而，至少对这一假设提出质疑或许是有益的，因为人们会以审美的方式来评价环境、情境和他人。

We may find that we still believe it is correct, but we may also find that there is a lot more to the situation than you suppose.

我们可能会发现自己仍然认为这一说法是正确的，但我们也可能发现，实际情况远比我们想象的复杂。

P2

A recent study exploring aesthetic taste was published in Cognition.

最近一项关于审美品味的研究发表在《认知》（Cognition）期刊上。

The results from this study show that people agree very much in their aesthetic evaluation of natural objects, but they disagree more about artifacts, or human-made objects.

这项研究的结果表明，人们对自然事物的审美评价高度一致，但对人工制品或人造物体的评价则分歧较大。

P3

The study found that shared taste was most common for faces and natural landscapes, but least common among works of architecture and art.

研究发现，人们对人脸和自然景观的审美品味最为一致，而在建筑和艺术作品方面的一致性最低。

The hypothesis is the commonly pleasing features, like proportion or symmetry, are at work.

该假设认为，诸如比例或对称性等令人愉悦的常见特征发挥了作用。

P4

Preferences for natural scenes might be learned through life experiences.

对自然景观的偏好可能是通过生活经验习得的。

Factors like habitability, safety, and openness might be preferred as people develop.

随着人的成长发展，宜居性、安全性和开阔性等因素可能更受青睐。

Some of the details of landscapes change, but the basics are more common, e.

景观的某些细节各不相同，但基本要素更为普遍，例如

g., water, open spaces, and signs of care.

水、开阔空间和被照料的痕迹。

P5

Art and architecture, unlike natural spaces, do not have the same level of exposure.

与自然空间不同，艺术和建筑的曝光程度并不相同。

So, people do not have the same level of shared taste.

因此，人们的共同审美趣味也并不一致。

P6

It's possible that the lower amount of agreement in the shared taste of artifacts has to do more with elements of style, rather than "behavioral consequences".

人们对人造物的共同审美趣味之所以分歧较大，可能更多与风格元素有关，而非所谓的“行为后果”。

P7

All of the consequences seem reasonable (or at least possible), but I think there might be an interesting philosophical conclusion that they did not draw.

所有这些结论似乎都很合理（或至少有可能成立），但我认为其中或许蕴含着一个他们未曾得出的有趣的哲学结论。

It seems that there is an objective ground to our aesthetic preferences or evaluations.

我们的审美偏好或评价似乎存在客观依据。

I am not saying this would mean beauty is completely objective, just that there could possibly be general objective principles at work, i.

我并不是说这就意味着美是完全客观的，只是说可能确实存在某种普遍的客观原则在起作用，即，

e., beauty is not simply in the eye of the beholder.

美并不仅仅取决于观者的眼光。

P8

Nature exhibits some of the universal aesthetic features, such as radiance, in a common way throughout the world.

自然界以一种普遍的方式，在世界各地展现出某些普遍的审美特征，比如光华感。

It's not exactly the same everywhere, but it is common.

各地的表现并非完全相同，但确是普遍存在的。

For example, a sunset is similar enough in different places to warrant almost universal appeal.

例如，日落在不同地方的景象足够相似，几乎能获得普遍的喜爱。

P9

Possible universal principles of beauty—such as proportion, fittingness, radiance, and others—are general categories, which allow for a wide array of embodiments.

可能存在的普遍美学原则——如比例、适宜性、光华感等——是一些概括性的范畴，可以有多种多样的具体呈现。

When people get involved in making artifacts in architecture or art, they apply these very general concepts in unique ways.

当人们从事建筑或艺术创作时，会以各自独特的方式运用这些非常概括的概念。

But the way they applied the principle may not have universal appeal.

但他们应用这一原则的方式未必具有普遍的吸引力。

P10

This is why it's important to experience diverse cultures and their artifacts because it opens us up to different ways of approaching or constructing beauty.

这就是为什么体验多元文化及其文物艺术很重要，因为它能让我们接触到理解和创造美的不同方式。

And it is always good to question our assumptions.

而且，对我们的固有观念提出质疑总是有益的。
